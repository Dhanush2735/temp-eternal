# Location and duplication review leads — 2026-10-28

These are investigation notes, not approved corrections. Title scans cover the 20 indexed event-containing packs, but differently worded or unindexed records may have been missed. No IDs below were removed or merged.

- **Los Angeles, 1781:** `wpd_3a47f16780` is incorrectly located at 10.6738, -89.6128. The regional record `wpr_2578874bc3` already describes the same founding in Los Angeles. Moving the decade record without resolving duplication could produce two active representations. The National Park Service identifies the 1781 colonial settlement and existing Gabrieleno village of Yangna: https://www.nps.gov/places/el-pueblo-de-los-angeles-historical-monument.htm. The current plaza is not necessarily the original precise pueblo site: https://ohp.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=21427. Candidate `26a8e083f56edd227ffd85a6`, 18th century rev 1378596652. Deferred.
- **Donglin Academy, 1111:** `wpd_49c7d30f53` has an incorrect point at 30.9975, 113.7047; `wpy_1bfd4ab9fa` already describes the same foundation in Wuxi. The Wuxi municipal account states 1111: https://en.wuxi.gov.cn/2024-06/27/c_999755.htm. Deferred as possible semantic duplicate.
- **Silla, traditional 57 BCE:** `wpd_b231a73266` is not in southeastern Korea; `wpy_1c27fe9593` describes the traditional accession of the founder in the same year, possibly the same foundation tradition. The date is traditional, not established fact; https://www.worldhistory.org/Silla/ notes modern historians prefer later state formation. Geographical reference is the Gyeongju area, not a proven exact founding point. Deferred for duplicate and uncertainty review.
- **Nalanda monastery:** `wpd_e9fdba5cce` is pinned in Sumatra although the named monastery was at Nalanda. The Indian Ministry of Culture identifies the inscription as a grant for a monastery constructed at Nalanda, issued in Devapala's 39th regnal year: https://indianculture.gov.in/artefacts-museums/nalanda-copper-plate-grant. The inscription's absolute date and its relationship to the construction date are uncertain: the Siam Society review cites circa 850, or as late as 860: https://thesiamsociety.org/wp-content/uploads/1974/03/JSS_062_2n_ChandChirayuRajani_ReviewArticleBackgroundToSriVijayaPartII.pdf. An inscription date is not necessarily a construction date. Deferred; do not silently treat 860 as established construction year.
- **Qing expedition to Tibet, 1720:** `wpd_5d4e9d7e01` and `wpw_fa0e00e5a9` overlap in subject and both have a point in Xinjiang. The campaign is broader than one location; Qing troops entered Lhasa in October 1720 according to https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/the-snow-lion-and-the-dragon-china-tibet-and-the-dalai-lama.html. A third century record `wpy_acc89f738a` has an unrelated midpoint year 1750. Deferred pending semantic duplicate and scope handling.
- **Concordat of Worms, 1122:** `wpd_1d6ddd1d8c` is not near Worms; https://www.britannica.com/event/Concordat-of-Worms confirms the 1122 agreement, and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordat_of_Worms describes documents exchanged outside the city and representatives. Candidate `7239ff17140995db4f475a85`, 12th century rev 1378517147. A targeted scan found no identical 1122 event name but cannot prove no semantic duplicate. Not applied in this pass; coordinate precision and runtime behavior still require review.
