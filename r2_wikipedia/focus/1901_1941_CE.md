# 1901–1941 CE: partial Wikipedia pass

**Years worked:** 1901, 1911, 1921, 1931 and 1941. These years are not fully collected. The intervening years and most linked articles remain for later.

## Implemented in indexed game data

Updated **25 existing records** and added **one** across three indexed history packs. IDs and stored year ranges of the updated records were preserved. Highlights:

- Placed Queen Victoria's death at Osborne House, distinct from her funeral at Windsor; corrected both Boxer Protocol entries to a Beijing city proxy.
- Corrected the Anglo-Aro War to an Arochukwu-area proxy, Wuchang Uprising to Wuhan, 1911 Tabriz fighting to Iran, the 1921–1924 Waziristan campaign to a Razmak-area proxy, the Cyprus revolt to Cyprus, and the Madeira uprising to Funchal. These were not single-site events: proxies do not represent their complete extent.
- Put the signing of the 1921 Treaty of Moscow in Moscow; clarified that the Anglo-Irish Treaty provided for the Irish Free State, which was established in 1922.
- Reworded the Tulsa massacre record to avoid presenting an uncertain death toll as certain. The execution of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev is now located at a Lahore city proxy; the Gandhi–Irwin Pact and Second Round Table Conference are placed in Delhi and London respectively.
- Moved the Hadamar killing-centre record from Berlin to a Hadamar town proxy; located Roosevelt's Four Freedoms speech at the US Capitol. The Subhas Chandra Bose record now places his 1941 arrival in Kabul without prematurely making him Indian National Army leader (his leadership began in 1943).
- Corrected the 1921 Santa Isabel shipwreck: the Wikipedia year page says Mexico and 244 deaths, but Spanish historical reports locate it off Sálvora in Galicia and report 213 deaths. The island coordinate is a proxy, not the wreck position.
- Added a Vienna proxy for the 1911 International Women's Day demonstration. An all-index name scan for Woman's/Women's Day and Frauentag found no match; this does not prove no semantic duplicate exists.

Each changed record's `r2SourceEvidence` in the game JSON and the review manifest contains links and a reason. Main Wikipedia sources include [Osborne House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osborne_House), [Boxer Protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxer_Protocol), [Wuchang Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuchang_Uprising), [Anglo-Irish Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Irish_Treaty), [Tulsa race massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_race_massacre), [Bhagat Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagat_Singh), [Hadamar killing centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamar_killing_centre) and [International Women's Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Women%27s_Day). The Santa Isabel correction uses the [Spanish maritime history account](https://vadebarcos.net/2019/01/05/el-naufragio-del-santa-isabel-la-mayor-tragedia-maritima-civil-de-galicia-en-el-siglo-xx/) and [another Spanish historical account](https://www.laaventuradelahistoria.es/el-vapor-santa-isabel-un-titanic-en-la-costa-gallega); the Gandhi–Irwin location is supported by the [Gandhi Heritage Portal](https://www.gandhiheritageportal.org/gandhi-irwin-pact).

## People and remaining work

33 linked people leads are saved in `history/sources/wikipedia_1901_1941_ce.json`. These are research leads, **not spawnable VIPs**; many linked biographies were not read in detail, and roles in one event should not be generalized to other years. Natural VIP spawning and historical branching are not implemented by this pass.

An inclusive stored-range scan found **814 hazard-pack overlaps** with these sample years. They are unchanged and unreviewed; the scan does not establish gameplay activation. The Waziristan entry's stored 1921–1922 interval remains shorter than the 1921–1924 campaign in its linked article; end-boundary behavior differs between readers, so it was not changed without further review. Several duplicate or overlapping records remain, including both Boxer Protocol and Anglo-Irish Treaty summaries, two Triangle fire records, and the 1931 Mukden summaries. Other obvious incorrect legacy locations, including Dendi, Agar Dinka and the Kenya revolt, remain uncorrected pending source verification. The Second Round Table Conference sources conflict on the opening day; no day is asserted. The current Russian occupation article dates its beginning to 1909; the preserved 1911 slot is qualified as an episode.
