## Indexed game history

Two cultural notes were added to the indexed regional pack:

* `r2_bent_pyramid_sneferu_context_c2600` references Sneferu's [Bent Pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bent_Pyramid&oldid=1381872821) at Dahshur, dated approximately c.2600 in the article. This is a reference slot, not an established construction, completion or personal-presence year. The 20 km radius is not the footprint of the pyramid.
* `r2_akalamdug_ur_context_c2600` references Akalamdug at the Ur cemetery site. The [century overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=26th_century_BC&oldid=1371895844) gives c.2600 for his reign; the [biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Akalamdug&oldid=1358747822) gives c.2550. Neither is proof of an exact accession, death, burial or presence in 2600. The cemetery coordinates are a site reference, not a confirmed palace or event location. The biography presents conflicting father/son hypotheses about Akalamdug and Meskalamdug.

Both retain explicit uncertainty in the visible name and source evidence. NewsIndex may include the stored end year, repeating a note. Indexed name scans found no dedicated entries for these specific subjects before addition, but a name scan cannot prove there are no semantic duplicates.

Existing qualified notes cover the Fourth Dynasty, Early Dynastic III, early written copies of the Instructions of Shuruppak, Crete, Los Millares, Stonehenge, Caral and early Mature Harappan regional context. The [Minoan civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoan_civilization) article uses EM I/II boundaries that differ from the 26th-century overview, and the existing Crete note already records differing phase labels. [Caral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caral) and [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1269/) describe archaeological context, not exact founding on a known day or annual activity. No new claims of precise year events were inferred.
