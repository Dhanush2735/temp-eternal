## Indexed game JSON

The additions cover Pepin the Short's Frankish kingship and anointing in 751, the *Kaifūsō* anthology compiled in 751, Carloman I's death and succession in 771, and the coronations of Pepin of Italy and Louis of Aquitaine in 781. Writes are atomic per pack, not across all packs. Each after-image records sources and a reason.

A Tang Annan note was moved from central China to a northern Vietnam regional reference; linked accounts disagree on the rebel chronology. Alfonso II's later move of the Asturian capital is distinguished from his 791 accession.

Coordinates labeled site, city, capital or region are gameplay references, not proof of exact event sites or territorial footprints. Readers use different end-year conventions, and an `effect: note` does **not** guarantee suppression in every reader. Moved records and saved-world behavior were not tested.
