# 2910 BCE — ten-year step

## History added to the game

The [30th-century Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=30th_century_BC&oldid=1371895853) gives around **2910 BCE** as an estimate for the death of Egyptian ruler Semerkhet. I added it to the indexed game history as **one debated estimate**. The page does not separately cite that date, and other chronologies differ.

The map point is near his tomb at Abydos, not a claim about where he died.
