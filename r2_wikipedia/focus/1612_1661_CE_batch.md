# R2 preliminary pass: 1612–1661 CE

Reviewed 2026-10-31. This is a **partial pass**, not full collection of any year or of all linked articles.

## Coverage

- Resolved all 50 annual titles and archived their pinned raw wikitext, with transclusions unexpanded. An indexed scan found 3,585 records starting in the interval; this is an inventory, not an individual review.
- The scan included 1,286 disaster-pack rows: 929 plague, 150 volcano, 127 earthquake, 47 tsunami, 30 famine and 3 flood. The changes below address selected rows only; the rest are not validated.
- Selective research graph: 40 people, 21 relationships and 33 event-role links across 12 indexed IDs. It includes rulers, opponents, relatives and an enslaved woman, but does not establish co-presence, a complete biography, spawn eligibility or natural NPC spawning.
- The title inventory recognizes 4,650 distinct annual titles (2,989 BCE and 1,661 CE), not completed histories. Eleven early BCE entries remain outside that inventory. **Fully collected years established: 0.**

## Indexed gameplay changes

Fourteen existing records were guarded and updated, retaining their IDs:

- Moved the Treaty of Concordia's active political record from Argentina to an approximate Saint Martin island reference. Its political effect and radius still need validation.
- Corrected Shivaji's birthplace from Pune to Shivneri Fort, and changed the birth record to a note rather than treating birth itself as a political effect. The Pratapgarh duplicate is a note; the battle article gives 10 November 1659 while the old record gave 9 November, so the exact day remains open.
- Corrected a Deccan gazetteer record's Bidar siege from 1656 to 1657, retaining its original quotation as provenance and the separate active world siege. Its gameplay impact remains unvalidated.
- Changed the 1619 record to describe enslaved Africans arriving in Virginia at Point Comfort, not a first arrival of Africans in the Americas or present-day United States. Earlier enslavement occurred in the Americas. The graph distinguishes Angela's likely arrival aboard the *Treasurer* from the *White Lion* group; her birth date and other details are not invented.
- Reframed the 1636–1637 Qing invasion of Joseon, the longer Ming–Qing transition and the 1644 change of control in Beijing. Corrected the 1642 Kaifeng flood's location and recorded differing accounts of who breached the dikes; the casualty estimate may include famine and plague.
- Corrected a 1626 Ndongo-related record from the Pyrenees to a broad Luanda regional reference, not a precise battlefield. Qualified Japanese foreign-contact restrictions, Morisco expulsions, and the Westphalia peace rather than treating complex developments as instantaneous or complete.

Twenty selected hazards were moved from the disaster pack to indexed regional notes: eight volcanic records with uncertain start years, and twelve NOAA tsunami records assigned event-validity values 0 or 1. Moving them avoids presenting an uncertain year or interpretation as a deterministic disaster; it does **not** mean the underlying observations did not happen. NOAA source descriptions sometimes disagree about whether the observed water movement was a tsunami. Catalog entries with no validity assessment were not moved solely on that basis.

Examples include Raoul Island 1630 ± 50, Saba in or before 1640, Long Island 1660 ± 20 and Nabukelevu 1660 ± 30, as given by the Smithsonian GVP. Lassen's catalog describes an uncertain eruption and a rock avalanche that may have occurred without one. Of the original scanned rows, 1,266 remain in the disaster pack, including all 929 plague rows; other hazards, duplicates, damages and radii require further work.

## Lossless storage consolidation

Thirty-three earlier indexed scans were consolidated as `indexed_scan_12_1611_solid.tar.xz`; `scan_archive.read()` provides fallback and `python r2_wikipedia/scan_archive.py <former-filename>` restores the original xz without overwriting. Every raw and original xz byte hash was verified. The old annual solid archive plus 1512–1611 annual members were consolidated with this interval. The original 31-member archive can be reconstructed byte-for-byte by `annual_archive.restore_original_solid_archive(path)`; its original manifest is retained and the reconstruction was tested. Earlier reports may name paths now provided through these restoration helpers.
