# 801–841 CE: partial Wikipedia research and installation

**Years sampled:** 801, 811, 821, 831 and 841 CE. This is a partial pass, not full collection of those years or their linked articles. Intervening years remain for later.

## Installed changes

31 existing records were updated and three added in five indexed game history packs. The guarded manifest is `r2_wikipedia/reviews/801_841_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `cd7e85928d239eccf4775e2f312db12b3c5b1cb74bf634df6cc72fdd89430617`). The installer is `r2_wikipedia/apply_801_841_ce_context.py` and contains exact before-images for reversal. It writes atomically per pack, not transactionally across all packs.

- **801:** Corrected the apparent Chelsea council's regional point, while keeping the council and site identification qualified; distinguished Rabanus Maurus's ordination from his undated vows. Added Bera's post-siege appointment in Barcelona. The indexed Barcelona siege is retained with its existing 800–801 stored boundaries; different readers handle end years differently. The 801 year page and Obotrites article disagree on the date of the Nordalbian land transfer (801 versus 804).
- **811:** Clarified the Pliska battle and its possible duplicate decade entry without claiming an exact ambush site. The treaty of Heiligen's former Baltic-coast point was moved to a broad Rendsburg/Eider reference; the treaty article does not establish the exact meeting site and rejects an unsupported contemporary boundary claim. The Rayy and Byzantine-succession entries now identify participants more clearly.
- **821:** Corrected the Yemen governorship point from Iraq to a Sana'a regional reference and noted the joint governorship. Corrected the Borna/Vladislav entry's geographic reference and added the death of Coenwulf and succession of his brother. The Kenelm murder account is a later legend, not established history. Lothair and Ermengarde's October marriage at Thionville is clarified.
- **831:** The Palermo year entry points to Palermo and cross-references the already indexed siege. The Theophilos article differs from the year page over the chronology of Tarsos/Tyana and the 831 campaign; these claims are qualified and the Cilicia point is not an exact battlefield. Charsianon remains a separate indexed battle. Nominoe's appointment and Ansgar's visit to Birka are qualified; his Rome visit is not asserted to have taken place in 831. Omurtag's succession uses Pliska as a regional capital reference, not a death site.
- **841:** The Mount Latros entry now correctly describes Cretan raiders attacking the monasteries and places the point in the mountain region in Anatolia, not Crete. The Jumièges point identifies the abbey, while the broader event includes Rouen. Dublin's Viking settlement is distinguished from the earlier Gaelic settlement; its longphort site is debated. Al-Mubarqa's revolt uses a Palestine regional reference instead of Baghdad and acknowledges conflicting dates. Added Ennin's recorded comet sighting at Chang'an; this is a historical note, not an implemented sky feature. Fontenoy names the battle participants.

Sources for each event are included in its `r2SourceEvidence`. Examples: [Treaty of Heiligen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Heiligen), [Nu'aym ibn al-Waddah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nu%27aym_ibn_al-Waddah_al-Azdi), [Theophilos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theophilos_(emperor)), [Constantine Kontomytes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_Kontomytes), [History of Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Dublin), and [Comet of 841–842](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_of_841%E2%80%93842). The decade pages often repeat year-page material and are not independent corroboration. Coordinates marked city, mountain, capital or region are proxies, not proof of precise event locations or territorial extent.

## Unresolved and active

- `q403416` and `wpd_7984a5b06a` both refer to the Pliska battle. `q139257629` and `wpy_dbd23f632a` both refer to Palermo's siege/fall. The 840 and 841 Dublin entries may overlap. Both IDs of each pair remain indexed; cross-reference wording does not prove that every reader suppresses duplicate effects. The 801 Bera appointment is distinct from, but historically related to, the earlier-starting Barcelona siege.
- The inclusive scan found **30 hazard rows** touching at least one sampled year, including `hz_d05073c25c` (811 St Andrews earthquake) and `hz_91432f3895` (841–846 famine). All remain active and unreviewed. Many begin in a prior year, and an inclusive overlap does not prove a given reader triggers the record. `Disasters.java` loads hazard rows independently; changing a Wikipedia record does not neutralize hazards.
- The active `q117751606` Apennine earthquake retains its old point. Its [linked article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/801_Apennine_earthquake) gives coordinates but also says the probable epicenter was between Spoleto and Perugia; the discrepancy needs specialist checking before relocation. Conchas de Arganzón and other unreviewed rows remain active. Other people, sources and linked articles have not been exhausted.
- Al-Mubarqa's 841–842 chronology differs across sources, so the stored 841 slot should not be read as a verified full duration. Historical end-year conventions vary between readers. Saved-world suppression and moved-event behavior remain untested.
