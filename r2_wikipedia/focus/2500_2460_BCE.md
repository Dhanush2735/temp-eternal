# 2500 to 2460 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2500, 2490, 2480, 2470 and 2460 BCE**. I followed the Wikipedia century pages and some linked articles. This is **not a complete collection** of those years.

## Game history updated

I corrected **seven entries** and added **one** in the game's indexed history files:

- **Malta and Scotland:** clarified the uncertain dates of the Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum and Skara Brae; Skara Brae's ending was not presented as a political event.
- **Iraq and Syria:** replaced an exact founding claim about Assyria with a cautious Assur site reference, and moved the Ebla archive reference to an approximate date supported by a source. Its dating remains debated.
- **South Asia and Europe:** corrected a broken Mohenjo-daro entry and a misleading claim about the origins of Corded Ware.
- **Malaysia:** corrected the rice entry to refer to evidence at Gua Sireh in Sarawak, with uncertain dating rather than an exact date for all Malaysia.
- **Egypt:** added a Fifth Dynasty period note using one approximate dating scheme. Other estimates differ.

Long period notes may appear in several years. That does **not** mean an event happened every year. Some corrected entries changed location or date; effects on existing saves have not been tested.

## People for later

I saved **five research leads**, including Userkaf, Sahure, Neferirkare, Neferefre and Meretnebty. They are **not active VIP NPCs**. Uncertain dates and family relationships remain marked as uncertain.
