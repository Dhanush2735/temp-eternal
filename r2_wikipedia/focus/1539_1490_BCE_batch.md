## Indexed game JSON

**Sixteen existing records were corrected or qualified; eight imprecisely dated volcano hazards were moved to non-destructive indexed notes.** IDs, nominal year slots and hazard coordinates were preserved. No new event was added.

- [Amenhotep I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amenhotep_I) and [Thutmose I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thutmose_I) have alternative high and low chronologies. Egyptian dynasty boundaries, succession, military campaigns and Hatshepsut's later reign no longer claim precise events in these reference years. Related notes overlap and are not proof of multiple distinct events.
- [Senenmut's tomb ceiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_ceiling_of_Senenmut%27s_Tomb) is associated with Hatshepsut's reign; 1534 BCE is a proposed sky configuration, not an established year of manufacture. The geographic reference was corrected to the Theban tomb area.
- [Miletus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miletus) was corrected from a Crete point to Miletus; earlier Minoan connections do not establish a one-year migration in 1500 BCE. [Phoenician script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenician_alphabet) proper is later than the early alphabetic traditions represented in that year's entry.
- Entries for Joseph, Jacob and the Exodus now describe biblical traditions or later chronological schemes, not verified political changes. Cranaus and Amphictyon are clearly marked as figures of Greek myth, not a confirmed 1497 BCE coup. These are notes; no judgement about religious belief is implied.
- **Apameia:** `q11908709` refers to a supposed 15th-century BCE conflict, not the separate [998 CE Battle of Apamea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Apamea). The [Wikidata item](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q11908709) has an unreferenced century date; its [Catalan article](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batalla_d%27Apamea) names Thutmose III and Parsatatar but gives no exact year. The cited book was not independently inspected. [Baratarna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baratarna) describes competing scholarly identifications of Parshatatar and Barattarna. The exact 1500 military effect was changed to a qualified note, with the original slot and coordinates retained only as references.
