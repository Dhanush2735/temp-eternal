# 2000–1960 BCE: five history steps, partly worked

**Years worked on:** 2000, 1990, 1980, 1970 and 1960 BCE.  
**Overall: 114 focus years partly worked on; 0 fully collected.** The years between these ten-year steps still need work. Full game review comes later.

## What changed in the game

The indexed game JSON now includes **17 corrections and 11 new historical notes**:

- **Thailand:** corrected Ban Chiang’s map position and marked its early bronze dates as disputed. A competing chronology places the settlement and bronze later.
- **Indus region:** replaced an exact 2000 BCE “decline” with qualified Harappan context. The famous “Priest-King” sculpture is now pinned to its findspot at Mohenjo-daro rather than a museum in Karachi; its title is speculative. A Harappa torso’s dating is also disputed.
- **Crete and Japan:** Knossos was settled long before 2000 BCE; the approximate first-palace reference was moved to about 1900 BCE. Jōmon phase dates vary, so 2000 is not presented as a definite boundary.
- **Egypt and Mesopotamia:** corrected a too-precise Egyptian dynastic transition and the location/date of Erishum I. Added approximate reign contexts for Mentuhotep III, Mentuhotep IV, Shu-Ilishu and Iddin-Dagan. Scholars use different chronologies; the notes do not establish their precise personal whereabouts.
- **Elsewhere:** corrected or qualified references to Wrangel Island mammoths, Capacha, Matanchén, Mantua, Stonehenge, Cagayan shell middens, horse domestication, Kura–Araxes and Beni Hasan. Added context for Kerma, Caral, Únětice, Sintashta, Shimao, Taosi and Crete.

Ranges are approximate historical context, not proof of an event every year. The game’s news loader may repeat them annually. Some corrections change dates and map positions of existing IDs, which may affect saved games.
