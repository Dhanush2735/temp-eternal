# 101–141 CE: partial research and game-data update

**Focus years:** 101, 111, 121, 131, 141 CE. These are five ten-year steps, not every intervening year. They are **partly worked, not fully collected**.

## What changed in the active history JSON

16 existing records were updated in four indexed packs; IDs preserved, with no new rows and no pack moves. Full before/after records, evidence links and rationales are in `r2_wikipedia/reviews/101_141_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `2e30a8b6d3d4a3e67191d920257bb333dd452fa935df13d30326619f2cf8b328`). Examples:

- The Centaurs and Wild Beasts mosaic at Hadrian's Villa was incorrectly a military battle. It is now an approximate-date cultural note. The quoted 120–130 range is approximate, not proof of an artwork being produced throughout those years.
- Hadrian's Wall marker moved from Italy to northern England; its construction probably began around 122, with possible earlier work. Hadrian's visit to Britain was moved from the 120 bucket to 122. The year-page claim about a 121 border decision is a qualified note rather than a certain political event.
- The Temple of Baalshamin was substantially rebuilt in 131, not first founded then. Temple of Faustina construction began in 141; initial dedication was to Faustina alone. Temple locations and stage of construction were clarified; Trajan's Column moved to its location in Trajan's Forum and became a cultural note.
- Pliny's 109 and 111 rows are conflicting references to the same governorship, not proof of two separate appointments. Aelia Capitolina's foundation date, and the Han 121 era-name entry, are qualified where sources conflict. Luoyang markers are modern city-level approximations to the Eastern Han capital, not proven ceremony sites.
- The Lycia earthquake epicenter and year are uncertain, so its world-pack point lockdown is now an informational note. The separate 142 NOAA earthquake and tsunami hazard records remain active and their relationship to the Lycia record is unresolved; they may overlap. The winter 101–102 Adamclisi battle is similarly an informational note rather than military presence for both entire years.

## People for later VIP research

15 sourced leads in `history/sources/wikipedia_101_141_ce.json`, including Trajan, Decebalus, Pliny, Antinous, Deng Sui, Cai Lun, Joshua ben Hananiah and Cheng Yu. These are research notes, **not loaded NPCs**. The AD 141 year page says Faustina died in 141; her biography dates her death to 140, so that conflict is recorded. Cai Lun improved papermaking rather than inventing the first paper. Taejo chronology remains disputed. Existing leads may overlap earlier research.

## Sources and remaining work

The five English Wikipedia focus-year pages, selected linked articles and selected sources were read, including [Hadrian's Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian%27s_Wall), [Baalshamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Baalshamin), [Lycia earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/141_Lycia_earthquake), [Pliny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pliny_the_Younger), [Deng Sui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deng_Sui), [Cai Lun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cai_Lun), [Bithynia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bithynia) and [Aelia Capitolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aelia_Capitolina). English Heritage and NOAA were used for comparison, as documented in the manifest. Wikipedia revisions are not independently pinned. This is a targeted partial pass, not recursive coverage of all linked articles or all worldwide events.

The 142 NOAA hazard rows still run, and the existing world-pack and Wikidata-pack Gazetteer/NewsIndex end-year conventions differ: NewsIndex may display an extra year for world-pack records. Other active records, war overlaps, place anchors and source conflicts remain unchecked. Saved-world effects have not been tested. Atomic writes are per pack, not transactional across all packs.
