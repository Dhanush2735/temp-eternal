## Installed corrections

- **1954 Rugby League World Cup final** (`wpr_083711ce87`): moved from a UK regional placeholder to Paris (48.8567, 2.3522), a city proxy rather than the Parc des Princes stadium. Reduced the incident's note radius from 800 km to an approximate 20 km; this is not a sourced venue boundary. The 1954 stadium was later rebuilt. [Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954_Rugby_League_World_Cup_final), [stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_des_Princes), [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris).
- **1955 Emmett Till lynching** (`wpr_ae7258df8c`): changed the old Money coordinate to Drew (33.81000, −90.53028), a city proxy for the murder, not an exact crime location. The wider case spans multiple places, including Money and the river; a single point cannot represent it. Reworded the disputed store encounter without asserting the alleged behavior as fact. Roy Bryant and J. W. Milam were acquitted in 1955 and admitted the killing later, in 1956; the annual record cannot represent that sequence precisely. [Emmett Till](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmett_Till), [Drew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew,_Mississippi), [Money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money,_Mississippi).

IDs, year ranges, kinds and `note` effects are unchanged. The corrections change geographic visibility and, for the final, the radius. Exact-phrase indexed searches found one record for each subject in this context, but cannot eliminate semantically different duplicates. Neither change was tested in a running game or saved world.

## Research people and limits

`history/sources/wikipedia_1954_1955_ce_people_links.json.gz` contains ten nonspawnable research leads, six sourced associations and ten event roles: three rugby finalists and seven people connected to Till's case, including his mother Mamie Till-Mobley, great-uncle Mose Wright, and NAACP figures Medgar Evers and Amzie Moore. Aftermath and family roles do not imply presence at the killing. These are not complete biographies, natural-spawn schedules or alternate-timeline behavior.

Deferred examples: the 1955 *Kashmir Princess* record is placed on Taiwan although its article says the aircraft crashed off the Natuna Islands; a suitable gameplay representation of the at-sea location and political effect remains to be checked. Hurricane Diane has a northeastern-US disaster record placed in Canada as well as a separate 1955 storm record; its geographic representation, differing casualty estimates and possible stacked disruption need review before edits. [Kashmir Princess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir_Princess) has citation warnings; [Hurricane Diane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Diane) describes a multi-state path and flooding.

## Space management

Two generated reference outputs were XZ-compressed and checked against their original bytes before removing the uncompressed copies: `harvest/events_v2_raw.jsonl.pages.json.xz` and `survey/sources/sources_density.json.xz`. Use `python r2_wikipedia/archive_generated_aux.py NAME` to verify or add `--restore` when sufficient workspace headroom is available; restoration refuses overwrite and checks raw and archive hashes. Their generators continue to write the original paths. The 125,000,000-byte storage guard was not increased. Continued collection needs more verified space management.
