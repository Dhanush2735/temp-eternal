# 2050–2010 BCE: five history steps, partly worked

**Years worked on:** 2050, 2040, 2030, 2020 and 2010 BCE.  
**Overall: 109 focus years partly worked on; 0 fully collected.** These counts describe collection, not the later full game review. The years between the ten-year steps still need work.

## What changed in the game

The indexed game JSON now includes **seven corrections, ten new historical notes and one corrected hazard record**:

- **Egypt:** corrected Mentuhotep II's unsupported death date and qualified the date of reunification. Scholars use different chronologies. The period boundary and reunification notes describe related, distinct aspects of the transition.
- **Mesopotamia:** added reign contexts for Amar-Sin, Shu-Sin and Ibbi-Sin. Corrected the Isin record for Ishbi-Erra and made clear that Naplanum's supposed kingship of Larsa is disputed.
- **Nubia:** marked the start of Middle Kerma as an approximate archaeological boundary; sources differ between about 2050 and 2000 BCE.
- **Britain:** clarified that Seahenge's timbers were tree-ring dated to felling in spring or early summer 2049 BCE; construction then is an archaeological inference. The site's purpose remains uncertain.
- **Korea:** moved an uncertain Halla eruption record out of the destructive disaster loader and into history as a non-disruptive note. The volcano catalog gives approximately 2050 BCE **±200 years**, not a confirmed exact year.
- Added continuation notes for the Indus region, Caral, Únětice, Shimao, Taosi, Crete and Sintashta. Date ranges are context, not proof of an event every year; the game's news loader may repeat them.
