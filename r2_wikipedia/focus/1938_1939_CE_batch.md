## Indexed gameplay corrections

Eleven existing records were updated, retaining their IDs:

- Moved two 1938 Munich Agreement summaries to a **Munich city proxy**, the signing location, rather than the Sudetenland. Another indexed agreement record was already in Munich. All three remain and retain their political effects; whether those overlapping effects stack needs a gameplay review.
- Moved two 1938 Yellow River flood records to a **Huayuankou town proxy** for the intentional levee breach, rather than near Chongqing. The flooding covered a much larger area and lasted beyond the initial year. Both records remain, including their existing disruption effects; possible duplicate behavior is untested. The separate hazard-pack inventory was not individually reviewed.
- Moved the start of the bombing of Chongqing to a **Chongqing city proxy**. Clarified the Kristallnacht summary to distinguish murder from mass arrests, without asserting an exact death toll; Berlin represents one location in a wider pogrom.
- Moved two 1939 Hungarian invasion of Carpatho-Ukraine summaries to a **Khust regional proxy**, not Russia. Moved a Khalkhin Gol battle summary to a battle-area proxy, adding Mongolian participation. Existing Khalkhin Gol battle records also remain.
- Moved the Khalkhin Gol ceasefire summary to the battle-area proxy, **not** a claimed signing location, and changed it from battle/military presence to a political note. Changed the founding of Hòa Hảo from an automatic political effect to a cultural note and moved it to a **Châu Đốc regional proxy** near the founding village, not an exact site.

All radii are gameplay approximations, not exact sites or complete event boundaries. Except for the ceasefire and Hòa Hảo changes stated above, existing kinds and effects were retained. The checked `1939 Ondonga uprising` title was missing; that record was left untouched. None of the 223 inventoried hazard-pack starts was individually reviewed or moved. The Yellow River changes above concern overlapping non-hazard indexed packs, not proof that other flood records are correct.
