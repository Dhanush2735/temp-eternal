## Eight targeted corrections

- **1956 Poznań protests** (`wpy_2157f80113`): corrected the claim that Soviet troops killed exactly 53. [The event article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Pozna%C5%84_protests) describes Polish forces under the Polish-Soviet general Poplavsky; death estimates range from 57 to slightly over 100 and differ in evidentiary support. Location, radius and disruptive effect retained. The other indexed row `wpw_8fa202300d` describes the same protests as a war/note; it remains. An exact/name search is not a complete semantic duplicate audit, and game stacking is not tested.
- **1956 South Pole landing/station** (`wpy_dfd3a90770`): moved from Virginia to the geographic South Pole (−90, 0), a proxy; longitude at the pole is arbitrary and this is not the exact moving station or landing point. Landing and construction are separately worded. [South Pole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Pole), [station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amundsen%E2%80%93Scott_South_Pole_Station). The Dufek biography has citation warnings; pole projection and gameplay behavior need live testing.
- **1957 Grapple 1** (`wpr_3f43f582c3`): moved from England to Malden Island proxy (−4.017, −154.933) and clarified that the explosion produced about 300 kilotons, below design yield, rather than implying no explosion. Radius 800→20 km is a local gameplay approximation, **not** a physical blast/fallout boundary. [Operation Grapple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Grapple), [Malden Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malden_Island).
- **1958 Explorer 4** (`wpr_ab3d2c7806`): moved from a Canadian regional placeholder to launch complex 5 (28.43944, −80.57333) at Cape Canaveral and 800→20 km. The complex is a launch-site proxy, not the satellite's orbit. [Explorer 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explorer_4), [complex 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Canaveral_Launch_Complex_5).
- **1959 Kitchen Debate** (`wpr_ad99c52265`): moved from a Canadian placeholder to Sokolniki Park, Moscow (55.80056, 37.66972), 800→20 km. Park coordinate is a proxy, not the exact kitchen; the event was a series of exchanges. [Kitchen Debate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitchen_Debate), [park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokolniki_Park) (latter has a citation warning).
- **1960 Avianca Flight 671** (`wpy_0deda83b99`): moved Kingston coordinate to Montego Bay airport area (18.5028, −77.9122). This is an airport-area proxy, not an exact impact point. [Flight 671](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avianca_Flight_671).
- **1960 New York mid-air collision** (`wpy_f518a721d6`): moved from Chicago to the Miller Field crash-site proxy (40.56861, −74.12194); the other aircraft fell in Park Slope. The annual row cannot represent both sites exactly. [Collision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960_New_York_mid-air_collision).
- **1960 USS Constellation fire** (`wpy_c053212422`): moved from Washington State to Brooklyn Navy Yard (40.70194, −73.96889), a yard proxy, and retained the existing 20 km disruption effect. This changes where disruption applies; its radius is not a sourced damage footprint. The carrier fire and New York air collision are two separate events three days apart. [Ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Constellation_(CV-64)), [yard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Navy_Yard).

All eight IDs, annual start/end fields, kinds and effects are unchanged; seven locations moved. Five retained radius 20 km and three broad note radii changed to 20 km. Each after-image contains pinned article links and rationale. The original annual scheduling and approximate radii remain gameplay simplifications. No live-game, pole-projection or saved-world test was performed.
