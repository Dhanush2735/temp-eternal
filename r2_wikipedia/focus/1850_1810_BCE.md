# 1850–1810 BCE: five history steps, partly worked

**Years worked on:** 1850, 1840, 1830, 1820 and 1810 BCE.  
**Overall: 129 focus years partly worked on; 0 fully collected.** The years between the ten-year steps still need work. The full game review comes later.

## What changed in the game

The indexed game JSON now has **19 new historical notes** and **10 corrected records**:

- **Mesopotamia and Egypt:** added approximate reign notes for rulers including Warad-Sin, Rim-Sîn I, Sabium, Senusret III and Amenemhat III. The dates depend on the historical chronology used; they are not exact life dates or personal schedules.
- **Egypt:** added reference notes for the Black Pyramid and the Kahun medical papyrus. Their years are approximate, not proven construction or writing dates.
- **Ecuador, South Asia, Sudan, the Eurasian steppe and Crete:** added regional culture notes. These describe broader periods, not evidence that a specific event happened every year.
- **China:** changed the old Mount Tai earthquake lockdown into a traditional-account note. The reported date and even the interpretation of the account are uncertain; alternative dates exist.
- **Volcanoes:** moved nine records out of the disruptive disaster pack into historical notes because their dates are uncertain. The Smithsonian lists these as **confirmed eruptions with uncertain dates**; this does not mean the eruptions themselves are unconfirmed. Eight other overlapping hazards were not reviewed and remain unchanged.

These notes use approximate dates and map references. Longer notes can repeat in game news; that does not prove annual activity. Changed dates or pack locations may affect saved games.
