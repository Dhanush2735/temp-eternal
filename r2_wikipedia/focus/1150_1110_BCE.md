**Years worked:** 1150, 1140, 1130, 1120 and 1110 BCE. The years between them remain for later. None of these years is fully collected.

## Changes made to the game

The indexed game history JSON now has **9 corrected records, 23 uncertain volcano records moved to history notes, one war note moved to history, and four new notes**. Seven corrections concern nearby years: 1154, 1152, 1145, 1137, 1136, 1122 and 1115 BCE. Existing IDs were kept.

- Corrected the location of a Deir el-Medina **workers’ strike** record from Munich to the Egyptian workers’ village. It happened in Ramesses III’s 29th regnal year; linked sources use different approximate BCE dates. The protests were not necessarily at the village itself.
- Corrected the **Ramesses VII** accession reference from Oxford to the Thebes region and a **Medinet Habu** inscription reference to the temple near Luxor. Their exact years or actions cannot be established from the displayed slots.
- Qualified the Egyptian withdrawal from **Canaan**, the archaeological evidence at **Troy VIIb1**, the **Gija Joseon** founding tradition, Egyptian reign dates and Assyrian accession. Myth and archaeological remains are not interchangeable. Some related records may appear more than once.
- Changed an existing **Nebuchadnezzar I** war-pack record into an uncertain reign note: it does not prove that a battle occurred in 1110 BCE. Added four reign-context notes for Ramesses VI, Ramesses VII, Nebuchadnezzar I and Tiglath-Pileser I. Their points are regional references, not proof of presence or an individual action that year.
- Moved **23 volcano records** from the active disaster pack to non-disruptive history notes. Smithsonian’s Global Volcanism Program records confirmed eruptive periods, but the dates are estimates, not proof of eruptions or damage in these exact years. Some nearby estimates may refer to related evidence. Coordinates for volcanic fields need not identify an actual vent.
