# 1989–1940 BCE: preliminary history work

**Progress: 1,458 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** The existing count uses shared period context as partial work, not a separate complete history for each year. Five of these 50 years—1980, 1970, 1960, 1950 and 1940 BCE—were already counted as endpoints, so this batch adds 45. Earlier years remain incomplete.

## Sources and scope

All 50 year titles resolved and redirect to the same [20th-century BCE article, revision 1378436905](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=20th_century_BC&oldid=1378436905). Resolving titles does not collect their histories. Selected linked articles and the individual GVP volcano profiles were checked; not every linked article was followed. The separate crawl remains blocked by its storage guard.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

Six existing records were corrected or enriched:

- Replaced the century article's unsourced claim that Egyptian authority became less centralized around 1985 BCE with a qualified administrative context. [Egypt's Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/en/historical-periods/middle-kingdom) describes consolidation during the Twelfth Dynasty; that does not prove a particular change in 1985. This retained ID overlaps a separate Amenemhat reign note and may need deduplication.
- Added the Metropolitan Museum's account of Amenemhat I and his son Senusret I's probable coregency to the existing [Amenemhat note](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544206). Chronologies vary and the precise location of the royal capital Itjtawy is not securely established.
- Added [Shu-Ilishu's](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shu-Ilishu&oldid=1359762483) recovery of a cult image and the Isin workshop archive as context. The article says whether the image was obtained by diplomacy or conflict is unknown.
- Added the regnal-year reference to [Iddin-Dagan's daughter Matum-Niatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Iddin-Dagan&oldid=1374010459) marrying a king of Anshan. Identifying him as Imazu is a proposal, not a certainty; the absolute date and location are not proven.
- Added Assur trade and inscription context for Ilu-shuma and Erishum I. The [Ikunum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikunum) and [Ilu-shuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ilu-shuma&oldid=1337665820) articles disagree about Ikunum's parentage, so the conflict remains open.

Eleven individually matched **GVP eruption rows** were moved from the destructive hazard pack to indexed non-disruptive historical notes, retaining their IDs, nominal year slots and reference points: Etna, Tenerife, Villarrica, Chikurachki, Graciosa, Ichinsky, Katla, Koryaksky, Mageik, Colima and Haleakala. All are confirmed eruptions in GVP but imprecisely dated. Examples include [Graciosa 1950 BCE ±1,400 years](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=382040) and [Chikurachki 1950 BCE (?) with VEI 4 also uncertain](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=290360). The manifest records every profile and dating method. A `?` is not a numerical interval, and a nominal date is not an exact eruption year. Moving the rows does not imply no damage occurred.

Game ranges and radii are reference conventions, not proof of yearly actions or actual footprints. Different readers interpret end years differently and may repeat notes. Date, kind and pack changes can affect existing saves.
