# 689–640 BCE: preliminary R2 batch

## Progress

The batch is **partly worked**, not fully collected. Under the existing shared-period counting convention, 45 additional years are counted after five previously counted decade endpoints: **2,628 years partly worked; 0 fully collected**. These figures are not counts of independent, complete annual histories. Next chronological batch: 639–590 BCE; all earlier work remains open.

## Research scope

- Resolved all 50 year titles: 30 redirect to decade articles and 20 resolve to individual year pages. The resolver results and revision identifiers are in `year_titles_689_640.json`. Resolution is not collection.
- Fetched complete revision wikitext for the 20 individual annual pages, archived as `year_wikitext_689_640.json.gz`; transclusions and templates remain unexpanded. Reviewed parts of all five decade pages and selected event and biography links. Several long pages were only read in part; this is not an exhaustive traversal of years, links, references, or the world.
- A preliminary `period_extracts_689_640.json` was deleted: MediaWiki limited multi-title whole-page extracts to one per request, so only three of 25 requested entries contained extracts. The compressed revision archive is a different, accurately scoped replacement, not 25 full rendered extracts.
- Scanned indexed event and site **start years** in this range: 173 rows (world 5, Wikidata 4, wars 1, Wikipedia annual 48, regional 88, decades 24, hazards 3). This is a candidate scan, not a review of every row or of events starting outside the range. Some entries overlap or contradict one another.

## People and limitations

`history/sources/wikipedia_689_640_people_links.json` contains **32 selected people references, 31 qualified relationships and 35 event roles across 10 indexed IDs**. It includes connected figures from Jin, Egypt, Elam, Assyria and Babylonia, not just principal rulers. Structural links were checked; identities, exact event attendance and narrative claims were not independently certified. This is research-only, not active VIP data: none were made spawnable. Natural VIP spawning, historically guided behavior and player-triggered branching remain unimplemented.

Many other indexed claims, possible duplicates and geographic errors remain, including incomplete decade-page and linked-article coverage. Astronomical BCE/year-zero and pack boundary conventions require further checking. No comprehensive loader, Java probe, game, save or integrity test was run this batch; the built jar remains stale. The crawler is storage-blocked.
