## Installed game-data changes

- **Madagascar / Indian Ocean:** The 2807 BCE entry `wpr_137316d6ad` classified a suggested impact and tsunami as a disruptive disaster. The [Burckle article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Burckle_Crater&oldid=1362897698) says the crater's impact origin is contested; the [Fenambosy article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Fenambosy_Chevron&oldid=1339428639) reports disagreement over whether the landforms are tsunami deposits. The entry now labels the 2807 date and event as a disputed proposal and uses `cultural` / `note` instead of `disaster` / `disruption`. Its retained Madagascar point marks the chevron region, not the proposed undersea crater; its radius is not a verified damage footprint. This changes the regional history record, not the separately loaded hazards pack; saved-world effects and runtime behavior were not tested.
- **Mainland Greece and Mesopotamia:** Extended existing Early Helladic I and southern Mesopotamian city background notes (`wpy_edef47079c`, `wpy_2e31a84306`) through the selected 2790 BCE slice. [Helladic chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Helladic_chronology&oldid=1380683880) gives a broader archaeological sequence; the [Met's Uruk essay](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/uruk-the-first-city) describes continuing city development and the Early Dynastic period. The selected endpoints and regional points are game references, not exact transitions or proof of events in each year. Notes can repeat in news and may overlap related background.

The existing 2832 BCE Methuselah note already marks its age as disputed. The [tree article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Methuselah_(pine_tree)&oldid=1373708832) describes a missing core and competing estimate. The note's location is only a broad regional reference.

The [Sumerian King List](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sumerian_King_List&oldid=1377491582) survives in later versions that mix historical and fictional material. Its flood stories and genealogies do not establish the proposed 2807 impact or reliably date individual kings to this batch. [Gilgamesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Gilgamesh&oldid=1376349542) may have had a historical basis but is also a literary hero; no exact-year NPC was inferred.
