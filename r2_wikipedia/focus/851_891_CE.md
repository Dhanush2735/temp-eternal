# 851–891 CE: partial Wikipedia research and installation

**Years sampled:** 851, 861, 871, 881 and 891 CE. These years are partly worked, not fully collected. The intervening years and many linked articles remain for later.

## Installed in the game project

Updated **33 existing records** and added **four records** across five indexed history packs. The guarded change manifest, with exact before- and after-images and links for each change, is `r2_wikipedia/reviews/851_891_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `0365d551903d0d15466e2dfe77a9aa0b0ba3358703e5ab28b7604bc93f56ce5f`). The installer is `r2_wikipedia/apply_851_891_ce_context.py`; writes are atomic per pack, not across packs.

- **851:** Corrected the Great Mosque of Samarra's point and qualified its completion as 850–851. Clarified that Bagrat II was taken to Samarra, and qualified the years of Pepin II's capture and Íñigo Arista's death. Added the Angers meeting after Jengland. The Battle of Sandwich article dates that naval engagement to 851, but the Alfred biography gives 850; the 851 slot remains a reference pending more research.
- **861:** Moved the assassination of al-Mutawakkil to a Samarra city reference, clarified al-Farghani's Roda nilometer and Ya’qub ibn al-Layth's Sistan accession, and added the Silla succession and Constantinople council. The Nilometer article has conflicting captions; al-Farghani's biography supports completion in 861.
- **871:** Corrected Bari and London-area points and replaced a misplaced Wessex accession point with a Winchester regional reference. Marked the Ashdown and Meretun battle sites as unknown. The Geniza is a collection spanning centuries, not a work written in 871. Tønsberg's exact founding date is uncertain; Rhodri Mawr's influence beyond Gwynedd is debated. The Tønsberg and Geniza records retain their 871 slots only as references.
- **881:** Reclassified the Bakong completion as a cultural note. The Caltavuturo battle article gives 881 or 882 and displays coordinates near Taormina, whereas the linked town of Caltavuturo is farther west. The stored point is now a town-region proxy, **not a verified battlefield**; the contradiction still needs checking. The Acre earthquake world record now notes that its historicity is questioned, potentially because of confusion with the 1303 Crete earthquake. This change does not disable any separately loaded hazards.
- **891:** Moved Guy III's imperial coronation to Rome and the Poley battle reference to Aguilar de la Frontera in Spain rather than Baghdad. The Aguilar article displays two different coordinates, so the town point is approximate. Clarified that al-Mu’tadid became regent in 891 and caliph in 892. Added a research-based death note for Fujiwara no Mototsune, with Kyoto only a court-region proxy.
- **Adjacent year correction:** The Battle of Englefield is dated around 31 December **870** by its linked article. Its existing ID `q940959` was moved from stored 871–872 to 870–871. Different game readers handle the end boundary differently, so this does not prove it disappears from all 871 views. Saved-world effects are untested.

Examples of linked sources: [Battle of Englefield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Englefield), [Great Mosque of Samarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Mosque_of_Samarra), [Al-Farghani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Farghani), [Tønsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B8nsberg), [Cairo Geniza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_Geniza), [Caltavuturo battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Caltavuturo), [Acre earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/881_Acre_earthquake), [Guy III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_III_of_Spoleto), [Umar ibn Hafsun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_ibn_Hafsun), and [Al-Mu’tadid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Mu%27tadid). Wikipedia year and decade pages often repeat the same material and do not count as independent corroboration. Geographic proxies do not establish exact event sites or territorial extent.

## Unresolved and active

- The inclusive scan found **43 hazard rows** whose stored ranges touch at least one sampled year. All remain active and unreviewed; the overlap scan does not establish when every consumer triggers them. `Disasters.java` loads hazards independently of the changed world record. The 881 Spain/Algeria/Morocco earthquake and Cádiz tsunami records are distinct from the disputed Acre world row and were not investigated or altered here.
- The 881 Mukhtara siege `wpy_7e5ebec3bf` remains at its old location near Ahvaz. The linked Basra article locates the rebel capital only broadly within the area of the revolt; exact site and siege chronology need further research. The Wilton record and other events remain unreviewed.
- The Wessex campaign note and separately indexed battles may still overlap; wording changes do not prove all readers deduplicate effects. Name and time scans cannot establish semantic uniqueness. City points are proxies.
- Previous batch unresolved: `wpy_65c3fd3d26` implies al-Ma'mun became caliph in 821, although his accession was 813. It was not changed in this batch.
