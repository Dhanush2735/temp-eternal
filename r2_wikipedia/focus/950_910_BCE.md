## Changes in the game data

Installed reviewed edits into the indexed history packs: **20 corrections, 22 moves and six additions**. Nine corrections overlap a focus year according to at least one loader; 11 concern nearby dated claims. Of the moves, 21 remove uncertain-date volcano entries from the disaster pack to non-disruptive history notes; one moves the disputed-date Bitter Lakes battle from the Wikidata pack to a history note. Original IDs and before-images are retained. The six additions are cautious reign or tradition context, not assertions of an action in the exact year. These changes reach the loaders; they do not constitute a full in-game review.

- **950:** Sparta's exact 951 foundation, Plymouth's 951 settlement and Menelik I's Ethiopia foundation are not verified facts. Their old 951–950 slots remain but are qualified: the Gazetteer excludes their end year, while NewsIndex converts Wikipedia-pack ends. Sparta article suggests c.900; Mount Batten has Bronze Age evidence; Menelik is a mythical dynastic ancestor in a much later Ethiopian epic. The map positions are regional references, not proof of founding sites. Added Psusennes II reign and Kurukshetra epic tradition notes; the latter is not evidence of a 950 battle.
- **940:** An unsupported claim of the first urban life in the Nile Delta and the beginning of Libyan rule in 950 is replaced with qualified context. Osorkon the Elder ruled earlier; Shoshenq I's biography places his reign 943–922. The 940 First Temple completion is a claimed date, not an established one; the linked article gives c.10th–8th century for completion and says the builder is debated. Added Shoshenq reign context.
- **930:** The 931 division record is qualified as biblical tradition and removes its implication that Samaria was already the capital; Samaria became the northern capital around 880. Added an Ashur-dan II reign note, not a 930 event. The 931, 928 and 925 partition claims may repeat one another and reflect different chronologies, not three proven partitions.
- **920:** Added Osorkon I reign context; 924/922 succession estimates differ. Shoshenq's Levant campaign has inscriptional support but the date, itinerary and biblical identification are debated; Jerusalem does not appear in the Karnak list. The 925 Bitter Lakes note retains the account but avoids an exact-year military effect; its Wikipedia article leaves several details uncited.
- **910:** Adad-nirari II succession is ordinarily placed around 911, not a new accession in 910. Added 910 reign context, not a new accession. Abijah and Nadab are treated as biblical traditions with competing chronological schemes; Nadab's accession and his father's death are no longer conflated.
- **Nearby years:** Qualified competing Egyptian, Zhou, Assyrian and Judahite reign/succession/death estimates. King Gong's biography disagrees sharply with the 940s/930s decade pages. Geographic region pins are not established personal positions.

## Venus transit correction

The [920s BC page](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=920s_BC&oldid=1353803946) lists a transit in 920 BCE. The [NASA transit catalog](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/transit/catalog/VenusCatalog.html) lists it in astronomical year −0920, equivalent to **921 BCE** (astronomical year zero is 1 BCE); it does not list a −0919 transit. NASA's early date uses the proleptic Julian calendar. No transit was added to 920 BCE. This is a calculated astronomical reconstruction, not evidence that people observed it.
