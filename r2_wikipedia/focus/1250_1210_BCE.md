# 1250–1210 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked:** 1250, 1240, 1230, 1220 and 1210 BCE. The years between them remain for later. None of these years is fully collected.

## Changes made to the game

The indexed game history JSON now has **16 corrected records, 17 uncertain disasters moved to history notes, and two new notes**. The corrections include two nearby-year records (1221 and 1213 BCE); the five listed years remain the focus. Existing IDs were kept.

- Corrected **San Lorenzo** to Veracruz, **Ugarit** to Ras Shamra in Syria and the **Battle of Nihriya** from Egypt to a broad, uncertain Upper Balikh/Şanlıurfa regional reference. Nihriya's exact location, date and Assyrian commander are disputed.
- Qualified records for the Lion Gate, Wu Ding's reign, oracle bones, chariots in China, Pantalica, the Papyrus of Ani, the Tawagalawa letter, Lusatian culture and the Igihalkid dynasty. Their reference years and map points do not prove the exact dates or personal locations.
- Marked the Argonauts' capture of Troy as **legend**. A 1240 BCE Philistine expansion claim conflicts with the linked Philistines article's approximately 1175 BCE settlement chronology; both the timing and origins are uncertain.
- Qualified a 1221 BCE Merneptah campaign claim that conflicts with his biography's 1213–1203 BCE reign, and clarified the Merneptah Stele's approximate 1208 BCE date in the nearby 1213 reference.
- Added cautious 1220 BCE Tudḫaliya IV and 1210 BCE Merneptah reign-context notes. These are not proof the rulers were at the reference points in those years.
- Moved **16 volcanic records and one earthquake record** from the active disaster pack to non-disruptive regional history notes. Smithsonian's Global Volcanism Program recognizes the volcanic eruptive periods, but dates are approximate; the Candlemas source attribution is also uncertain. The NOAA Jericho earthquake entry gives 1250 BCE and magnitude 6.5, but its date, location and consequences have not been independently verified. No active hazard records now overlap these five exact focus years; this does not cover other years or saved-world effects.
