# 2600 to 2560 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2600, 2590, 2580, 2570 and 2560 BCE**. These year titles lead to a shared Wikipedia century page, so I followed some of its linked articles too. The research is **not complete**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **five entries** and added **four**, in the game's indexed JSON files. For example:

- **Spain and Portugal:** separated Los Millares and Zambujal, which are different sites. Neither is presented as founded exactly in 2600 BCE.
- **Mesopotamia:** separated Uhub of Kish from the possible ruler En-hegal of Lagash. I also corrected entries for the Early Dynastic period, the Instructions of Shuruppak and Mesannepada of Ur. Their dates are approximate or disputed.
- **South Asia and Crete:** added an early Mature Harappan context and corrected an early Minoan period entry. Historical dating systems differ.

I also checked the Great Pyramid. The game already had an entry for it, so I kept that rather than adding a duplicate. A period note can appear in multiple years; it does **not** mean a separate event happened in each one.

## People for later

I saved research leads for **Uhub, En-hegal, Mesannepada, Akalamdug, Khufu and Hemiunu**. They are **not active VIP NPCs**; uncertain dates and details must not be invented.
