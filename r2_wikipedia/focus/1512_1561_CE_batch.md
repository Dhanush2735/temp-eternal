# 1512–1561 CE — preliminary R2 pass

Reviewed 2026-10-31. **This is a partial pass, not completion of these years.** `year_titles_1512_1561_CE.json` resolves 50 annual titles; `year_wikitext_1512_1561_CE.json.xz` preserves the raw revisions. Transclusions are not expanded. `indexed_start_scan_1512_1561_CE.txt.xz` snapshots 3,016 indexed records whose start falls in this interval, not older records that overlap it. Most annual links, references and indexed records are unexamined.

## Changes applied to indexed gameplay data

- Sixteen guarded before/after updates across five indexed packs, no additions. Manifest: `../reviews/1512_1561_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`; canonical JSON SHA-256 `d6321e5e60df35d736a6f4dd527aa460088bd63605b3c46652c951d638b1b83f`.
- Five uncertain-date volcano rows moved from the automatic hazard pack to indexed non-hazard notes. Manifest: `../reviews/1512_1561_ce_hazards_2026-10.json.gz`; canonical JSON SHA-256 `fab3820d6c81b7c42a0ebf7ba6435be8561cc4b7e7b76e245d6a2dc93768d66b`. GVP dates Aniakchak 1540 ±100, Aluto 1550 ±50, Barú 1550 ±10, and lists Wells Gray-Clearwater and Witori as 1550 (?). A confirmed eruption is not proof of a precise year. Moving a row does not assert an eruption never occurred.
- Three 1550 battle entries were misdated: Chaldiran belongs to 1514 and Raichur to 1520. Their duplicate effects are now notes at the corrected years; world/Wikidata battle effects remain, but their gameplay radii are unvalidated. A 1550 entry for the Magellan expedition reaching Maluku now uses 1521 and a regional reference instead of Ambon.
- Moctezuma II's death and Cuitláhuac's succession are referenced to Tenochtitlan, not Chiapas. Sources disagree about the circumstances of Moctezuma's death, so no assassin is asserted. The Tenochtitlan smallpox record starts in 1520 rather than 1519, consistent with the cited article; its 150 km effect remains unvalidated.
- The Magellan expedition's departure marker now references Sanlúcar, not Maluku or Mexico. The voyage began in 1519 and ended under Elcano in 1522, after Magellan died at Mactan in 1521. The unsupported claim that de Soto mapped the Gulf in 1519 has been retained as a flagged note, not a political effect; his Florida expedition began in 1539. Tamão's exact island is disputed and must not be asserted as Macau. The early Macau settlement date is marked for review, with a city reference rather than a point in Spain.
- Ismail I's succession record no longer generates a siege in Rhodes; Tabriz is an approximate reference near his death. The Pavia record was relocated from Germany to Italy and kept as a note alongside the world battle. The Süleymaniye Mosque entry now distinguishes foundation in 1550 from inauguration in 1557. The 1556 Shaanxi earthquake label no longer treats 830,000 as an established exact direct death count: the cited article discusses broader population loss, migration and indirect mortality. Its remaining effect and radius are unvalidated.

## Unreviewed hazards and limits

The start-year scan contained 1,026 hazard rows: 803 plague, 115 volcano, 56 earthquake, 27 famine, 20 tsunami, three flood and two epidemic. **1,021 scanned hazard rows remain in the hazard pack**, including all 803 plague rows. Apart from the five moved volcanoes and the Shaanxi label, most have not been checked. Other packs also produce effects, and earlier-starting overlaps were not included. Do not infer that every remaining row is accurate or that similar quake and tsunami records are independent events.

Examples of underlying sources are [Battle of Chaldiran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chaldiran), [Battle of Raichur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Raichur), [Fall of Tenochtitlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Tenochtitlan), [Magellan expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magellan_expedition), [Jorge Álvares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_%C3%81lvares), [1556 Shaanxi earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1556_Shaanxi_earthquake) and the GVP links in the hazards manifest. Revision-pinned source links are stored with the actual before/after changes and research graph. Some guessed article names did not exist; title redirects and sources were checked rather than assumed.

## People and validation

`history/sources/wikipedia_1512_1561_ce_people_links.json` contains 35 research-only people, 18 qualified relationships and 31 event-role links across 12 indexed event IDs. It includes Indigenous leaders and people on opposing sides, not just principal European figures. Associations do not prove individual co-presence throughout events, and the graph is **not spawnable VIP data**.

Thirty batch/resolver/scope/pipeline tests passed, including guards, unique after-images, annual revisions, graph references and ordered in-memory reversal/reapplication of the preceding layer. Both new layers were also actually rolled back and reapplied on disk. Forty-seven Wikipedia title/revision pairs used by the batch context and graph matched the API; this checks revision identity, not the truth of every historical claim. Another seven annual-archive and corrected old-interval title tests passed. No live game, Java build, saved-world, natural VIP spawn or branching test was performed.

The inventory finds **4,550 recognized distinct annual title resolutions** (2,989 BCE and 1,561 CE), not collected histories. Eleven early BCE titles have evidence outside the recognized annual-title files. The legacy count of 3,168 partly worked years remains unreconciled. **Zero years have been established as fully collected.**

## Storage and reversibility

To make room, 31 older annual wikitext archives were consolidated losslessly into `annual_ce_12_1511_solid.tar.xz`; the mixed 39 BCE–11 CE archive is also included despite the filename. The map `annual_ce_solid_1512_2026-10.json` records original compressed and raw hashes. All 31 raw hashes and exact preset-9 recompressed xz hashes were verified; 30 archives with matching annual-title filenames also matched their title/revision sets. Python reader `../annual_archive.py` provides exact-path fallback and a checksum-guarded restore command; existing standalone files take precedence. Old reports may name standalone files that are now archive members.

During reader changes, three old tests were found to have checked the wrong annual interval: 312–361 and 362–411 checked 262–311, while 612–661 checked 562–611. They now check their own interval and the corrected title tests passed. Earlier reports' test counts should not be interpreted as proof that those three original tests checked the intended interval.

Installers `../apply_1512_1561_ce_context.py` and `../apply_1512_1561_ce_hazards.py` provide `stage`, `apply` and `rollback` with hash and before-image guards. Undo later dependent layers first, then hazards, then context. Writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs; saved-world effects are untested. Storage remains very tight and must be checked before further collection.
