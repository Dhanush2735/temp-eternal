## Game history changes

Nineteen existing indexed rows were updated with stable IDs, sourced qualifications and guarded before/after images. Two uncertain Etna entries were moved out of `events_hazards.json.gz` into the indexed regional note pack. These records can still appear as history or news; notes and IDs may interact differently with different readers and saved worlds. The engine's `wikipedia_region` confidence value selects regional handling; explicit Smithsonian evidence, not Wikipedia, is attached to the Etna rows.

- **Wrong locations corrected:** the 722 BCE Zheng–Duan conflict was pinned to Taipei. It now uses the modern Yanling County area in Henan; the historical account places the event north of the county, not at a precisely verified battlefield. The 707 BCE Xuge account was pinned to Gansu and now uses a Changge-area reference in Henan, again not an exact battlefield. The latter's claim that this was the first vassal revolt was removed as unestablished.
- **Syracuse and Naxos:** the 734 Syracuse row and its likely duplicate now distinguish the decade's date from the city article's 733 Thucydidean chronology and note pre-existing inhabitants and the uncertain founder tradition. Naxos's 735 reference is qualified against the linked 734/735 account and differing ancient founder traditions. Duplicate rows have not been deleted.
- **Egypt and Kush:** the 728 Memphis and 727 Kushite-invasion rows may describe the same Piye campaign. Both now point to a broad Memphis-area reference instead of using a Sudanese point for Egyptian invasion, and state that the linked account uses Piye's regnal year 20 and does not establish uniform lasting rule over Lower Egypt. Dates and the exact campaign route remain uncertain.
- **Assyria, Tyre and Babylon:** two 739 Hiram II rows now preserve the conflict with the Hiram biography's 737 succession estimate; two 724 Tyre-siege rows are notes rather than an exact four-year military trigger. The 727 claim of Babylonian independence conflicts with the account of Shalmaneser V's rule and Marduk-apla-iddina II's later 722 seizure. The 733 claim of a complete conquest of Israel is qualified against the later Samaria siege and fall. The 693 Babylon-destruction row is explicitly marked as a contradicted date claim: the linked Sennacherib and siege articles date the destruction to **689**. It was not silently moved to 689; an existing separate `q6646260` siege row uses −688 and needs review in the next batch.
- **Messenia and Judah:** 725 and 715 Messenian-war date claims and an unsourced 726 Mount Ithome Wikidata row are now qualified notes; the linked article describes competing chronologies. The 699 and 697 Manasseh rows are not treated as two proven successions: the linked biography attributes a 697/696 coregency and 687/686 sole reign to Thiele. The claim that Hephzibah was regent was not established by the linked accounts.
- **Etna:** Smithsonian GVP lists the 735 BCE entry as an *uncertain* eruption with unknown evidence and stop date; the 695 BCE entry is confirmed but dated **±2 years**, with uncertain flank location and unknown stop. Neither establishes the former exact-year 40-km disruption. Moving them is necessary because `Disasters.java` processes every row in the hazards pack even when kind/effect alone is changed. The original rows remain recoverable; a note does not assert that an eruption never happened.

The start-year scan found **130 indexed rows** in this interval, including two then-active hazard rows; it is not a distinct-event or completeness count. Many other claims and likely duplicates were not individually reviewed.
