# 2980 BCE — first collection

## Game history added

Added an approximate regional history note about **Djet**, an Egyptian First Dynasty ruler who succeeded Djer. Wikipedia places Djet’s reign around 2980 BCE, but the dates are uncertain. The game’s 2980 BCE slot is a reference, not a proven exact accession date. The map point is near his tomb at Abydos; it does not claim he was at that spot in 2980 BCE.

The 2990 BCE Djer entry remains separate: it concerns one proposed range for Djer’s death. The new entry is about Djet’s reign, not a second claim about Djer’s death.

## People collected for future VIP NPCs

- **Djet** — ruler and Djer’s successor; his reign length and exact dates are disputed.
- **Merneith** — a First Dynasty consort and regent; whether she ruled as pharaoh in her own right is debated.
- **Amka** — an official whose career is associated with Djer, Djet and Den.
- **Sekhemkasedj** — an official associated with Djet and Merneith.

Their source-linked profiles are saved in the project at `history/sources/wikipedia_2980_bce.json`. These are **VIP candidates, not active NPCs**: no birth dates, death dates or positions were invented. Nor does this pass implement historical NPC behaviour or player-triggered alternate timelines.
