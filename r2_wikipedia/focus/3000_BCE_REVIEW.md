# 3000 BCE — first collection for review

**Status: unverified source claims, not a completed year or game-ready events.** Wikipedia’s `3000 BC` link redirects to the 30th-century page. These records were selected from two already-collected, pinned Wikipedia revisions; other linked articles have not been exhaustively collected.

Most entries say “around 3000 BCE,” not exactly that year. Some source claims conflict with other chronologies, and some may describe the same event. No dates, coordinates, NPC spawns or effects have been added to the game from this batch.

## Important review issues

- **Stonehenge:** English Heritage dates the first earthwork to about 3000 BCE, but says its 56 posts may have been timber **or stone**, rather than definitely wooden. Its large stone settings were later, around 2500 BCE. [English Heritage](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/stonehenge/history-and-stories/building-stonehenge/).
- **Egyptian unification:** the millennium page says around 3000 BCE, while other sources place unification around 3100 BCE or earlier; the date and attribution need further review. [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/Narmer/).
- **Helladic chronology:** the century page says about 3000 BCE, but [another Wikipedia chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Helladic_chronology&oldid=1380683880) dates Early Helladic I to about 3200–2650 BCE.
- **Nubian A-Group:** two overview bullets may overlap. A [University of Chicago museum publication](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/oimp24.pdf) gives a different approximate endpoint, around 3100 BCE. The cause and date require review.
- **Sydney engravings:** two bullets may refer to the same claim. Their identity and date have not been independently established.
