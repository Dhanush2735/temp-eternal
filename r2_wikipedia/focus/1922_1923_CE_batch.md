## Indexed gameplay corrections

Eight existing records were updated, retaining their IDs:

- **1922:** corrected the Irish Civil War summary: fighting began before the Free State was formally established; Dublin is an opening-battle proxy, not the war's full geography. Moved the Michael Collins ambush record from Dublin to a Béal na Bláth village proxy, the Treaty of Rapallo from Berlin to a Rapallo town proxy, and the Bondelswarts uprising from an erroneous South Sudan coordinate to a Warmbad regional proxy in southern Namibia. The latter represents neither the whole uprising nor a precise bombing site.
- **1923:** moved two overlapping Ruhr occupation records from erroneous coordinates to an Essen regional proxy, without deleting either. Corrected a hunger-strike summary that attributed all five 1923 hunger-strike deaths to the autumn mass strike, although the source includes earlier separate strikes. Moved the Coolidge succession record to a Plymouth Notch proxy for his first oath, distinguishing it from Harding's death in San Francisco.

Proxy radii are gameplay approximations, not verified boundaries or precise sites. The Irish Civil War record was also changed from `treaty` to `political`; its political effect remains. Other effects were retained. Annual records do not establish exact in-game days.

The reversible manifest is `reviews/1922_1923_ce_context_2026-11.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `bd75a688a7e588fdc2a4f658d65c1c904c09b73bb6de711559dbe22e05bc55ff`; installer `apply_1922_1923_ce_context.py` preserves before/after states. All 26 indexed packs were hash-checked through apply, rollback and reapply; three packs changed. Writes are atomic per pack, not across packs. No live-game or saved-world test was performed.
