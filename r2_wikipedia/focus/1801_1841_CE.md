# 1801, 1811, 1821, 1831 and 1841 CE — partial collection

**Overall progress: 494 years partly worked; 0 years fully collected.** This is a five-year sample, not all the years between them. Many linked articles and references are still unread.

## Implemented in project history JSON

Updated **34 existing records** and added **one event** in three indexed game-data packs. IDs and stored year ranges of existing records were preserved. Examples:

- **1801:** Corrected Toussaint Louverture's title to governor-general for life, located Elgin's removals in Athens and the Treaty of Lunéville in Lunéville, and added Piazzi's observation of Ceres at Palermo.
- **1811:** Relocated the German Coast uprising to Louisiana, the Paraguayan revolt to an Asunción proxy, Thompson's notice to the Columbia–Snake confluence, Hidalgo's execution to Chihuahua and the Klågerup confrontation to Scania. Corrected locations of records for the battles of Anholt and Fuentes de Oñoro.
- **1821:** Moved the Congress of Laibach to Ljubljana, the Congress of Cúcuta to Villa del Rosario and the Central American independence act to Guatemala City. Corrected the Alexander Island sighting and Acatempan locations. Distinguished Gran Colombia's 1819 proclamation from its 1821 constitutional organization.
- **1831:** Corrected locations for Merthyr, Warsaw, Wallachia, the Baptist War and other records; qualified Faraday's induction experiment and removed disputed casualty totals.
- **1841:** Moved the Dhaka College event from Kolkata to Dhaka, corrected the unratified Convention of Chuenpi, the location of Harrison's death, and Thomas Cook's excursion origin.

Locations labeled *proxy* are approximate anchors, often for events spanning a region or route. Their radii are game representation choices, not measured historical boundaries. The coordinates for the Ceres observation refer to the observer's observatory, not the position of Ceres. The person and event articles are not independent corroboration when they reuse the same source material.

## Remaining issues and uncertainty

- This pass did **not** collect every event, person, source, or article linked from these years. The 1831 and 1841 year pages, some decade pages and several individual articles have citation warnings.
- The Ranjit Singh year page and biography disagree on the investiture day; the entry uses only 1801. The Congress of Laibach year page and its article disagree on a February end; the year-only record avoids settling that dispute. The Dhaka College article describes a July approval and a November establishment/foundation stone; the year page gives July as establishment. Harrison's cause of death differs between the year page and biography, so it is omitted. The 1821 year page contains a questionable calendar conversion; no conversion was added.
- Events may appear more than once: German Coast, Merthyr, Warsaw, Fuentes de Oñoro, Anholt and Baptist War have overlapping records. The Central American independence act also appears in a 1824 record (`wpy_5b7908a326`) that remains unchanged. Las Piedras (`wpw_211210db66` and `wpy_f73d8ce3cc`) and other battle overlaps remain. Names and IDs alone cannot prove semantic uniqueness, including for the Ceres addition. No broad deduplication was attempted.
- Known incorrect records **still unchanged** include the New Zealand separate-colony event (`wpr_a7035a94fe`) pinned in Britain, Graham Island (`wpy_8d21ec4c6e`) pinned too far east, and a First Barbary War record (`wpd_0a35b06c5f`) that also mentions the later Second Barbary War. Other incorrect locations and dates likely remain.
- An inclusive stored-range inventory found **214 hazard overlaps** across the sampled years. These were **not reviewed or changed**; an overlap alone does not prove activation. Different game readers interpret end boundaries differently.
