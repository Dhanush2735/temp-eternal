# 1462–1511 CE — preliminary R2 pass

Reviewed 2026-10-31. This is a **partial pass, not completion of these years**. `year_titles_1462_1511_CE.json` resolves all 50 annual titles; `year_wikitext_1462_1511_CE.json.xz` preserves their raw revisions. Transclusions are not expanded. `indexed_start_scan_1462_1511_CE.txt.xz` snapshots 2,403 indexed records starting in this interval, not older records still active during it. Most records and linked articles and references remain unexamined.

- Applied 32 guarded before/after updates in six indexed packs, with no additions. Manifest: `../reviews/1462_1511_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`; canonical JSON SHA-256 `28a79a956eebc93a38635d80c8637e4c0e3d27a2ad9bfa41dbf32332d385169e`.
- Moved seven uncertain-date volcanic hazard records to indexed non-hazard notes. Manifest: `../reviews/1462_1511_ce_hazards_2026-10.json.gz`; canonical JSON SHA-256 `7e9fb721f2eca52ac9a626c3590a2e638643780ecb456e91421a8ce3b94c1e60`. Their removal from automatic disaster effects does **not** deny that eruptions occurred. GVP dates Aniakchak 1470 ±20, Chyulu Hills 1470 ±200, Andahua-Orcopampa 1490 ±40, Khangar 1500 ±40, Shikotsu 1500 ±150 and Tongariro 1500 ±50; Deception Island is dated 1500 (?). The linked GVP profiles in the manifest document each estimate.
- Corrected Nancy from 1476 to 1477 and Garigliano from 1504 to 1503. Danes Moor and Edgcote are alternate names for one 1469 battle; the former is now a note while the Edgcote effect remains. The decade record for Bosworth was likewise changed to a note in favor of its world battle entry. Diu's misplaced Calicut point was corrected to Diu and its duplicate decade effect replaced by a note; the regional battle effect remains. Retained radii and effects are not validated simulations.
- Corrected Columbus's Palos departure, the approximate Bahamas landfall reference, Malindi, the Sanlúcar departure, and Cabral's approximate Brazil landfall area. The locations are proxies, not validated precise footprints. The Americas were already inhabited. Da Gama's 1497 rounding of the Cape is not represented as the completion of the sea route to India, which was reached in 1498. Treaty of Tordesillas records now distinguish asserted Castilian–Portuguese claims from control or Indigenous consent; signing is referenced to Tordesillas and Portuguese ratification to Setúbal. The location for Castilian ratification remains a signing-site reference, not a claim of its ratification venue.
- Corrected the wording of the establishment of Portuguese India and used Cochin as an initial-capital reference rather than a claim that Calicut was taken from Bijapur. The Kongo succession record now flags conflicting 1506/1509 chronology and no longer asserts that the battle established Catholicism as state religion. Sennar is a capital-area proxy for the conventionally dated founding of the Funj Sultanate.
- The 1507 Hispaniola smallpox assertion and the 1494–1496 yellow-fever diagnosis are now notes rather than deterministic disease effects: earlier illnesses and retrospective diagnoses are disputed. This is **not** a denial of early sickness or mortality; 1518–19 is the first incontrovertible smallpox epidemic according to the historical reassessment linked in the manifest. Nine NOAA death-order categories were removed from names that incorrectly represented them as exact death counts. The 1498 Meiō earthquake death toll is qualified as uncertain; its remaining hazard effect and radius are unvalidated.

## Unreviewed hazards and other limits

The scan contained 879 hazard starts: 723 plague, 67 volcano, 61 earthquake, 16 famine, 10 tsunami and two epidemic. After seven moves, **872 scanned hazards remain in the hazard pack**, including all 723 plague records. Some earthquake labels were corrected, but most remaining records have not been source-checked. In particular, several records for 1498 Japan and 1509 Marmara may represent related shocks or impacts and need careful source-by-source analysis before deduplication. The 1509 Constantinople death estimate varies considerably. Other packs also supply effects. Earlier-starting overlapping events were outside this scan.

NOAA tsunami records with no indexed geographic point were not treated as indexed rows. NOAA validity 1 means very doubtful as a tsunami; validity 2 is uncertain, not automatically false. Earthquake death-order categories are not precise death counts. The paper [Return to Hispaniola](https://read.dukeupress.edu/hahr/article/83/1/3/26855/Return-to-Hispaniola-Reassessing-a-Demographic) and [Sickness, Starvation, and Death in Early Hispaniola](https://nemosine.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/sickness-starvation-and-death.pdf) provide important disease-history cautions. Wikipedia and GVP/NOAA references for the individual changes are in the manifests.

## People and validation

`history/sources/wikipedia_1462_1511_ce_people_links.json` contains 36 research-only people, 18 qualified relationships and 37 event-role links across 17 indexed event IDs. It includes people on opposing sides, allies and predecessors, not just principal figures. These associations are research leads, not complete biographies or proof of co-presence. **They are not spawnable VIP NPCs.**

Thirty Python tests passed: 11 batch checks and 19 resolver/scope/pipeline checks. These include tamper guards, unique after-images, current and preceding-layer in-memory reverse/reapply, graph references and annual revision matching. The two current layers were also actually rolled back and reapplied on disk. Fifty-three Wikipedia title/revision pairs used by the context and people graph matched the revision API; this verifies revision/title identity, not every underlying assertion. Some attempted titles did not exist, including `First voyage of Christopher Columbus` and `1494 Hispaniola yellow fever epidemic`; canonical available articles and other sources were used instead. Redirects included Moquihuixtli → Moquihuix and René II, Duke of Lorraine → René II of Lorraine.

No live game, Java build, current saved-world behavior, natural VIP spawning or branching test was performed. Existing automatic effects, radius defaults, saved-world suppression and cross-pack dependencies require further review.

The inventory finds **4,500 recognized distinct annual title resolutions** (2,989 BCE and 1,511 CE), not collected histories; 11 early BCE titles have evidence outside the recognized annual inventory. The legacy figure of 3,168 partly worked years remains unreconciled. **Zero years have been established as fully collected.**

## Reversibility and storage

Installers `../apply_1462_1511_ce_context.py` and `../apply_1462_1511_ce_hazards.py` support `stage`, `apply`, and `rollback` with manifest hashes and before-image guards. Undo later dependent layers first, then this batch's hazards, then context. Writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs, and saved-world effects are untested.

To make room, 17 annual archives and several old manifests were losslessly compressed with readers adjusted and hashes recorded in `annual_archive_compression_1462_2026-10.json`, `../reviews/hazard_manifest_compression_1462_2026-10.json`, `../reviews/bce_context_compression_1462_2026-10.json`, and `../reviews/ce_context_compression_1462_2026-10.json`. The staged candidate archive uses the xz fallback documented in `../candidate_archive_1462_2026-10.json`; newly produced gzip takes precedence. Not all modified older BCE test suites were rerun. Historical reports may name original gzip paths. Snapshot capacity remains extremely tight; check and safely compact before another collection pass.
