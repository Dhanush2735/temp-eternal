# 1001–1041 CE: partial collection and project update

**Years worked:** 1001, 1011, 1021, 1031 and 1041 CE. These years were partly researched, not fully collected. English Wikipedia year pages, the beginnings of decade pages, selected linked articles and one archaeological research paper were consulted. Year and decade pages often repeat each other and are not independent confirmation.

## What changed

- Updated **36 existing records** and added **6 records** in four indexed game-data packs. The guarded manifest preserves before- and after-images and source URLs: `reviews/1001_1041_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `2209d56dc841805072c7c614ad93f14710cb83bafcf85780ba56b966fd04737c`). Two updated records are in adjacent legacy years 1017 and 1042.
- Added **38 research-only people leads** in `history/sources/wikipedia_1001_1041_ce.json`. They are not VIP profiles and do not spawn NPCs.
- Thirteen installer tests passed before and after installation. Four staged pack reversals and 42 unique installed after-images were verified. Writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs.

## Examples

- **1001:** Corrected the Butuan/Song mission references, the Vinland proxy from Ireland to Newfoundland, and Lloret de Mar to its Catalan coast. Leif's date and landing site are not established. The Lloret article gives a written reference in 966, not proof of a 1001 founding; the old 1001 slot remains qualified.
- **1011:** Marked Kyiv's Saint Sophia foundation date as contested and moved the Japanese succession reference to Kyoto. Added Byzantine authority's return to Bari and corrected Book of Optics records from Iran and Basra to a broad Cairo reference.
- **1021:** The [archaeological paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03972-8) dates worked wood at L'Anse aux Meadows to 1021; it does not identify Leif, date the settlement's founding or prove continuous occupation. Moved the Vaspurakan reference from Constantinople to the Lake Van region, and the vague Tripolitania claim from Chad to Libya. Qualified al-Hakim's disappearance and the timing of the Chola campaign.
- **1031:** Added the end of Córdoba's caliphate, the French and Goryeo successions, and the Abbasid succession in Baghdad. The 1031 capture of Edessa already had an entry; another year-page entry placed a similar capture in 1042 and has been qualified, not removed.
- **1041:** Added Michael V's Byzantine accession. Qualified the date of Airlangga's division of his realm and Bi Sheng's ceramic movable type. Existing entries for the battles of Olivento, Montemaggiore, Montepeloso and Ostrovo were clarified, not duplicated.

Selected sources: [Butuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butuan_(historical_polity)), [Leif Erikson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leif_Erikson), [L'Anse aux Meadows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Anse_aux_Meadows), [Kyiv's Saint Sophia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Sophia_Cathedral,_Kyiv), [Ibn al-Haytham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_al-Haytham), [Vaspurakan's last king](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senekerim-Hovhannes_Artsruni), [Córdoba's caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caliphate_of_C%C3%B3rdoba), [Hyeonjong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyeonjong_of_Goryeo), [Bi Sheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bi_Sheng), and [Michael V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_V_Kalaphates). Full per-record sources and reasoning are in the manifest.

## Important limits

- **39 hazard rows** have stored ranges touching at least one sampled year when endpoints are counted inclusively. They remain active and unreviewed; their damage behavior was not tested or changed. Event readers differ in how they treat stored end years.
- Potential duplicate Butuan, Book of Optics, Bi Sheng, Kyiv cathedral and Edessa entries remain active. The 1001 Lloret and 1042 Edessa entries still occupy questionable legacy year slots despite corrected text. A scan for names is not proof of semantic uniqueness.
- Many mapped positions are broad proxies, not verified event sites. Changes to locations, kinds and effects may change gameplay or interact with saved states; this was not tested in a world.
- All linked pages were not collected. No Java, Forge, in-game or saved-world test was performed; the integrity manifest was not regenerated and the mod jar was not rebuilt. The old jar does not include these changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
