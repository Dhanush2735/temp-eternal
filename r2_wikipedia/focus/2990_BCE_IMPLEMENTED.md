# 2990 BCE — collection and implementation

## What was added

The active game data now has one regional note about **Djer**, a ruler of ancient Egypt’s First Dynasty. The [30th-century BCE Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=30th_century_BC&oldid=1371895853) gives **about 2990–2980 BCE** as one estimate for his death. Other chronologies differ substantially, so the game entry says the date is debated. It does not state that he definitely died in 2990 BCE.

The map reference is the area around his burial place at **Umm el-Qa’ab, Abydos**. His place of death is not known from these sources; the marker does not claim otherwise.

The titles “2990 BC” and “2990s BC” redirect to the same century page; they are not separate year and decade articles. This pass concerns **2990 BCE**, not all years in the decade. The new related articles and their links are saved for further collection.

A source-linked Djer person record has also been saved for the future NPC work, but it is **not a spawnable NPC**. His profile includes aliases, reign-length alternatives, family claims with uncertainty, tomb evidence and relative regnal records. His birth date and birthplace are not filled in by guesswork. See `history/sources/wikipedia_2990_bce.json` in the project.
