## What changed in the game

The indexed game JSON now includes **10 corrections and 10 new historical notes**:

- **Mexico:** moved the Mokaya cacao reference away from Cambridge, England, to the Soconusco region. The residue-bearing vessel is dated broadly to 1900–1500 BCE; it does not prove cacao was domesticated in 1900. A separate corrected note describes the uncertain Barra pottery phase.
- **India:** changed the claim that Lothal was abandoned in 1900 to a cautious note about a broader transition. Later habitation and the function of the large basin remain matters for study.
- **Mesopotamia:** corrected an unsupported “last Sumerian dynasty” collapse and an Assyrian succession dated too late. Added eight approximate reign contexts for rulers of Isin, Larsa and Assur.
- **Egypt:** qualified Senusret II’s reign dates and Senusret III’s Nubian campaigns. Added a note about Sithathoriunet’s pectoral; her parentage and the exact year of the object are uncertain.
- **Greece and China:** qualified the Middle Helladic chronology and added a note explaining competing Erlitou dates rather than assuming an established state in 1900.
- **Tibet:** identified Tonpa Shenrab as a legendary Bön founder. The old birth and death claims are now explicitly uncertain, not political events; the 1900 death slot is only a warning about a contradictory claim, not evidence of a death then.

Dates and reign schemes are approximate. Longer notes give historical context, not proof that something occurred every year; the game’s news system may repeat them. Changed dates or locations may affect saved games.
