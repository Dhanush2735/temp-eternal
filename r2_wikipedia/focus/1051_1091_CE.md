# 1051–1091 CE: partial collection and project update

**Years partly worked:** 1051, 1061, 1071, 1081 and 1091 CE. English Wikipedia year pages, beginnings of the associated decade pages and selected linked articles were consulted. These years are **not fully collected**; year and decade pages can repeat one another and do not constitute independent confirmation.

## Installed changes

- Updated **33 existing records** and added **6** in six indexed game-data packs. Full source URLs, rationales and original/replacement records are in `reviews/1051_1091_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `76bbea73fd0d500cc63193a17dfd9387ae4808d38230e72531f70c5b48a226fe`. Existing IDs and stored year ranges were preserved.
- Added **40 research-only people leads** to `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1051_1091_ce.json`. These are not playable or spawnable VIP profiles.
- Thirteen installer tests passed both before and after applying. All six staged pack transformations were logically reversed against their original documents, and all 39 installed after-images were verified unique and exact across the index. Writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs.

## Examples by year

- **1051:** Added Godwin's exile and Drogo of Hauteville's assassination. Moved the Hungarian Vértes campaign from far eastern Hungary to a broad hills proxy, and the English heregeld from Ireland to a broad England proxy. Hilarion was the first non-Greek metropolitan **of Kyiv**, not of all Eastern Orthodoxy. William's supposed promise of the English succession is qualified as a contested account, while the two marriages previously joined in one record are separated in the wording.
- **1061:** Moved the Messina capture to the city; changed Floris I's misleading treaty entry to an incident about his death, with an approximate Nederhemert point. Added a regional Vengi reference for Rajaraja Narendra's reported death, with the conflicting 1061/1062 date noted. Corrected the Tartu/Pskov location description and qualified the pope-succession reference.
- **1071:** Clarified Manzikert, Cassel, Bari, Palermo, Ely and Romanos IV's deposition. Romanos was declared deposed while returning, not necessarily after reaching Constantinople. Bari's year entry overlaps a longer existing siege record; the Palermo siege continued into 1072.
- **1081:** Added Alexios I's accession without choosing between conflicting exact coronation dates. Qualified Chaka Bey's Smyrna date, the uncertain Mynydd Carn battlefield location, and Cardiff Castle's competing dates. The St Canute Cathedral article instead describes its predecessor under construction in 1095, after the king's death in 1086; the 1081 legacy slot remains but is explicitly disputed. A Toledo siege record still starts in 1081 although its linked article gives 1084–1085; the discrepancy is now visible.
- **1091:** Moved the Norman Malta expedition from Normandy to the Mdina area and described tribute instead of asserting immediate full annexation. Added the Almoravid takeover of Seville and the fall of Noto; the latter is a town-area proxy and overlaps the larger Sicily war. The London tornado's T8/F4 rating is a modern retrospective estimate, not an instrument measurement. Adjusted the Scottish raid's proxy to northern England and clarified the Syriac patriarch's titular see.

Selected sources: [Vértes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_V%C3%A9rtes), [Godwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godwin,_Earl_of_Wessex), [Drogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drogo_of_Hauteville), [Rajaraja Narendra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajaraja_Narendra), [Manzikert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manzikert), [Romanos IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanos_IV_Diogenes), [Cardiff Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_Castle), [St Canute's Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Canute%27s_Cathedral), [Malta expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_invasion_of_Malta), [al-Mu'tamid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Mu%27tamid_ibn_Abbad) and [London tornado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_tornado_of_1091). ## Important limits and gameplay effects

- **35 hazard rows** have stored ranges touching at least one sampled year if endpoints are counted inclusively. This is not proof that every row triggers in that year: readers use different boundary conventions. All 35 remain **active and unreviewed**; their effects were neither changed nor tested.
- Possible or intentional overlap remains active: the Sicily campaign with Messina/Noto and other campaign entries; Bari year and siege entries; alternative 1081/1091 Cardiff foundation entries; and the 1091 London tornado in the year pack (`wpy_4bfc6bfae3`) and nature pack (`q3082211`). A name search cannot prove semantic uniqueness. The nature record was not changed.
- The Former Nine Years’ War article dates fighting 1051–1062; the existing war record still stores end 1064. The Toledo siege, Chaka Bey, Cardiff and St Canute entries also retain questionable legacy year slots. Review/migration and reader-boundary checks are still needed; preserving the stored ranges does not make them correct.
- Broad points represent areas, not exact sites. Moving records can change where they are encountered. Floris I changed from `treaty/political` to `incident/note`; Cardiff's 1081 record changed from `political/political` to `cultural/note`; the heregeld record changed from `incident/note` to `cultural/note`. Such changes may affect gameplay and existing saves; no saved-world test was performed.
- Not all linked pages or historical details have been collected. No Java, Forge or in-game test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was done. The old jar does **not** include these changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
