# 1189–1140 BCE: partial R2 collection

**Progress: 2,178 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** This batch checks all 50 intervening year titles, not just the five ten-year endpoints. All 50 redirect to five decade articles. Selected linked articles were researched, but the linked graph has not been exhausted and redirects do not provide independent annual histories. Under the existing broad shared-period convention, 1180, 1170, 1160, 1150 and 1140 BCE had already been counted, so this batch adds **45** to the previous 2,133. Earlier years remain incomplete.

## Indexed history changes

Nine existing records were qualified or corrected, one note added, and one hazard moved to a historical note. Their IDs are retained except for the new note. These are indexed game source resources, not just research files:

- **Djahy campaign:** the old pinpoint military trigger became a regional note. Ramesses III's Medinet Habu account is royal evidence, not a neutral battle report; the exact battlefield and absolute year remain uncertain. The separately reviewed Nile Delta conflict is related but not automatically identical.
- **Hattusa and the Egyptian dynastic transition:** separated archaeological abandonment, burning, broader political change and later dynastic labels. Dates and causes remain uncertain; other reviewed notes may overlap.
- **Troy, Helen and Menelaus:** the 1184 BCE fall of Troy and 1154 death dates are traditional or mythological claims. They do not establish historical people or an exact date. Helen's Rhodes tradition was corrected from the inherited Sparta map point to a Rhodes reference, not proof of burial or presence.
- **Hekla 3:** replaced the exact-year disruption claim with a note. The 1159 BCE attribution and claimed 18-year climatic connection are debated; the linked article also discusses substantially different date estimates. An eruption, its year and its effects must not be conflated.
- **Shutruk-Nakhunte:** clarified that the broad range represents an approximate reign and campaign context, not continuous war. The dates, motives and attribution of particular campaigns require further research.
- **Ramesses III's assassination and trial:** added a contextual note linking the Judicial Papyrus of Turin and mummy examination, with uncertainty about absolute chronology and the exact incident location. The papyrus is now in Turin; the Egyptian map point is a historical context, not its modern collection location.
- **Proposed Baltic tsunami:** moved `hz_f2f42a7980` out of the active disaster pack into a note. Mörner and coauthors proposed a tsunami and 1171 BCE varve date; broader prehistoric flooding evidence is sparse. The event interpretation, common origin of deposits, Kaali impact link, date and inherited circular footprint are not treated as established game damage. This is not a claim that flooding could not have happened.

These changes reduce unsupported exact-year triggers in the indexed files. They have not been verified in a live game or on existing saves.

## People and relationships

`history/sources/wikipedia_1189_1140_people_links.json` contains **43 people or figure references, 40 qualified relationships and 27 event roles across nine indexed event IDs**. It includes Ramesses III, Tiye, Pentawer, Ramesses IV, court officials and named judges; Shutruk-Nakhunte and other rulers; and separately labelled mythological figures. Judicial names can be pseudonyms and court records are not neutral biographies. Roles do not prove physical presence at a mapped location. The graph is research-only: no one was made a spawnable VIP, and it is not comprehensive or independently identity-certified.
