# 2589–2540 BCE — preliminary consecutive batch

## Coverage

All 50 English Wikipedia year titles in the interval resolve to [26th century BC, revision 1371895844](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=26th_century_BC&oldid=1371895844). These are title checks, not 50 independently researched annual histories. We followed selected links about Khufu, the Great Pyramid, Merer's diary and related officials, Mesilim, Ur and other early Mesopotamian people. Many links and regions remain unreviewed. The resolver output is `year_titles_2589_2540.json`.

## Indexed gameplay data

* Added `r2_khufu_great_pyramid_reign_context_c2589` as a regional cultural note at Giza. [Fourth Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Fourth_Dynasty_of_Egypt&oldid=1376337093) gives Khufu's reign approximately 2589–2566 BCE, whereas the [century overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=26th_century_BC&oldid=1371895844) gives approximately 2551–2526. The stored range reflects **one** convention for context, not construction every year, exact completion, or proof of personal presence. The 20 km radius is a game setting. A separate [Great Pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Great_Pyramid_of_Giza&oldid=1378503482) page offers broad dating. NewsIndex can include the stored end and repeat the note. A narrow name scan found no dedicated Great Pyramid event beforehand, but does not rule out semantic duplicates.
* The [Diary of Merer](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Diary_of_Merer&oldid=1378079947) records limestone transport from Tura to Giza during Khufu's reign; the use of this stone for pyramid cladding is an interpretation, not specified by the diary. The page refers to years 26 and 27 in different sections. The diary was found at Wadi al-Jarf, not at the destination; the note does not assign an exact transport year. Hemiunu as architect is a presumption, not a certainty.
* Enriched existing `r2_mesilim_kish_reference_c2550` with the linked Mesilim inscription evidence: [Lugalshaengur](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lugalshaengur&oldid=1369481139) is named on a mace, and [Nin-kisalsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Nin-kisalsi&oldid=1379930246) on a bowl. The Adab ruler's subordination is an interpretation, and neither inscription proves a specific-year meeting. The existing Kish pin is not their verified location or the inscriptions' provenance. Dates and identity with Mesannepada are disputed.
* Existing indexed reference notes already cover Uhub, En-hegal, Mesannepada, Puabi, the Standard of Ur, Khafre's pyramid and the Sphinx. They are not independently certified complete in this pass.

Each was matched by volcano and nominal date to the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program. GVP calls these eruptions confirmed but dates them approximately; its question marks are not numerical error bounds. They have been moved from the dedicated hazard pack into indexed regional cultural notes with stable IDs, existing dates/coordinates and inherited 40 km radii. On a fresh resource load the dedicated disaster reader will no longer receive these rows as deterministic dated hazards. This does not prove an eruption had no consequences; the radius is not an observed impact footprint. Other related rows and saved-world behavior remain untested. ## Connected people for later R3 research

`history/sources/wikipedia_2589_2540_people_links.json` contains 18 research references and 15 qualified relationships. These include Khufu, Merer, Ankhhaf, Hemiunu, Hetepheres I, Mesilim, Lugalshaengur, Nin-kisalsi, Mesannepada and A'annepada. Merer uses a local identifier: the Diary's Wikidata item is for a document, not a verified identifier for the person. Named-together, proposed, and later-inscription relationships are kept distinct. A'annepada's biography and century page give substantially different dates, so presence in this batch is not asserted. Entemena's retrospective account does not make him a contemporary of Mesilim. No birth/death dates were invented and **none of these research profiles is spawnable**.
