# 2450 to 2410 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2450, 2440, 2430, 2420 and 2410 BCE**. Their Wikipedia year and decade titles lead to the same century page. I also followed some linked articles. These years are **not fully collected**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **six entries** and added **three** in the game's indexed history files:

- **Mesopotamia:** made the Early Dynastic date and an account involving Kish and Hamazi more cautious. I also removed an unsupported claim that a conquest began in exactly 2410 BCE.
- **Egypt:** added a cautious reign reference for Nyuserre and a separate note for his pyramid at Abusir. Their dates are approximate.
- **Iraq:** added a note for the Stele of the Vultures, linked to Eannatum and conflict between Lagash and Umma. Sources give different approximate dates.
- **Syria and Peru:** corrected an older statue entry and two Huaca Prieta food and crop entries while following related articles. These three corrections use dates outside this batch.

Long period notes may appear in several years; that does **not** mean a separate event happened every year. Changed dates and locations have not been tested in existing saves.
