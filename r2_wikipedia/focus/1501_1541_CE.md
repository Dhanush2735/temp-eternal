# 1501, 1511, 1521, 1531 and 1541 CE — partial collection

**Progress: 464 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** These five ten-year steps are not complete histories. Intervening years, unread parts of year and decade pages, and many linked articles remain.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

- Updated **26 existing records** and added **one**, across five indexed packs. The addition covers Bahadur Shah of Gujarat's 1531 capture of Mandu during the conquest of Malwa. A name search found no same-period indexed Malwa or Mandu entry; that does not prove semantic uniqueness.
- Replaced wrong or misleading map points with qualified proxies for the 1501 Ascension Island account, the First Battle of Cannanore, a Corte-Real expedition, the Spanish–Taíno conflict in Borikén, the Lateran Council, the Philippine Mass, the Jiajing accession, the capture of Belgrade, the Schmalkaldic League, Min Bin's coronation, the Battle of Amba Sel, Buda, Santiago and Orellana's expedition. A proxy is an approximate reference point, not proof of an exact event site.
- Corrected the 1541 Buda account: Habsburg forces besieged the city before Ottoman intervention. A separate indexed siege record remains. Corrected a legacy 1550 Santiago entry to a note explaining its 1541 founding; its stored year was not moved.
- The Philippine Mass note identifies Limasawa as the official identification while acknowledging the location debate. Tamão's precise location is disputed. The Guadalupe entries identify the apparition as religious tradition, not an independently established supernatural fact. The Orellana notes distinguish the expedition's 1541 beginning from arrival at the Amazon mouth in 1542.
- Existing overlapping records were not deleted; some are now contextual notes. They can still produce repeated news or interact with other indexed records.
