# 2250 to 2210 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2250, 2240, 2230, 2220 and 2210 BCE**. These years are **not fully collected**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **four entries** and added **six** in the game’s indexed history files:

- **Shetland:** corrected the location of the Scord of Brouster farm site. Its age is approximate; it was not necessarily founded in 2220 BCE.
- **Alaska:** changed an exact-year volcano disaster into a non-disruptive note because the evidence gives a much wider possible date range.
- **Mesopotamia:** added references for Naram-Sin, Shar-Kali-Sharri and the victory stele. Akkad’s precise location and its population ranking are uncertain.
- **Honduras:** added a reference to dated maize remains from El Gigante, without claiming they are the earliest evidence of maize in Central America.
- **Egypt, Peru and South Asia:** clarified the dating of Pepi II’s reign and continued approximate Caral and Harappan period references.

Some earlier entries changed location or effect, which may affect saved games. **21 other volcano entries and two old Akkadian war entries remain unreviewed.** A separate older Edgecumbe hazard still has a disruptive effect and may refer to the same tephra. Period references may appear repeatedly in game news; that does not prove activity every year.

## People for later

I saved **15 research leads**, including Naram-Sin, Shar-Kali-Sharri, Ankhesenpepi II and Harkhuf. They are **not active VIP NPCs**; dates and relationships remain uncertain where the sources disagree.

Sources include [Scord of Brouster](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Scord_of_Brouster&oldid=1359046353), [Historic Environment Scotland](https://canmore.org.uk/site/405/scord-of-brouster), [Smithsonian’s Edgecumbe record](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=315040&vtab=Eruptions), [Naram-Sin](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Naram-Sin_of_Akkad&oldid=1381909462), the [Louvre’s stele record](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010123450) and [research on El Gigante maize](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1705052114).
