# R2 preliminary pass: 1662–1711 CE

Reviewed 2026-10-31. This is a **partial pass**, not full collection of any year or of every linked article.

## Coverage

- Resolved all 50 annual titles and archived their pinned raw wikitext; transclusions are not expanded. A pre-update scan found 2,641 indexed records starting in the interval, including 647 disaster-pack rows. This is an inventory, not a review of every record.
- The disaster inventory comprised 230 plague, 169 volcano, 153 earthquake, 61 tsunami, 23 famine, six epidemic and five flood records. Only selected records were investigated; the remainder are not validated.
- A selective research graph links **54 people, 23 relationships and 51 event roles** to 16 indexed event IDs. It is not a complete biography collection, proof of co-presence, or a source of spawnable NPCs.
- The title inventory now recognizes **4,700 distinct annual titles**: 2,989 BCE and 1,711 CE. Eleven early BCE entries remain outside that recognized inventory. Title resolutions are not completed histories. **Fully collected years established: 0.** An older count of 3,168 partly worked years has not been reconciled and must not be added to the title count.

## Indexed gameplay changes

Eighteen existing records were updated with stable IDs and source evidence:

- Corrected the date of Abdullah Qutb Shah's death and Abul Hasan's succession from 1674 to 1672. The 1909 gazetteer's conflicting original quotation remains preserved as provenance; the newer source identifies Abul Hasan as his son-in-law, not nephew.
- Corrected the Great Turkish War from 1667 to 1683–1699; Vienna is only a reference point for a much wider war. Corrected the start of the siege of Vienna from Istanbul coordinates to Vienna, without creating another active siege effect.
- Moved the Pueblo Revolt references from offshore to a Santa Fe regional reference. Santa Fe does not represent all affected communities. Corrected the 1683 Tungning reference to Taiwan, the Siamese revolution from France to a Siamese reference, and the Glorious Revolution from India to England.
- Distinguished Sambhaji's symbolic 1680 coronation at Panhala from his 1681 official coronation at Raigad; corrected the location of Shivaji's 1665 Treaty of Purandar and Rajaram's 1689 succession reference. Linked pages disagree about Rajaram's precise coronation date, so the revised record asserts only the year.
- Corrected the Finnish famine record from Edinburgh to a Finland reference and from a single year to 1695–1697. Helsinki is a regional reference, not the location of every famine death.
- Corrected the claim that Shahu and Yesubai were both released in 1707: the cited Sambhaji biography says Shahu was released and his mother Yesubai remained in Mughal custody until 1719. Sources disagree about who authorized Shahu's release. Satara is a later political reference, not an asserted release location. Corrected Shahu's 1708 coronation reference to Satara.
- Corrected the 1711–1715 duration and a regional reference for the Tuscarora War. The indexed dates use an exclusive end year. Many complex or potentially overlapping records remain as non-disruptive notes rather than additional deterministic effects; existing duplicates have not been comprehensively reconciled.

Twenty-one selected hazards were moved from the disaster pack to indexed regional notes: ten volcano entries whose dates or eruption status are uncertain, and eleven tsunami entries with specific NOAA-documented doubts or conflicting interpretations. This does not assert that underlying observations never occurred. NOAA's validity value alone was not used to prove a tsunami false. Examples include a Tseax eruption dated 1690 ± 150 years and a potentially duplicate 1700 Tseax entry; the 1700 casualty estimate was not independently established. The 1681 Arica report says no tsunami occurred, whereas other reports are disputed or may describe storms, seiches, subsidence or different events. **626** of the original interval's disaster records remain in the hazard pack and require further review.
