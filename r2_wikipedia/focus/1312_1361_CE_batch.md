# 1312–1361 CE — preliminary R2 pass

**Status: partial, not 50 completed histories.** Archived 50 distinct annual titles and their raw pinned revisions; transclusions were not expanded. Scanned 1,451 indexed records starting in the interval, including 427 hazards. Earlier-starting records that overlap the interval were excluded. Most records, year sections and linked articles remain unchecked.

## Changes in indexed game data

- Staged and applied **29 guarded updates** across seven indexed packs, preserving IDs and before-images. Qualified Tenochtitlan's traditional 1325 foundation, moved Vijayanagara references to its Hampi-area capital, distinguished the Bahmani rebellion from the kingdom's conventional 1347 founding, and moved Lan Xang's early-capital references from Vientiane to Luang Prabang. These points are references, not verified political footprints.
- Corrected references for Majapahit, the historically contested Bubat account, Nuremberg's phase of the Golden Bull, Tver, the Bruce campaign in Ireland, and Red Turban-era Nanjing, Bozhou and Wuchang. The Golden Bull also had a Metz phase; Bubat is known chiefly from later texts. A separate Bubat battle effect remains active and is unvalidated.
- Corrected the statement that Ibn Battuta visited Mansa Musa's court in 1352: he visited Mali under Sulayman. Mali's capital is disputed, and the revised West African point is only a regional reference. The exclusive end year was changed to 1354 to cover the 1352–1353 visit.
- Qualified Black Death chronology and spatial claims. The 1338–1339 Chüy Valley burials yielded *Yersinia pestis* DNA related to later pandemic lineages, but do not prove a single birthplace. The pandemic-wide `q42005` record was changed to an indexed note rather than a geographically misleading China-centered effect; the 1346–1348 Europe/Mamluk hazard, misclassified as famine and centered on Sinai with a 744 km radius, was likewise moved to a note. Local plague records remain active and largely unverified; this does **not** model the actual pandemic footprint.
- Two separately indexed Basel and Eastern Alps earthquake lockdowns remain active; their radii, effects and casualty estimates are unvalidated. One Sichuan earthquake name now correctly says deaths are unspecified: NOAA's death-order category is not an exact count.
- **20 hazards moved to indexed non-damaging notes:** 11 volcanological entries with uncertain dates, four NOAA waves rated possible rather than confirmed, a doubtful 1341 Crimea wave and disputed related earthquake, duplicate Basel and Eastern Alps earthquake hazard markers, and the broad misclassified plague marker. GVP's confirmed geological eruptions are not denied: for example, Kussharo is dated **1320 ±300 years**, Tseax River Cone **1330 ±75**, and Kīlauea **1340 ±40**. Krakatau's 1320 date is marked questionable. NOAA's Crimea tsunami record cites a possible confusion with the 1343 Marmara event; it does not establish a separate 1341 disaster.
- **407 of the 427 scanned hazards were not moved**, including **all 300 location-specific plague rows**. Most remain unreviewed, and their dates, identities, locations, radii and game effects must not be assumed correct. Other catalogued volcanoes, earthquakes and tsunami effects also remain active without a full assessment.
