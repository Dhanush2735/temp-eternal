## Limits and tests

The five year pages and selected linked articles were read; their Wikipedia revisions were not pinned and the link chain was not exhaustively followed. Other possible semantic duplicates and active events remain unreviewed. In particular, `wpr_85232e4f89` still describes Zenobia's Asia Minor campaign but is incorrectly centered in Greece; it remains a note and needs a supported regional correction. The indexed hazard pack was not modified; other 250–290 decade-bucket hazards remain active and unreviewed. World/Wikidata pack end-year interpretation differs between NewsIndex and Gazetteer, so range notes can appear an extra news year. Existing saves and other event loaders may react differently to changes; no Java/Forge, Minecraft or saved-world testing was performed and no jar rebuilt. Old jars do not contain this batch.

Twelve Python tests passed before and after installation; five staged packs passed logical rollback comparison; all 21 installed IDs were unique and matched their after-images across the full index.
