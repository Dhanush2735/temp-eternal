# 951–991 CE: partial collection and project update

**Years worked:** 951, 961, 971, 981 and 991 CE. Each was partly researched, not fully collected. English Wikipedia year pages, decade-page beginnings and selected linked articles were consulted. The decade pages often repeat year-page material and are not independent corroboration.

## What changed

- Updated **28 existing records** and added **4** in five indexed game-data packs. The guarded manifest preserves the before- and after-images and source URLs: `reviews/951_991_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `afdc3eeeed63867020b353b8c2be9192c1cc3e1e19aab56fdbd0c719141f9611`).
- Added **38 research-only people leads** in `history/sources/wikipedia_951_991_ce.json`. These are not VIP profiles and do not spawn NPCs.
- Thirteen installer tests passed before and after installation. Five staged pack reversals and 32 unique installed after-images were verified. Installer writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across all packs.

## Examples

- **951:** Added Later Zhou's foundation under Guo Wei and the León succession. Corrected the Ma Chu conquest reference to its capital region.
- **961:** Corrected Tavistock Abbey's location and made its older 961 foundation attribution explicitly disputed: the abbey article prefers 974 and notes a 981 charter. Located Abd al-Rahman III's death in Córdoba. The Chandax siege record still ends at 961; readers differ on whether that stored endpoint includes 961.
- **971:** Added the Song conquest of Southern Han and Jawhar's defeat of the Qarmatian invasion of Egypt. Made the Chola succession and assassination chronology uncertain, and corrected the Ma-i reference to a broad Philippine region without claiming its debated location is settled.
- **981:** Corrected Goryeo succession to the Kaesong area and the Song–Đại Cồ Việt war reference from central China to a broad northern Vietnam area. Recorded the different 981–983 dates given for the Gommateshwara statue and the uncertainty in the chronology of Erik the Red's Greenland voyage.
- **991:** Described the Treaty of Rouen as a papally mediated Anglo-Norman agreement, rather than calling it the first Truce of God; the latter movement's linked article dates its first proclamation to 1027. Qualified the Dagome iudex and Battle of Maldon records.

Selected source articles: [Tavistock Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tavistock_Abbey), [Gommateshwara statue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gommateshwara_statue), [Erik the Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_the_Red), [Uttama Chola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttama_(Chola_dynasty)), [Ma-i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma-i), [Song–Đại Cồ Việt war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song%E2%80%93%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i_C%E1%BB%93_Vi%E1%BB%87t_war), [Treaty of Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Rouen_(991)), [Peace and Truce of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_and_Truce_of_God), [Dagome iudex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagome_iudex), and [Battle of Maldon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Maldon). Full per-record source links and reasons are in the manifest.

## Important limits

- Thirty-two hazard rows have stored ranges touching at least one sampled year, counted inclusively. They remain active and unreviewed. Their damage behavior was not tested or changed.
- The Song–Đại Cồ Việt war has a broader war record, a year-page record and a river-battle record that may overlap in gameplay; they remain active. The 961 Chandax reference, the 981/982/985 Erik records and other events may also overlap. No comprehensive deduplication was done.
- The 950 Brihadisvara Temple entry was corrected to warn that construction is dated 1003–1010, but **still occupies the wrong 950 slot**. Existing 1003 and 1010 temple entries remain; a guarded migration/deduplication is still needed. The 961 Tavistock and 981 Erik entries likewise retain legacy reference-year slots, now explicitly qualified rather than presented as certain event dates.
- Several geographic points are broad proxies rather than established exact event locations; the original Liao coup point remains unverified. Historical article disagreements are not resolved simply by choosing one source.
- All linked pages were not collected. No Java, Forge, in-game or saved-world test was performed; the integrity manifest was not regenerated and the mod jar was not rebuilt. The old jar therefore does not contain these changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
