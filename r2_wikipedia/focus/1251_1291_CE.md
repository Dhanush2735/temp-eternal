# Wikipedia history pass: 1251–1291 CE

**Five years partly worked:** 1251, 1261, 1271, 1281 and 1291. Many links and intervening years remain; these are not fully collected years.

## Added to the project's indexed game JSON

**38 existing records updated and five added across six indexed packs.** Examples:

- **1251:** improved records for the Shepherds' Crusade, Möngke's accession, a Mongol embassy, a Scottish royal marriage and Launggyet. The Tripitaka Koreana's location was moved from its later repository at Haeinsa to a broad Ganghwa-region reference.
- **1261:** improved the reconquest of Constantinople, the Treaty of Nymphaeum, Callann, Yang Hui's mathematics and Northampton's university. Added the proclamation of al-Mustansir II in Cairo.
- **1271:** corrected Kamyenyets tower's coordinates, improved the Yuan proclamation, Krak des Chevaliers and the papal election. Added the Peace of Pressburg and Nichiren's exile. Repositioned Lord Edward's campaign from France to a broad Levantine reference.
- **1281:** improved entries for Berat, Homs and the second invasion of Japan. Added a separate Berat relief entry so that the 1281 episode can appear where a reader excludes the old siege's end year. Repositioned the larger Japan invasions record from Xinjiang to a Kyushu campaign reference.
- **1291:** improved Acre and the Swiss charter; corrected the locations of Atlit, Norham Castle, Nanzen-ji and Kunming Lake. Added the Vivaldi brothers' departure from Genoa. Venice's glassmaking regulation is now a cultural note rather than a fire disaster entry.

Each changed record has source URLs and a reason. The review manifest includes before and after images. Existing event IDs and stored ranges were retained; coordinates may be city or regional proxies, not precise locations.

## Important limits

- **Wrong-year legacy slots remain active:** three 1250–1251 notes describe the 1274 and 1281 invasions of Japan and Marco Polo's later Sumatra visit. They are now explicitly labeled incorrect, but remain indexed in the old time slot. Urban IV's Sicilian offer is now a 1261 note that says Britannica dates the offer to 1263; this change removes its former political effect but does not move the original slot. A saved-world-aware migration needs separate work. Other readers may handle event effects differently.
- **Conflicting dates:** Hariphunchai's article says both 1281 and 1292 for its capture, so the old 1281 reference remains qualified. Launggyet has competing 1237/1251 chronicle dates. The Tripitaka Koreana article's 1237-plus-twelve-years chronology differs from the 1251 year page and KBS's 1236–1251 account. The Swiss charter is dated early August, not definitively August 1, and its article contains inconsistent statements on dating. Tower of Kamyenyets construction may span 1271–1289; 1271 is not a firm start. Margaret of England's marriage date differs by one day across article sections. The Yuan proclamation's exact date differs across entries. The Bahaa el-Din biography carries original-research and neutrality warnings; its vizier appointment needs stronger verification.
- **Overlaps:** the 1281 Berat relief addition overlaps the existing siege in readers that include its stored end. Japan campaign/battle rows also overlap. Name scans cannot rule out other duplicates. The war range 1274–1282 for the Japan invasions was kept, though actual invasions occurred in separate years, not continuously.
- **47 hazard rows** overlap one or more sampled years under an inclusive range scan. They were not reviewed or changed and may or may not trigger in any given reader. See `reviews/1251_1291_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`.
- **39 people leads** are saved in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1251_1291_ce.json`. These are research notes only: not complete profiles and not naturally spawning VIP NPCs.
- No Java/Forge, in-game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was done for this batch. The old jar does not include these new JSON changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
