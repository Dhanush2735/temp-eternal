# 2800 to 2760 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2800, 2790, 2780, 2770 and 2760 BCE**. All five Wikipedia year titles lead to the same century page; they are not five separate articles.

## Game history updated

- **Cyclades, Greece:** Added a note for a marble harp-player sculpture dated roughly **2800–2700 BCE**. Its original findspot is uncertain; the game point represents the wider islands.
- **Tagus region, Portugal:** Added a note about early Maritime Bell Beaker pottery dated to the **28th century BCE**. Its map point is a regional reference, not an exact dig site.
- **Egypt:** Corrected an old entry that stated Second Dynasty wars as fact. Conflict and its timing are disputed.
- **Eastern Europe:** Corrected an old entry about the end of Cucuteni–Trypillia culture: sources disagree about the date.

The Egypt and Eastern Europe corrections use nearby **2775** and **2750 BCE** reference slots. The two longer date ranges are approximate: they do **not** mean the same event happened every year. They may nevertheless appear in game history across multiple years.
