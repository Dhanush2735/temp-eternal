# 151–191 CE: partial research and indexed game data

**Focus years:** 151, 161, 171, 181 and 191 CE. These five years are partly worked, not fully collected. The intervening years were not comprehensively researched.

## Active history JSON updates

17 existing records in four indexed packs were updated, preserving IDs; no additions or pack moves. Complete reversible before/after images and evidence are in `r2_wikipedia/reviews/151_191_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `4c270c930d21798114055abde6bbd1a028322b1b296b64baa2cee23e3b504bcf`). Highlights:

- The 151 Han Yuanjia-era marker was moved from Gansu to a modern Luoyang regional reference, without claiming the exact ceremony site. The 151 earthquake claim remains a note pending verification; the later 177/178 Smyrna earthquake is not assumed to be the same event.
- Elegeia was moved from Iraq to a proposed site in northeastern Turkey, with the 161/162 date and site uncertainty explained. The Antonine Wall reference was moved from Rome to Scotland and its 160 and 181 claims qualified.
- The location of the Scillitan Christians' 180 execution was corrected from Scillium to a Carthage city marker. Marcus Aurelius's possible death sites, the start of his column, the statue's date and original site, Meditations' place of writing, the Pyramid of the Sun construction date and Porta Nigra were clarified.
- Taupō's older 180/181 date claims conflict with modern evidence for **232 ± 10 CE**. The 180, 181 and 233 entries are retained as non-disruptive references to the same eruption, not three separate eruptions; the 180 marker was moved from Poland to New Zealand. This does not decide the exact eruption year or establish that ancient red skies were caused by Taupō.
- The Battle of Jieqiao is qualified as winter 191 or spring 192. The Marcomannic Wars record now describes 166–180 rather than treating the whole conflict as a single year at one battlefield. Both are non-military notes where the existing one-point game model would overstate duration or timing.

## People for later VIP research

## Sources and limitations

Read the five focus-year pages and selected linked articles, including [Elegeia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elegeia), [Hatepe eruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatepe_eruption), [Jieqiao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Jieqiao), [Scillitan Martyrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scillitan_Martyrs), [Meditations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meditations), [Emperor Huan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Huan_of_Han), and [Marcomannic Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcomannic_Wars). The [2023 Taupō ice-core study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-42602-3) supports the newer estimate. Wikipedia pages and sources can disagree and revisions were not independently pinned; this is not full recursive coverage.

The focused indexed scan also found numerous other 150, 160, 170, 180 and 190 hazard records, including eruption rows with decade-bucket start years. **These were not verified or altered in this batch and remain active**; their exact dates, duplicates and damage effects need later research. The GVP Taupō 260 eruption is a separate record and was not changed. The 190/191 Dong Zhuo campaign and overlapping Chinese battles remain for deeper reconciliation. World/Wikidata packs have different Gazetteer and NewsIndex end-year conventions; certain notes may display for one extra year. Writes are atomic per pack, not across packs.
