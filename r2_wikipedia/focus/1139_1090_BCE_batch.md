## Indexed game history

Five existing entries were corrected or qualified and two context notes were added to indexed game resource packs:

- **Pyongyang:** the 1122 BCE founding claim is marked as tradition rather than an established founding act. The separate Kija Chosŏn migration and foundation tradition is disputed; modern Pyongyang coordinates are a reference point, not proof of the ancient city's extent.
- **Ramesses X and XI:** both inherited 1107 BCE entries now describe the transition with uncertain date and parentage. The duplicate entries remain: they were not silently deleted because identity and saved-world effects need a separate deduplication review. KV18 was intended for Ramesses X but is not proof of burial.
- **Thymoetes and Melanthus:** changed the inherited military-presence trigger to a cultural note. The Athenian battle and succession belong to mythology, and accounts differ about Melanthus and his father Andropompus. No exact historical battle is established.
- **MUL.APIN:** the entry no longer presents it as an Assyrian creation precisely in 1109 BCE. The Babylonian compendium is commonly placed around 1000 BCE, with earlier underlying material and an earliest surviving copy dated 686 BCE. Those are different kinds of dates.
- **Year of the Hyenas:** added a contextual note for the decade article's 1090 claim. The sources consulted do not establish the exact year, the proposed Nile-flow cause, its extent, or a causal chain of state collapse. No disaster was added.
- **Babylonian succession:** added a note connecting Enlil-nādin-apli, his father Nebuchadnezzar I and uncle Marduk-nādin-aḫḫē. The revolt comes from a reconstructed chronicle. Scholarly chronologies differ; 1096 BCE and Babylon are reference slots, not a verified battle date or location.

The one-year slots and points are inherited or practical references rather than proof of exact ancient dates and places. A scan of the indexed hazard pack found no rows whose stored intervals overlap this range; that does not establish that no disasters occurred or that other unreviewed records are correct. The indexed changes have not been checked in a running game.
