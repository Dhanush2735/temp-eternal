# 962–1011 CE — preliminary research and implementation

**Status:** 50 consecutive annual titles were resolved and their raw revisions archived. This is a preliminary pass, not complete collection of those years or linked articles. Raw wikitext may contain unexpanded transclusions. The indexed scan found 564 records starting in this interval, including 59 hazards; it excludes earlier-starting overlaps. Most records, links and hazards remain unreviewed.

## Indexed history changes

24 existing records were updated in seven indexed packs; 12 selected hazards were moved out of the damaging hazards pack into indexed research notes. Stable IDs were retained and no event rows were added.

- The [Siege of Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Antioch_(968%E2%80%93969)) ended in 969. Its exclusive gazetteer end was extended to 970; different readers use different end conventions. The [Fatimid conquest of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatimid_conquest_of_Egypt) is now dated 969, with Fustat as a partial reference instead of a point in Palestine. A broad campaign circle became a note.
- [Al-Azhar Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_Mosque) began in 970 and was dedicated in 972, rather than being completed in 970. Cairo's Fatimid residence/seat is anchored to 973, not 972. The House of Knowledge's misplaced point was moved to Cairo as a research note.
- The [Chanyuan Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanyuan_Treaty) is represented by one active entry with a Chan Prefecture-area proxy; two repeated descriptions became notes. The point is not proof of the treaty's exact signing location.
- [Lý Thái Tổ's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%BD_Th%C3%A1i_T%E1%BB%95) 1009 accession was moved from Cambodia to a Hoa Lư capital proxy; a duplicate became a note. The linked biography and dynasty page give different days, so neither is imposed. The 1010–1011 [Goryeo–Liao conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_conflict_in_the_Goryeo%E2%80%93Khitan_War) was separated from the preceding coup and expressed as multi-site context.
- [Greenland Norse settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norse_settlements_in_Greenland) have approximate and differing dates in the sources; the Eastern Settlement is a partial reference, not the location of the Western Settlement. Rajaraja I's succession uses a Thanjavur capital proxy rather than a point in central India. The [Brihadisvara Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brihadisvara_Temple) construction and completion rows became cultural context, not political occupation.
- Two NOAA earthquake names no longer claim exactly three deaths: the source provides a **death-count category**, not the number three. Their magnitudes and effects remain estimates requiring further review.

## Selected hazards

Ten geologic entries were moved to non-damaging notes because their exact years are not established by the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program: Fuego, Taranaki, three separately listed Haleakalā events, Axial Seamount, Kostakan, Harrat Lunayyir, Mono-Inyo Craters and Medicine Lake. Their uncertainty and source links are in the hazard manifest; overlapping Haleakalā dates are not automatically merged. This does not mean these eruptions did not happen.

Two 1008 records were also moved: [NOAA earthquake 318](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/318) combines geographically separate Dinawar and Siraf reports and flags location, magnitude and effects; [NOAA tsunami 3562](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/tsunamis/events/3562) gives validity 2 and describes competing storm/sea-wave explanations and no established coastal inundation. They are preserved as uncertain reports, not asserted as a single exact local disaster.

## People and relationships

`history/sources/wikipedia_962_1011_ce_people_links.json` contains **39 research leads, 25 relationships and 44 event-role associations across 17 indexed event IDs**. They include related and opposing figures in Egypt, Korea, Vietnam, the Indian subcontinent, Greenland, Bulgaria and elsewhere. Relationships and roles span different phases and do not imply physical co-presence. The graph is research-only: it does **not** create spawnable VIPs or implement natural spawning, historical routines or alternate timelines.
