## Six targeted gameplay corrections

All six existing records had misplaced coordinates. Stable IDs, annual start/end fields, kinds and note effects are unchanged; no event was added or removed. Three formerly 800 km note radii were reduced to 20 km: these radii are gameplay visibility approximations, not sourced geographical boundaries.

- **1961 Checkpoint Charlie standoff** (`wpy_028f5bfc6f`): moved from Siberia to Berlin's Checkpoint Charlie (52.50750, 13.39028). The checkpoint is a site proxy, not individual tank positions. [Checkpoint Charlie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checkpoint_Charlie) has a citation warning.
- **1962 prisoner exchange** (`wpr_c41c84ae7d`): moved from a Soviet regional placeholder to Glienicke Bridge (52.413431, 13.090114), where Francis Gary Powers and Rudolf Abel were exchanged. The separately released Frederic Pryor was at Checkpoint Charlie, not on the bridge; the graph links him to the wider negotiations without claiming he was at the bridge. [Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glienicke_Bridge), [Pryor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederic_Pryor).
- **1963 Vostok 6 launch** (`wpr_7d49a97c1d`): moved to Baikonur launch pad 1/5 (45.92028, 63.34222), a launch-site proxy, not the spacecraft's orbital path. [Vostok 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vostok_6), [Gagarin's Start](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gagarin%27s_Start) (launch-site article lacks sufficient inline citations).
- **1963 Tereshkova's return** (`wpr_b7d9bb594e`): moved to the Vostok 6 article's landing-site coordinate (53.209375, 80.80395), a proxy not asserted as her exact parachute landing location. Launch and landing are separate phases of the same flight; year-only gameplay cannot express their timing precisely.
- **1964 Che Guevara's UN address** (`wpr_b5a02c2133`): moved from Melbourne to UN headquarters, New York (40.74944, −73.96806), a proxy for the speech, not the firing or impact position. Wording notes that a rocket aimed at the UN exploded in the East River. The [Che Guevara article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Che_Guevara) and a [contemporary New York Times report](https://www.nytimes.com/1964/12/13/archives/no-clue-is-found-to-un-attackers-but-anticastro-exiles-are-believed.html) differ about launch circumstances; no attacker attribution is asserted here. [UN headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headquarters_of_the_United_Nations).
- **1965 Operation Piranha** (`wpy_2074cb5a23`): moved from Virginia to the operation article's Batangan Peninsula coordinate (15.219, 108.913). This is a site proxy for a multi-place operation, not its perimeter; the [operation article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Piranha) relies on one source. [Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangan_Peninsula).

A subject-name search over all 26 indexed packs is not a complete semantic duplicate audit. No live-game or saved-world test was performed.

Links do not establish full biographies, presence at every location, a spawn schedule or alternate-timeline behavior.

**Deferred:** The 1963 Vajont disaster has multiple existing entries, including two geographically overlapping disruption records (`wpy_786af8df79`, `q3709251`) and other note records. Moving/removing them without testing possible stacking risks gameplay. Two 1965 Da Nang rows remain in Virginia: one has `military_presence`, another `political` effect, and exact incident attribution and stacking need further review. The 1965 Operation Piranha note corrected above remains a geographic proxy with a source-quality warning. Potential duplicate storms and hazards have not been adjudicated.

## Storage
