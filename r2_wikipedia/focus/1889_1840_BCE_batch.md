## Changes to indexed game JSON

Three existing cultural notes were enriched without changing their IDs or date ranges:

- Sumu-la-El of Babylon: his daughter Šallurtum and her marriage to Sîn-kāšid of Uruk, plus [CDLI's list of year names](https://cdli-gh.github.io/year-names/HTML/T12K2.htm). The marriage date and exact calendar dates are not established. He is not Sumuel of Larsa.
- Sumuel of Larsa: his daughter Enshakiag-Nanna and her role as en-priestess at Ur, based on the [Sumuel article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumuel). The note does not assign an exact appointment year.
- Enlil-bani of Isin: royal claims about taxes and building, and a later medical colophon naming Enlil-muballiṭ. The inscription's claims are not independently verified actions; the colophon does not prove an exact meeting or date of the original text. His substitute-king story remains qualified as legend.

One new cultural context note records **Sîn-kāšid of Uruk**, his family and palace inscription. The [Walters Art Museum](https://art.thewalters.org/object/48.1804/) describes the inscribed cone and uses a date range of 1865–1833 BCE. Wikipedia gives that approximate range in its infobox but says the reign length is uncertain and labels him 18th century in its text. The date range is a reference, not an established exact reign or lifetime; the Uruk point does not establish annual presence. No matching event name was found in the indexed packs, though that does not prove semantic uniqueness.

Six uncertain-date volcanic events were moved from the destructive hazard pack to indexed non-disruptive notes. Five were individually matched to GVP profiles: Michoacán-Guanajuato (1880 BCE ±150), Campi Flegrei (1870 ±50), Redoubt (1870 ±150), Guagua Pichincha (1860 ±100), and Taapaca (1860 ±100). The sixth, St. Helens, matches [GVP's 1860 BCE (?) Yn tephra event](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=321050) and the directly checked [NOAA event 5233](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/volcanoes/5233). NOAA list and detail responses disagree on its `publish` flag; the reason remains unresolved. NOAA and GVP are not assumed independent. A question mark does not imply a numerical uncertainty interval; moving these rows does not deny eruptions or possible damage. The Michoacán point is a volcanic-field reference, not a verified Cerro el Jabalí vent position.

Nominal dates, inherited one-year slots, map points and radii are not exact historical dates or measured damage footprints. Indexed readers differ in boundary behavior; changes may affect saved games.
