# 500–460 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked on:** 500, 490, 480, 470 and 460 BCE. This is a partial pass, not a complete collection of those years or every linked article.

## Changes made to the game

Updated the indexed game history JSON: **27 records corrected and 17 moved; no new event added**. Some corrections concern nearby years or records that overlap the focus years.

- Corrected the game dates of the **Battle of Marathon** to 490 BCE and **Thermopylae** to 480 BCE. Both were stored one year late under the game's BCE convention. Their existing IDs and military effects were retained. The 499 BCE siege of Naxos was likewise corrected from 498; the 490 BCE Persian attack on Naxos was a separate event. The decade overview gives approximately 501 for the earlier siege, so its chronology remains qualified.
- Corrected the 480 BCE Persian capture of Athens from a point in Iraq to Athens, retaining its military effect. Corrected the naval Battle of Artemisium from Iraq to Euboea; its separate active battle record remains. The linked article's descriptions of the exact day and outcome differ.
- Corrected two references to Xerxes' departure from Iraq to a regional reference at Sardis, preserving one political effect and changing the duplicate to a note. Corrected the Egyptian revolt's reference from Athens to the Nile Delta, retaining one political effect and changing the duplicate to a note.
- Corrected the Qin emperor's mausoleum from a 501 BCE claim to an approximate **246 BCE construction-start reference**. Corrected Shoshenq I's Levant campaign from 501 BCE to an approximate **925 BCE** reference. These notes may overlap other records.
- Changed questionable exact political claims about Nok, Vaishali, Aramaic, the Nordic archaeological period boundary, Bhattiprolu and Udayin into qualified historical context. Udayin's linked biography gives substantially different alternative reign chronologies. The Bhattiprolu article itself has a warning about possible LLM-generated text and does not establish a 500 BCE kingdom.
- Corrected the location of the Painted Stoa to Athens, the Apollo of Veii's attribution to Vulca to a possibility, Abdera to Thrace, and Hippocrates' birthplace reference to the island of Kos. Qualified Hanno's route, the disputed age of the Capitoline Wolf, the Yayoi and Chinese period boundaries, and Pausanias' death date.
- Moved **14 volcano records** from the disaster pack to historical notes because their dates are approximate or uncertain in the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program. Confirmed eruption evidence is not the same as a precisely established year or proof of uniform damage. Three military records were moved to the regional history pack with their effects retained and their dates corrected.

Reference slots for approximate periods do **not** establish exact years. Location references may represent a broad region rather than a proven specific site. Moving records or changing dates may affect saved worlds.
