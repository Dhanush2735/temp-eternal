# History collection: 1–41 CE

## Progress

Partly examined **1, 11, 21, 31 and 41 CE**, their English Wikipedia decade pages and selected linked articles. This is a limited pass, not a complete survey of these years or their linked articles. **Total: 314 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** Intervening years still need work.

## Changes to indexed game JSON

Five existing records were changed, keeping their IDs. These affect project resources; no new mod jar was built or tested in Minecraft.

- **21 CE:** corrected the date and description of the Florus–Sacrovir revolt. Florus was associated with the Treveri, Sacrovir with the Aedui. The existing location is a regional marker, not a confirmed battle position; its political effect remains.
- **c. 21–22 CE:** corrected Daeso's 20 CE battle entry to a qualified historical note. The 20s page places his death in 21, while his linked biography contains both 21 and 22. The prior location does not establish the battlefield; the military-presence effect was removed rather than presenting an invented site as certain.
- **29 CE:** corrected Agrippina the Elder's exile to Pandataria; her son Nero was exiled separately to Pontia. Removed an unsupported statement about all her sons.
- **26 CE:** corrected Tiberius's withdrawal to Capri from the decade-start date 20; Sejanus's growing influence is qualified.
- **43 CE:** corrected the Trưng sisters' defeat from 40. The linked rebellion article describes accounts of execution as well as Vietnamese traditions of suicide; neither is presented as certain. The location is a regional marker.

Eight people leads were saved as research only in `history/sources/wikipedia_1_41_ce.json`, not as spawnable VIPs. They include the Trưng sisters, Daeso, Sacrovir and Sejanus; uncertain dates and conflicting traditions remain marked. No VIP spawning or historical behavior was implemented.

## Important remaining problems

Several existing entries appear to have been assigned the **start of a decade rather than the event's year**. Other examples were found but not fully researched or fixed. For example, a Yellow River flood listed for 11 CE is recorded in the game at 10 CE, with a modern river-mouth coordinate that does not prove the flood location. Other Trưng rebellion entries overlap and some place the Han intervention at the decade start; their treatment needs separate review. A modern geographic coordinate should not be treated as proof of an ancient event location. The problems found in the Uruk test—duplicate UUID warnings, no natural VIP spawning and three data-check failures—were not fixed by this research batch.
