# 1212–1261 CE — preliminary R2 pass

**Status: partial, not 50 completed histories.** Archived 50 distinct annual titles and their raw pinned revisions; transclusions were not expanded. Scanned 892 indexed records starting in this interval, including 79 hazards. Records starting earlier but overlapping the interval were excluded. Most records and linked articles remain unchecked.

## Changes in indexed game data

- Staged and applied **20 guarded updates** across six indexed packs, preserving IDs and before-images. The 1250 Magna Carta duplicate is corrected to 1215 and made a note; the misdated Genter duplicate is corrected from 1233 to the uncertain 1221–1222 range and made a note. The Mongol invasion of Rus' is represented as 1237–1241 campaign context, not a 1237 conquest in Mongolia. Corrected Fifth Crusade and first Vietnamese invasion reference locations. The Seventh Crusade already had an exclusive end of 1255, correctly including 1254; its Damietta point is only a partial campaign reference.
- Qualified Mapungubwe's approximate emergence, Mali's traditional dating and the Kouroukan Fouga oral tradition. The earlier Sukhothai coordinate was at Phitsanulok rather than the capital site. Kertanagara's 1254 effective power, 1268 succession and later Bali campaign are distinguished. Approximate dates and reference points do not establish uniform political footprints.
- **21 hazards were moved from the damaging hazard pack to indexed notes:** 18 eruptions whose dates are uncertain in the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program catalogue, one poorly attested 1231 Marmara wave, one misplaced 1212 Dutch flood, and one misplaced duplicate 1219 Dutch flood. The 1219 flood's separate retained record describes 36,000 as a listed but weakly supported estimate, not an established count. The Dutch Wikipedia list itself requests more citations. NOAA rates the 1231 wave's validity 2 and describes the source reports as too vague to identify its effects or epicentral region.
- Examples of GVP uncertainty include Osorno 1220 ±100 years, Akan 1250 ±300, Mammoth Mountain 1260 ±40 and Pelée 1260 ±20. Confirming an eruption does not confirm its exact year or a game damage footprint. Profile URLs and uncertainty methods are recorded per move in the hazard manifest.
- **58 of the 79 scanned hazards were not moved**, many unreviewed. The separate world-pack **1257 Samalas lockdown remains active**, as do the existing Kirina and Baghdad/Ain Jalut military effects. The new evidence notes do not validate these retained effects, radii, durations or severity. No new Samalas duplicate was added. NOAA lists 1241 and 1257 Japanese tsunamis; their interactions with corresponding earthquake records remain for later review.
