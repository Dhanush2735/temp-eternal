# 401–441 CE: partial research and game history updates

**Years partly worked:** 401, 411, 421, 431 and 441 CE. These are not fully collected; intervening years and many linked articles remain. The five year pages, the five decade pages and selected linked articles were read. Decade pages largely transclude year pages, so are not independent verification. Sources were current pages, not revision-pinned.

## Indexed game JSON

23 existing records were updated and three new references added in five indexed packs. Existing IDs are retained. The guarded, reversible before/after manifest is `reviews/401_441_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `457a6ae37e3f6bcf93fcb82c47a63406d4fbff39059fc9d5713dacb13ca99fb4`. Use `apply_401_441_ce_context.py` to stage, apply or roll back. Writes are atomic per pack, not across all packs at once.

- **401:** Qualified an uncertain date for dismantling the Temple of Artemis. Kept two Kumārajīva accounts, marking one as a duplicate note. Moved the Gothic war reference from Greece to a **northern Italy regional proxy**, not a battle site; it remains a war record. Another war record, `q3625654`, overlaps the same conflict and may still duplicate effects. The previous Alpine point is only a regional reference.
- **411:** Marked Ingyō’s succession as traditional and uncertain, rather than an established date. Moved an Alan settlement reference from Porto to a broad Lusitania proxy. Added the Conference of Carthage as a note, using the city center as a reference.
- **421:** Moved the Constantius III reference to the western court at Ravenna, not a proven coronation site. Labeled Venice’s 421 foundation as legend, rather than a treaty. Moved the Roman–Sasanian invasion reference from central Iran to a broad Mesopotamian frontier proxy; the separately indexed war remains. Added former Jin emperor Gong’s death at Jiankang as a political event, using the modern city as a proxy; his biography needs more citations.
- **431:** Corrected both Hippo year-page records that were misplaced in Poland; changed them to notes while keeping the separate siege event `q7510087` active. Its end was extended from 431 to 432 because the Gazetteer treats end years as exclusive; **NewsIndex treats world-pack ends differently and can still report it in 432**. Qualified Aspar’s African campaign and the alleged Chlodio treaty. Kept Kʼukʼ Bahlam I’s 431 accession but noted conflicting calendar days. Added Palladius’s Irish mission with Rome as a dispatch reference, not a proven exact spot.
- **441:** Qualified conflicting Pulcheria and Chrysaphius accounts, moved the Armorican Bagaudae reference from Spain to a regional proxy in northwestern Gaul, corrected Seville’s location and Rechila’s accession date. Corrected the Hunnic Constantia story: Margus is a neighboring Danube-area proxy, not the exact fort, and the year requires confirmation. The year page’s link to coastal Constanţa is geographically inconsistent; [an external historical account](https://erenow.org/biographies/attila-the-hun-a-barbarian-king-and-the-fall-of-rome/6.php) describes Constantia across the Danube from Margus and varies its dating.

Coordinates marked as city or regional references are gameplay approximations, not evidence for the exact place or footprint. Moving records and changing effects can affect existing worlds; saved-world behavior was not tested.

## Ilopango and active hazards

The 431 Wikipedia event `wpy_237cdd7a35` remains an **active disruption** at Ilopango. Its wording now gives the 431 ± 2 estimate and uncertainty. The [dedicated eruption article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_Blanca_Joven_eruption) cites a 2020 estimate of 429–433 but also lists a 2019 study proposing 539/540. The NOAA record `hz_f0713bd92e`, dated 450 at nearly the same place, remains an independently loaded **active volcano hazard** and may describe the same eruption. It was not neutralized or deduplicated; `Disasters` loads its hazard pack separately, and changing the Wikipedia event does not turn it off. The 431 and 450 records can cause separate effects in the game. Further hazard-source and gameplay review is needed.
