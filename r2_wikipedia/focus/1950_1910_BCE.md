# 1950–1910 BCE: five history steps, partly worked

**Years worked on:** 1950, 1940, 1930, 1920 and 1910 BCE.  
**Overall: 119 focus years partly worked on; 0 fully collected.** The years between the ten-year steps still need work. Full game review comes later.

## What changed in the game

The indexed game JSON now includes **5 corrections and 20 new historical notes**:

- **China:** corrected the location of the Lajia site and turned two disruptive Jishi/Lajia records into historical notes. A proposed flood near 1920 BCE and its connection to the Xia legend are disputed. The two notes concern the archaeological site and the flood hypothesis; they are related, not proof of two separate disasters.
- **Egypt:** qualified the date and location of a Senusret I campaign; added reign notes for Senusret I and Amenemhat II, the pyramid at Lisht and the Meir faience hippopotamus. The hippopotamus has a broad museum date, not a known exact creation year.
- **Mesopotamia and Anatolia:** corrected an uncertain claim about the fall of Lipit-Ishtar; added reign contexts for Isin, Larsa and Assur, a note on Lipit-Ishtar’s legal collection and a Kültepe trading-district context.
- **Germany:** corrected the map position and date qualification for the Leubingen burial mound. Its occupant’s name and actual title are unknown.
- Added continuing contexts for the Indus region, Kerma, Caral, Únětice, Sintashta, Shimao, Taosi and Crete.

Dates and reign schemes are approximate. Longer notes are historical context, not evidence of an event every year; the game’s news system may repeat them. Changed dates or locations may affect saved games.
