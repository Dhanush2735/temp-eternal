# Wikipedia partial pass: 1851–1891 CE

**Sampled years:** 1851, 1861, 1871, 1881 and 1891. **Status:** partly researched, not fully collected. The intervening years and many linked articles remain to be researched.

## What changed

The active indexed history packs received **29 updates and one addition** across four packs. Each edited record has links and a reason in its `r2SourceEvidence`; the full before/after images are in `reviews/1851_1891_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`. All existing IDs were preserved. Most stored date ranges were preserved. Two related records were moved to their supported year:

- Sojourner Truth's Akron speech moved from 1850 to **1851**. Its later popular title and wording should not be assumed to be the exact contemporary text. [Speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain%27t_I_a_Woman%3F), [convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_Women%27s_Convention_at_Akron_in_1851).
- The Portland naming coin toss moved from 1851 to **1845** at an Oregon City proxy, as stated in the [linked coin article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Penny); the year page had placed it in 1851, the year Portland was incorporated. The exact house is disputed.

Other examples include the Brandtaucher sinking in Kiel; the 1851 Roorkee construction railway; Wilson's Creek in Missouri and Kỳ Hòa near Saigon; Henri Mouhot's death near Luang Prabang and the correction that Angkor was already known; the 1871 Treaty of Frankfurt, Liberia's coup, and the first rugby international in Edinburgh; Sitting Bull's surrender at Fort Buford in present-day North Dakota; Billy the Kid's escape from Lincoln, not Mesilla; the 1881 Treaty of Saint Petersburg; the New Orleans lynchings; the Jindandao incident; the SS Utopia sinking; and Pahang and Bafut conflicts in their correct regions. The Criminal Tribes Act entry no longer calls the 1871 designations 'denotified'. The Foucault demonstration is located at the historic Paris Observatory proxy, not the modern Meudon campus; the [heritage listing](https://web.astronomicalheritage.net/show-entity?identity=72&idsubentity=1) supplies the historic coordinates.

**Added:** the January 1871 proclamation of Wilhelm I as German Emperor at Versailles, distinct from the preliminary Treaty of Versailles and the constitution that took effect on January 1. A name scan found no matching event; semantic uniqueness cannot be guaranteed.

Coordinates marked as city, regional, town or harbour proxies are approximations, not claims of exact sites. The broad Bafut war record still spans the years 1891–1908 in its stored interval even though the cited article describes separate episodes in 1891 and 1901–1907. The Pahang war range was also retained; the game's readers do not all interpret end boundaries identically. Surrender records in year and decade packs remain potential duplicates.

## People and hazards

`history/sources/wikipedia_1851_1891_ce.json` contains **30 source-linked people leads** for future R3 work. They are research only, not naturally spawning VIP NPCs or completed biographies. Uncertain details were not converted to default dates or locations.

An inclusive stored-range scan found **307 hazard records** overlapping at least one sampled year. These were **not reviewed or changed**; overlap alone does not establish that any event actually activates in a given year. The 1851 year page calls the Yellow River sequence 1851–1855, while an existing regional record calls it '1855' and only stores 1851–1852; the [linked article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1851%E2%80%931855_Yellow_River_floods) describes the floods as a series culminating in a channel change in 1855. That entry and any hazard duplicates remain unresolved.

## Validation and limits

Four staged pack reversals matched their pre-change logical documents, and 30 installed after-images were verified unique across indexed events. Do not reapply to an already updated workspace.

This is not an exhaustive Wikipedia graph traversal, deduplication, or gameplay test. Other incorrect locations, dates, duplicate entries and source conflicts remain. No Forge/game or saved-world validation, integrity-manifest regeneration, or jar rebuild was done. Natural VIP spawning and the historical alternate-timeline behavior are not implemented; the existing `/ee check` issues remain unresolved.
