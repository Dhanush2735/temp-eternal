# 2400 to 2360 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2400, 2390, 2380, 2370 and 2360 BCE**. I followed some linked Wikipedia articles and other sources. These years are **not fully collected**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **seven entries** and added **three** to the game’s indexed history files:

- **China:** expanded existing Baodun and Shijiahe culture entries to cover the period, with approximate dates. I also corrected the Shijiahe map point.
- **Kenya:** added a cautious note about the Lothagam North communal cemetery near Lake Turkana. Sources disagree about its map coordinates, so the game uses a broad regional reference, not an exact site pin.
- **Mesopotamia:** made the conflict and Assur entries less definite and added an approximate reference to an inscription linking Entemena of Lagash with a ruler of Uruk.
- **Egypt:** added a reign reference for Unas and corrected the beekeeping entry to refer to a relief in Nyuserre’s sun temple. The exact year is not known. An old courier claim is now marked uncertain.
- **Iceland:** sources give different dates for the Hekla-4 eruption. I changed the 2360 BCE entry to a note rather than a second disruptive eruption. The separate, existing hazard at 2310 BCE remains unchanged and its timing still needs review.

Long period notes may appear in several years; that does **not** prove something happened every year. Dates and locations changed in old entries may affect saved games and have not been tested in one.

## People for later

I saved **seven person research leads**, including Entemena, Lugal-kinishe-dudu, Djedkare and Unas. They are **not active VIP NPCs**. Dates and uncertain relationships are not filled in as facts.

Sources include [Baodun culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Baodun_culture&oldid=1322638543), [Shijiahe culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shijiahe_culture&oldid=1349754993), [Lothagam North research](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1721975115), [Entemena](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Entemena&oldid=1368312406) and [Hekla](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hekla&oldid=1369147650).
