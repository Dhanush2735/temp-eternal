## Verification and boundaries

Twenty focused Python tests passed before and after installation. One context-pack and both hazard-pack stages were reversed in memory against their actual pre-application packs; all 15 installed after-images were checked unique in the indexed regional pack. Replacements are atomic per pack, **not across packs**. Reverse the hazard move before reversing the older context update to restore the previous pack order. No live loader, Minecraft gameplay, saved-world, manifest-integrity or jar test was run. The existing jar is stale and the separate crawler storage guard remains blocked. Changing IDs' packs or kinds may affect saved worlds; that is not tested.

Five endpoint years were previously counted. Under the project's broad shared-period-context convention, this batch adds 45 to **1,098 partly worked years across periods; 0 fully collected**. This is a bookkeeping convention for partial work, not 1,098 independently researched annual histories or a completeness percentage. Next chronological slice is 2339–2290 BCE, but all preceding slices still need further collection and review. Workspace storage is approximately 128 MiB and near the snapshot cap; durable capacity is urgently needed before substantially expanding the collection.
