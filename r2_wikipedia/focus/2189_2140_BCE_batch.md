# Preliminary historical work: 2189–2140 BCE

**Status: partly worked, not fully collected.** All 50 English Wikipedia year titles resolve to the 22nd-century BC article, revision 1381193324 (`year_titles_2189_2140.json`). Redirects are not separate annual histories; worldwide linked-article research remains incomplete.

## Indexed game-data changes

Three existing records were improved without creating additional event IDs:

- The Ur-Baba of Lagash context names his daughter Ninalla and her husband Gudea. The biography uses one approximate 2157–2144 BCE chronology. The [Louvre catalogue for the Ur-Bau statue AO 9](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010119547) instead dates the object to 2135–2125 BCE. Both are recorded without combining them into an exact reign or marriage date.
- The Gudea context includes Ur-Ningirsu, whom a [Metropolitan Museum statue inscription](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/329069) names as Gudea's son and successor. A separate [Met discussion](https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/immaterial-stone) dates a Gudea statue around 2090 BCE, different from the context's selected c.2144–2124 reign chronology. This illustrates differing chronological schemes, not a verified exact succession.
- The Egyptian First Intermediate Period context adds evidence for Qakare Ibi and Netjerkare Siptah, while retaining uncertainty about Eighth-Dynasty dates and the possibly fictitious Seventh Dynasty. The later Nitocris tradition is not automatically treated as a separate historical queen. A period reference does not mean the people were present throughout it or met.

Source examples: [Ur-Baba](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ur-Baba&oldid=1342422728), [Gudea](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Gudea&oldid=1382613160), [Qakare Ibi](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Qakare_Ibi&oldid=1382108314) and [Eighth Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Eighth_Dynasty_of_Egypt&oldid=1376337160). The independent museum records are cited as catalogues; their dates are not forced to agree with Wikipedia.

Two context packs and both hazard packs were staged and logically reversed against their real inputs; all 14 installed after-images were verified. Writes are atomic per pack, **not across packs**. Context was applied first and hazards second; to reverse this batch without intervening changes:

PYTHONPATH=r2_wikipedia python r2_wikipedia/apply_2189_2140_bce_context.py rollback
