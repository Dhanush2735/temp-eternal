## What changed in the game

I updated the indexed game JSON: **18 corrections and 7 new history notes**.

- **South Asia:** described Cemetery H as a regional Late Harappan culture, rather than an exact collapse of the whole Indus civilization in 1700 BCE.
- **Crete:** qualified the palace-period dates and Knossos destruction. Its exact date and cause are uncertain; the old fixed fire/disruption effect was removed.
- **China and Central Asia:** qualified the dates and identity of early bronze cultures, Shang and the Bactria–Margiana complex. These are not certain one-year beginnings or endings.
- **Mesopotamia:** corrected four Assyrian ruler notes and added approximate Babylonian reign notes for Abi-Eshuh and Ammi-Ditana. Different historical chronologies give different dates.
- **Elsewhere:** added approximate regional context for Kerma, Erlitou, Sechin Alto, Valdivia and Bronze Age Britain. These notes do not prove that a particular event happened every year.
