[Shahr-e Sukhteh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahr-e_Sukhteh) and [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1456/) also give differing overall site chronologies. None of these ranges should be used to date every feature or event precisely.
