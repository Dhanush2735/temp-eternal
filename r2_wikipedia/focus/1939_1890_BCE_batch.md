## Indexed game JSON changes

Five existing regional records were improved while retaining IDs and nominal slots:

- Khnumhotep II's Beni Hasan tomb was wrongly treated as a political event. It is now a cultural note. The century article names a modern facsimile of an ancient painting; the exact harvest-scene object was not independently identified. The [Met's Aamu facsimile](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544548) is a different work, made in 1931, and is evidence of the distinction between modern copy and original—not proof about the harvest facsimile. Neither the old slot nor the site coordinates establish an exact date.
- The existing Gungunum note now mentions qualified family connections and the uncertain transfer of Ur. The Ur-Ninurta note references the Nippur model court case, not a proven historical trial. Scholarly readings of Nin-dada's verdict differ: the [Penn Museum interpretation](https://www.penn.museum/sites/bulletin/3462/) reads acquittal, while another translation reads condemnation. The [JSTOR catalog preview](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt19wccw4.44) identifies the relevant translation; its full text was not accessed.
- The Kültepe note links [CDLI's account of Pushuken, Lamassi and Ahaha](https://cdli.earth/cdli-tablet/123). CDLI places the beginning of Pushuken's Kanesh trade before 1895 BCE; it does not date the particular letter to this batch or put the whole family in Kanesh. The period slice is not an annual presence claim.
- The Late Harappan boundary is qualified as a conventional approximate regional transition, not a single civilization-wide event. Existing location and radius remain broad references.

Sixteen uncertain-date volcanic records were moved from the destructive hazard pack into indexed, non-disruptive cultural notes, preserving IDs and nominal slots. Fourteen were individually matched to Smithsonian GVP profiles. Two NOAA-sourced records were also matched directly to [NOAA Black Peak event 5235](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/volcanoes/5235) and [Cerro Hudson event 5234](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/volcanoes/5234), by year, volcano number, and coordinates, in addition to GVP. NOAA's list and detail responses disagree on their `publish` flags; the reason is unresolved. The datasets are not assumed independent. Each profile, dating method and uncertainty appears in the hazard manifest. A question mark is not a numerical uncertainty range; the converted notes do not deny real eruptions or possible damage.

The records' inherited dates, durations, map points and radii are reference values, not exact historical dates, occupancy proofs or measured footprints. Reader boundary behavior differs by pack, and changes may affect saves.
