## Indexed game JSON

**Thirteen existing records were corrected or qualified, one note added, and 15 imprecise disaster records moved from the active hazard pack to indexed historical notes.** IDs and nominal year slots were retained; potential duplicates were not silently merged. The updates are in game resources, but no updated jar was built or tested.

- **Egypt:** qualified a claim that Amenhotep III attempted a Nile–Red Sea canal. The 1380s page cites a dead link; the linked [Canal of the Pharaohs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canal_of_the_Pharaohs) article discusses differing ancient attributions and does not establish this particular attempt. Qualified the marriage of Amenhotep III and Tiye without inventing an exact place or year. Added a separate [Gilukhipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilukhipa) diplomatic-marriage note: her scarab records Amenhotep III's regnal year 10, while its absolute conversion is uncertain. Gilukhipa is distinct from her niece [Tadukhipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadukhipa).
- **Amarna:** corrected an Amarna site point and converted a fixed political effect to context. Another inherited Amarna entry remains in its 1372 slot but now explicitly flags its conflict with Akhenaten's [approximate reign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhenaten); the two entries may overlap. The [city article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amarna) proposes c. 1346 BCE, but reign chronology differs. A separate name-change entry now notes conflicting 1348/1345 claims; neither is treated as an exact event.
- **Crete:** the 1370s page says Minoan culture ended and Knossos was abandoned c. 1375. The linked [culture article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoan_civilization) instead describes later continuation and change; [Knossos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knossos) describes a debated palatial destruction, conventionally c. 1350, and later occupation of the wider site. Both entries are now qualified.
- **Levant:** moved the [Labaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labaya) reference point from near Megiddo to Shechem/Tell Balata, while retaining the approximate date and explaining that letters contain competing claims involving Biridiya and others. A Jerusalem/Amarna record no longer claims a verified first mention in 1369 BCE. The source archive spans decades.
- **Eastern Mediterranean:** the inherited Lukka entry combined a mid-14th-century raid on Alashiya with the much later [Battle of Kadesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukka_lands), around 1274 BCE. It is now a qualified raid-context note with a Cyprus regional reference—not a claimed exact raid site or a 1351 Kadesh battle. An Aegean–western Mediterranean contact record no longer asserts a fixed-year political event across several sites.
- **Assyria and Mitanni:** qualified an alleged 1378 disestablishment as an approximate period boundary, not an established collapse. Qualified the Hittite–Mitanni campaign entry: the [Tushratta article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tushratta) describes plundering the district of Washukanni; neither the date nor the location of its capital is securely established by this record. Political and military chronologies remain disputed.

Reference slots, regional coordinates and radii do not prove exact dates, individual presence, territories or battlefields. Earlier notes on the Egtved burial, Yinxu and the Amenhotep III succession remain qualified. Other inherited and overlapping records remain unreviewed.

**Two Ugarit disaster rows:** [NOAA earthquake 9712](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/9712) explicitly links [tsunami 4](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/view/hazards/tsunami/event-more-info/4); they are not independent evidence.
