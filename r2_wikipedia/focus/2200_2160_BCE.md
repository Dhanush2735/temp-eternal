# 2200 to 2160 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2200, 2190, 2180, 2170 and 2160 BCE**. These years are **not fully collected**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **10 entries** and added **8** in the game’s indexed history files:

- **Mesopotamia:** added approximate references for the Akkadian rulers Dudu and Shu-turul, and removed claims that Akkad was certainly destroyed by a Gutian army in a particular year.
- **Egypt and Crete:** clarified disputed Egyptian reign dates and changed the Minoan period entry to an approximate Early Minoan reference. A Theban garden model was moved to a later date based on a possible museum identification; that identification is not certain.
- **Philippines, India and Australia:** marked the timing of early Batanes settlement and a proposed India–Australia genetic connection as uncertain. Added a non-disruptive climate-record reference at Mawmluh Cave; it does not claim a worldwide drought or a civilization’s collapse in a particular year.
- **Peru, South Asia, Nubia and central Europe:** continued approximate Caral, Harappan, Kerma and Únětice history references.

Some changed dates, locations or effects may affect saved games. The seven overlapping volcano entries were not reviewed. Other site dates and old war entries also need checking. Period references may repeat in game news; that does not prove activity in every year.

## People for later

I saved **9 research leads**, including Dudu, Shu-turul and several Egyptian rulers. One lead, Meketre, is from a later period and was researched because of the garden model. These are **not active VIP NPCs**. The Nitocris story is recorded separately as a tradition, not established history.

Sources include [Dudu of Akkad](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dudu_of_Akkad&oldid=1342421671), [Shu-turul](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shu-turul&oldid=1342421404), [Minoan chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Minoan_chronology&oldid=1379622875), [the Met’s garden model](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544256), [the 4.2-kiloyear event](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=4.2-kiloyear_event&oldid=1380005033) and [research on early Batanes](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/p269441/pdf/ch057.pdf).
