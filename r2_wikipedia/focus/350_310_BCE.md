# 350–310 BCE: partial collection and game-data update

**Focus years:** 350, 340, 330, 320 and 310 BCE. These are five ten-year steps, not a review of every year between them. Each year remains partly worked, not fully collected.

## What changed in the project

The indexed game-history packs now contain **30 corrected records, 23 moved records and one new record**. The 53 existing records kept their IDs. The new active political entry represents the traditional succession of King Wei after King Xuan of Chu in 340 BCE; the Jingzhou-area marker is a broad capital-region reference, not a known death site.

- Corrected years for the Persian Gate, Lake Vadimo, Crannon, Maling, Tyre, Gaza (332 and 312 BCE), Uxian Defile, Gaugamela and Caudine Forks while preserving their active military effects. Corrected the 311 BCE Himera River battle from Syracuse to the approximate Licata area, and extended the Syracuse siege to start in 311 BCE. The Caudine Forks encounter is described as a surrender without a battle, not invented fighting.
- Marked disputed alternatives for Megalopolis (331 or 330 BCE), Crimissus (339 or 340 BCE) and the Hellespont (321 or 320 BCE). A single year is used for the active game slot, not a claim of certain dating. Battlefield positions and ancient details can also be uncertain.
- Retained one active Perinthus siege and changed two overlapping records to non-damaging references. Corrected the Byzantium siege dates and removed an incorrectly placed Crimea siege effect. A second Lake Vadimo record and an incorrectly Carthage-pinned Himera record are now notes rather than duplicate battles. Other overlaps remain to be checked.
- Located the Derveni papyrus at its findspot, not its composition site, and moved the Aphrodite of Knidos reference from a modern museum to the ancient site; the original is lost. Qualified the motive for the Persepolis fire and the Alexandria population claim. The former combined Nicaea/Troas entry now concerns Nicaea alone, with its different approximate founding date. Mauryan accession is qualified; an overlapping political entry is now a note.
- Moved **22 uncertain-dated volcanic records** from active disasters to non-damaging historical notes. GVP lists confirmed eruptions for most; the date is uncertain, and for Feni the eruption itself is uncertain. Two Augustine entries have overlapping date estimates but are not assumed to be duplicates. The stored one-year slot is a reference, not proof of an eruption in that exact year.
- NOAA catalogues an Aegean earthquake at 330 BCE with a coarse location and estimated magnitude; its active entry remains but is labelled uncertain. The linked NOAA tsunami record has event-validity rating 1, **very doubtful**; its record was moved to a non-damaging note. This rating concerns the tsunami and does not prove the earthquake was false. Historical dates, locations, game radius and magnitude need further research.
- Added **10 research-only people leads**, including Chandragupta, Bindusara, Porus, Qu Yuan, Xunzi and King Xuan. They are not spawnable VIPs. Their links and uncertainties are in `history/sources/wikipedia_350_310_bce.json`.
