## Indexed game JSON

**Eleven existing records were corrected or qualified, one contextual note added, and 14 imprecisely dated volcano hazards moved to non-destructive indexed notes.** IDs and nominal date slots were retained. Related duplicate IDs are preserved pending careful reconciliation.

- **Megiddo:** the [battle article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Megiddo_(15th_century_BC)) places the battle in Thutmose III's regnal year 23 and discusses competing calendar dates, including 1457, 1479 and 1482 BCE. The 1460s overview gives c.1469. The inherited 1469-year pin was wrongly placed in Egypt; it was corrected to Megiddo and qualified. Three entries describe the same battle at different reference slots, not three proven battles; fixed-year military effects were removed. Egyptian inscriptions are the principal account, not neutral independent evidence for every detail.
- **Egypt:** the [mortuary temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortuary_temple_of_Hatshepsut) record is now a building-context note, not an exact-year political change; the article places construction across regnal years 7–20 and leaves builder attribution unclear. The [Met's sphinx](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544442) is dated c.1473–1458 BCE, not one verified year. Thutmose III's Mitanni campaigns have uncertain absolute dates and the stored pin is not a verified 1468 battlefield.
- Added a **Punt expedition** note linked to [Hatshepsut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_of_Punt) and the official [Nehsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nehsi). The source places the depicted expedition in regnal year 9; Nehsi's exact personal role is interpreted. Thebes pin refers to the temple reliefs, **not a verified location of Punt**, departure point or route; 1470 is only an approximate slot. The note does not assert conquest.
- **Indonesia:** a misleading first-introduction claim and a Java pin were corrected to the documented Minanga Sipakko site in Sulawesi. [Deng et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-67747-3) report evidence of domesticated rice by at least 3,500 years ago; this does not establish the exact first arrival year or the same practice across all Indonesia.
- **China:** an incomplete Shang wall claim pointed at Anyang. It now references [Zhengzhou Shang City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Shang_City), with its identification as Ao or Bo marked disputed; the [Cambridge article abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/early-china/article/2-the-shang-city-at-zhengzhou-and-related-problems/CC79184977EE1B2D6143C9EB03FA9720) does not choose between those names.
- **Ireland and religious traditions:** Fíachu Labrainne's succession is a medieval legendary chronology, not a proven 1449 political event. The 1451 entry into the Promised Land is attributed to Ussher's later chronology, not treated as a treaty. The [Book of Leviticus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Leviticus) article describes long and disputed composition history; the 1440s page's exact 1445–1444 writing claim was not installed as fact.
- **Ganibatum:** [the linked article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganibatum) mentions the name in 18th-century BCE Mari texts. A proposed connection to Gnbtyw in an Egyptian regnal-year 32 inscription is contested, so the old claim that the people first appeared there in 1448 BCE was qualified. The primary works were not inspected.
