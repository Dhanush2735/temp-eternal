# 2970 BCE — ten-year step

## History added to the game

The [30th-century BCE page](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=30th_century_BC&oldid=1371895853) gives **around 2970 BCE** as an estimate for Egyptian ruler Djet’s death. I added this as a regional game-history note, clearly marked as uncertain. The map point is near his burial place at Abydos, **not a claim about where he died**.

The earlier 2980 BCE entry about Djet’s reign remains separate; neither date is known exactly. The Early Harappan period note also remains available at 2970 BCE.

## VIP research

I updated Djet’s saved candidate profile with the death estimate and collected a profile for [Den](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Den_(pharaoh)&oldid=1381183065), a First Dynasty ruler linked to Djet and Merneith. His accession date is debated: the article’s approximate 2970 BCE date and radiocarbon models have different ranges. I have not invented a birth date, death date or year-by-year location for him.

These profiles are **not active VIP NPCs**. NPC historical behaviour and alternate-timeline interactions have not been implemented by this step.
