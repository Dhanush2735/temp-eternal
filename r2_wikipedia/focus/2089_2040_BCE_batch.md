# Preliminary historical work: 2089–2040 BCE

**Partly worked, not fully collected.** All 50 English Wikipedia year titles resolve to the 21st-century BC article, revision 1371895845; they are not separate annual histories. More worldwide linked articles and records remain to be researched.

## Indexed game-data changes

- Expanded the existing **Seahenge** note to distinguish Holme I from nearby Holme II. Tree-ring dating identifies 2049 BCE for the timber felling; the construction date is inferred, and the monuments' purpose remains uncertain. The [Norfolk Museums record](https://www.museumscollections.norfolk.gov.uk/article/30264/Holme-II) describes Holme II separately; the [Norfolk heritage record](https://www.heritage.norfolk.gov.uk/record-details?MNF33771) describes Holme I.
- Expanded the **Intef III** context to include Iah and the succession to Mentuhotep II. The article describes officials Tjeti and Henenu in inscriptions; Iah's marriage to Intef III and Neferu II's paternity need qualification. Competing Egyptian chronological schemes do not establish co-rule or exact birth, death, meeting or succession dates.
- Expanded the **Shulgi** context with Nippur governor Ur-Nanibgal and his father Lugal-engardug, named on [Louvre seal AO 22312 in CDLI](https://cdli.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/search?all_composite_no=Q001751). A dedication for Shulgi's life is not proof of a meeting or of the officials' presence at the game's Ur reference point.

Thirty existing GVP volcano entries were moved from deterministic hazards to indexed non-disruptive regional notes: 27 have confirmed eruptions but imprecise dates, and **Nevsehir-Acigöl, Mariveles and Soufrière Guadeloupe are themselves classified as uncertain eruptions**. IDs, nominal game slots and inherited coordinates/radii were preserved. Their placement and dates do not establish a specific vent, exact year, extent, absence of effects or eyewitness record. The manifest records each volcano, GVP link, dating method and uncertainty.

Notable caveats: the [Erebus GVP profile](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=390020) shows 2050 BCE ±3000 years in the eruption summary but ±1000 on its VEI line. The [Northern East Pacific Rise](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=334050) entry shows ±2000 in the eruption summary and ±1000 on the VEI line; it is submarine. [Rungwe's profile](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=222166) says VEI 5 while a GVP regional table shows VEI 4. None of these competing values has been silently reconciled.

The **NOAA-tagged Long Island 2040 BCE hazard remains unchanged**: [GVP's profile](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=251050) lists a confirmed VEI 6 event dated 2040 ±110 years, but the specific NOAA source row has not been independently retrieved or matched. This is a remaining review item, not a claim that 2040 is exact.
