## Scope

All 50 year titles redirect to [18th century BCE, revision 1370570236](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=18th_century_BC&oldid=1370570236); see `year_titles_1789_1740.json`. Redirects are not individual year histories. Selected linked articles and other sources were reviewed, **not every linked article**. Reading included Mari, Yamhad and Eshnunna; [Sechin Alto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sechin_Alto), [Valdivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valdivia_culture), [Kerma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerma_culture), and [Sintashta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sintashta_culture) were also consulted for broader geographic context, but their existing indexed notes were not changed in this batch. Dates and interpretation vary; period overlap is not proof of an annual event. The crawler remains blocked by its storage guard.

## Indexed game JSON

Six existing notes were enriched or corrected, and one new note was added:

- The Shibtu note now records her parents, Yarim-Lim I and Gashera, and [Asqudum and Rishiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shibtu) as envoys in the reported marriage negotiations. The Zimri-Lim note adds qualified Yamhad connections and his uncertain relationship to Yahdun-Lim. These links do not imply exact meeting dates or annual presence.
- The Mari investiture mural note now cites the [Louvre catalogue](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010144553), AO 19826, which gives the findspot as palace courtyard 106 and discovery in 1935–1936. Its catalogue labels the date **2000–1800 BCE**, whereas the [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investiture_of_Zimri-Lim) places the mural in the 18th century BCE. This unresolved conflict is explicitly recorded; the inherited 1775–1761 slot is only one reference convention, not a confirmed creation date.
- The Samsu-iluna note distinguishes Rim-Sîn II from Rim-Sîn I and notes a disagreement between the linked biographies on whether the rebellion began in Samsu-iluna's eighth or ninth regnal year. His relationship to the earlier Rim-Sîn is uncertain.
- The Hammurabi note now flags its older statement that the Mari capture and Zimri-Lim fate records were unreviewed as **stale**: those related records were qualified in the earlier 1750–1710 batch. The correction is appended so the inherited evidence remains visible. Dates and fate remain uncertain.
- The generic Eshnunna war-pack note records [Dadusha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadusha) and family alongside qualified relations with Shamshi-Adad I and Ishme-Dagan I. Its inherited range and city pin do not identify a specific battle or continuous war.
- A new note for **Yarim-Lim I of Yamhad** uses an approximate 1780–1764 BCE reign and an Aleppo regional reference. He is connected to Shibtu and Zimri-Lim; his son Hammurabi I of Yamhad is distinct from Hammurabi of Babylon. The city point is not a personal itinerary or territorial boundary. A name scan found no matching indexed event, but cannot rule out semantic duplicates.

Five GVP-confirmed eruptions with uncertain dates were moved from the destructive hazard pack into indexed non-disruptive notes: [Piton de la Fournaise](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=233020), 1785 BCE ±90; [Ata](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=282070), 1780 BCE (?); [Arenal complex/Cerro Chato](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=345033), 1770 BCE ±100; [Merapi](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=263250), 1770 BCE ±75; and [St. Helens](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=321050), 1770 BCE ±100. Ata's question mark is not a numerical uncertainty range. The Arenal-complex map point is not necessarily the Cerro Chato vent. Moves do not deny eruptions or possible damage; inherited slots, radii and points are not precise dates or measured footprints.
