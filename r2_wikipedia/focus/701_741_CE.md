# 701–741 CE: partial research and game history updates

**Years partly worked:** 701, 711, 721, 731 and 741 CE. The five year pages, initial sections of their decade pages and selected linked articles were checked. Many links remain unchecked. Decade pages reuse year-page material and are not independent confirmation. Wikipedia pages were current when consulted and generally not revision-pinned.

## Indexed game JSON

Installed **30 updates and three additions** across four indexed packs. Existing IDs and start/end fields were preserved. The additions cover Philippicus taking the Byzantine throne in 711, a Rome synod on religious images in 731, and Charles Martel's death and succession in 741. A name scan across indexed packs and a targeted 699–744 scan found no matching additions; this cannot prove semantic uniqueness. The 744 Kempraten record was also corrected because it was encountered during this pass.

The guarded before/after manifest is `reviews/701_741_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `083ce13840999206f4e669fe341beb4a1fbc872392f19ea8d7c312ab6752c89f`. `apply_701_741_ce_context.py` stages, applies or rolls back with exact-image checks. Writes are atomic per pack, not across all packs. Each after-image records sources and a reason.

- **701:** Adulis now uses the port site rather than Aksum and describes its poorly understood abandonment, not a certain 701 destruction. Bulgarian and Lombard successions have competing chronologies. The Taihō Code remains in the 701 slot as a qualified note: its linked article gives 703 for enactment, and Fujiwara-kyō is only a capital reference. The Dayr al-Jamajim article says 701 in text but 703 in its infobox; the active battle retains a qualified Kufa regional reference.
- **711:** The linked Multan battle article dates its conquest to 714 rather than the 711 year-page claim, so the 711 entry is a qualified note. Toniná captured Palenque's ruler, but his later fate is uncertain; lasting conquest was removed from that entry. The Hōryū-ji reconstruction date is approximate. The Beorhtfrith battle and Grimoald's marriage have regional rather than precise-site references. The 711 Iberian conquest entry is a note, since conquest took years; a separate campaign and battles remain active. Guadalete's exact battlefield is unidentified, although its separately indexed battle remains active at its existing approximate point.
- **721:** A legendary Prague foundation and an approximate Yangzhou storm claim are qualified notes. Bertrada's disputed parentage and Rigobert's uncertain chronology are no longer asserted as settled. The Tervel/Kormisosh transition is disputed. Kʼakʼ Tiliw's linked biography dates his reign to about 728 rather than a 721 death, so the Naranjo succession story was corrected; Naranjo's archaeological site is a polity reference.
- **731:** Bede's book uses Jarrow as a twin-monastery reference, not a proven writing location. Tatwine now uses Canterbury as the archiepiscopal-seat reference, not an asserted consecration site. Ceolwulf's brief deposition could be 731 or 732. The Munuza entry is a regional note and may overlap the still-active Wikidata Battle of Llívia (`q17605393`); the exact relationship needs more evidence. The detailed Ragenfrid meeting account also needs verification.
- **741:** The earlier York church may have suffered fire damage, but its exact site and the extent of damage are uncertain; the record no longer claims the whole city burned. Kempraten references were moved from St. Gallen to Kempraten, including the 744 document. Shōmu ordered both provincial monasteries and nunneries; construction was not necessarily immediate. Lady Six Sky's death is separated from the unsupported claim that Yax Mayuy first succeeded her in 741; his parentage is uncertain. Dos Pilas ruler Kʼawiil Chan Kʼinich's accession is retained in 741 without choosing between conflicting June dates.

Coordinates labeled site, city, capital or region are gameplay references, not proof of exact event sites or territorial footprints. Readers use different end-year conventions, and an `effect: note` does **not** guarantee suppression in every reader. Moved records and saved-world behavior were not tested.

- The 741 Artabasdos year-page account and his biography disagree on coup/coronation timing; no new 741 coup was added. The Dayr al-Jamajim article's date conflict needs independent research.
- Hazards remain **active and unreviewed**, including the exact-start 701 Japan earthquake `hz_b66708a63b` and tsunami `hz_db5546a3c5`, and 711 Chokaisan `hz_d325c516be`. An inclusive 690–750 interval scan also found many decade-end hazards, including 700, 710, 720, 730 and 740 entries, and the 698–701 plague. A stored boundary overlap does not prove a hazard triggers in every reader. `Disasters.java` loads hazards independently; changing kind/effect is not a reliable way to neutralize them.
