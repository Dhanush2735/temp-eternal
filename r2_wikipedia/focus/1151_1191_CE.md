# 1151–1191 CE: partial collection and project update

**Years partly worked:** 1151, 1161, 1171, 1181 and 1191 CE. English Wikipedia year pages, beginnings of their decade pages, and selected linked articles were consulted. These five years are **not fully collected**: the intervening years and many links remain. Decade transclusions are not independent evidence from the year pages.

## Installed changes

- Updated **40 existing records** and added **7** across six indexed game-data packs. Related 1150 and 1189 legacy entries were also qualified. Existing IDs and stored start/end years were preserved. Exact before/after images, source URLs and reasons are in `r2_wikipedia/reviews/1151_1191_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `c627bf8c54cb19b3fd40defe8c6b895b33574e6aac0130aeaf52cb019c6c2432`.
- Added **47 research-only people leads** in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1151_1191_ce.json`. These are not finished profiles or naturally spawnable VIPs. Date, place and biographical uncertainties still need review.
- Thirteen guarded-installer tests passed before and after installation. All six staged packs were logically reversed to their original documents, and all 47 installed after-images were checked for unique exact matches across the index. Writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across all packs. Searches cannot prove semantic uniqueness.

## Examples

- **1151:** Corrected the location for Geoffrey of Anjou's death to Château-du-Loir, the Treaty of Tudilén to a Navarre-area reference, and Anping Bridge to Fujian. The year page dates Bolton Priory to 1151, while the linked abbey article gives 1154 for its move to Bolton; the old 1151 slot remains with that conflict stated. Added Suger’s death at Saint-Denis.
- **1161:** Located the Battle of Tangdao off Shandong in the Yellow Sea rather than the Yangtze, and distinguished it from the Yangtze battle at Caishi. Magnus Henriksson was reportedly killed in battle near Örebro; his biography also gives 1162 in its infobox. Added the Cross of Saint Euphrosyne, using Polotsk only as the commissioning-place reference; the article requests more citations.
- **1171:** Moved accounts of the end of the Fatimid caliphate to Cairo and Henry II’s Irish landing to the Waterford area. Saladin was already vizier; his biography dates his sultanate to 1174. The linked Ayla siege article dates the capture to late **1170**, but its 1171 legacy row remains and overlaps a 1170 account.
- **1181:** Moved Henry the Lion's submission to Erfurt and Pope Alexander III's death to Civita Castellana. Added former Emperor Takakura's death. The 1150 and 1181 Polonnaruwa–Pagan campaign rows are qualified because the linked article describes multiple campaigns and cautions against accepting all claims at face value. Nara's December 1180 Japanese-calendar date corresponds to January 1181 Julian in the siege article. For Sunomata-gawa, the article itself conflicts on June versus August.
- **1191:** Added the First Battle of Tarain, Richard I's Cyprus conquest, the deaths of philosopher Suhrawardi and the imperial coronation of Henry VI and Constance. Moved Bern’s traditional foundation to Bern, the Naples naval action to Naples, and the Khmer–Champa campaign from Angkor to a broad Champa-region proxy. The Glastonbury monks' identification of Arthur's grave is now presented as a claim, not proof.

## Important limits

- **41 hazard rows** overlap at least one sampled year when both stored endpoints are counted inclusively. This does not prove each triggers in that year: different readers interpret end boundaries differently. All 41 remain **active and unreviewed**; their list is `r2_wikipedia/reviews/1151_1191_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`. Neither hazard nor nature packs were edited.
- Overlapping active entries include the Fatimid transition; Ayla 1170/1171; Burma campaigns including the disputed 1150 slot; Yōwa famine in regional and nature packs; and the siege/naval action/campaign of Naples. Some are genuinely distinct subevents; others could duplicate one another. They were not deduplicated. The preserved siege-of-Acre range ends in 1191, but some consumers treat that end as exclusive, so visibility in 1191 is not guaranteed.
- The 1191 Taungoo-region appointment concerns Kanba Myint; the town of Taungoo itself was founded later, in 1279. The Danish Finland crusade uses a very broad uncertain proxy. The battle of Dösjebro has conflicting chronologies across articles. These qualifications do not prove the legacy timestamps or locations correct.
- The regional Iceland insurance row changed from disaster/disruption to cultural/note, because a report about insurance is not an observed plague or fire. Richard I's biography row changed from war to political/note but remains in the war pack. The Ayyadieh massacre remains classified as a battle with military effect; changing its title does not neutralize that effect. Location, kind or effect changes can affect gameplay and saved-world interpretation; regional points are proxies, not exact sites.
- The 1181 guest-star event and uncertain Yesügei death were not given invented Earth coordinates. Many other linked events and people remain to check. No Java, Forge, in-game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was performed. The old jar **does not include** these JSON changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.

**Overall:** 429 years partly worked; 0 fully collected. **Next:** 1201, 1211, 1221, 1231 and 1241 CE.
