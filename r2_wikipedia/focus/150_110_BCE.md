# History collection: 150–110 BCE

## Progress

Worked on **150, 140, 130, 120 and 110 BCE**, using English Wikipedia year pages, selected linked articles and Smithsonian volcano records. This is partial work, not complete collection.

**Total: 299 years partly worked; 0 years fully collected.** Intervening years still need their own work.

## Changes in the game files

- Updated **14 existing records**, keeping their IDs. Corrected the Battle of Suthul from 109 to 110 BCE and corrected the Stratonicea battle's location from Caria to a broader area around Stratonicea in Lydia. Both remain military events; the exact battle locations are uncertain.
- Relocated the marriage of Alexander Balas and Cleopatra Thea from Egypt to Ptolemais Akko, where their biographies place it. Qualified the dates or claims around Simon Thassi, Pontic succession, Sanchi, Buyeo, Indo-Scythian migrations and the Hellenistic Prince statue.
- Moved **15 uncertain-date volcano records** and **one disputed flood record** out of the damaging hazard pack into non-damaging historical notes. Smithsonian confirms the volcano eruptive periods but does not establish the exact game years. The Cymbrian flood is an ancient account whose occurrence and extent are uncertain. The one-year slots are references, not exact dates.
- Kept the earlier Etna eruption record active while marking its date and reported fatalities uncertain. GVP lists a reported 141 BCE eruption with a ±365-day date uncertainty; the existing imported record's 40-deaths claim needs further checking. Its stored end overlaps 140 BCE in the disaster loader.
- Added **one historical note** about historian Sima Tan, who died in 110 BCE; his son Sima Qian completed the *Records of the Grand Historian*.
- Recorded **13 possible people leads** for later R3 VIP research, including Cleopatra Thea, Wei Zifu, Huo Qubing, Jugurtha and Sima Tan. These are research notes, **not spawnable VIPs**. No historical behavior or alternate-timeline feature was implemented here.
