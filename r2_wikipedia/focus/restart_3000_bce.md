I removed that unsupported civilization hint from `places_pleiades.json.gz`; I did not substitute a guessed civilization. The site's previous stored dates and `town` rank remain unchanged and unverified; they are not evidence of continuous occupation. Other game systems or saved data may still provide a civilization. This related Skara Brae article was checked as contextual research; its exact path from the millennium's link chain was not established in this pass.
