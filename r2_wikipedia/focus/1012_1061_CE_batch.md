# R2 preliminary pass: 1012–1061 CE

**Status:** Partial research and indexed-data improvements, not a complete collection of any year. This batch archives all 50 resolved annual pages and reviews a selection of linked articles. Raw wikitext does not expand transclusions, and most linked articles and references remain unexamined. The scan contains 666 indexed rows whose **start** falls in this interval, including 67 hazards; events starting earlier but overlapping the interval were not covered by that scan. Most rows were not individually reviewed.

## Active indexed changes

Applied 23 guarded context changes to five indexed packs. Examples:

- Relocated the 1054 Manzikert siege from an Ankara-area coordinate to the Manzikert/Malazgirt reference point; it is distinct from the 1071 battle.
- Replaced Thanjavur with a Gangaikonda Cholapuram reference for the Chola capital, and made the 1025–1026 Srivijaya campaign multi-site context rather than a single precise location or uniform political footprint.
- Corrected a 1050 entry that was actually the 1010–1011 Goryeo–Khitan conflict; distinguished the 1018 invasion from the 1019 Battle of Kwiju.
- Qualified Nri origin chronology, the traditional Thaton conquest story, Airlangga's partition chronology, and Bi Sheng's invention period. Moved the Sanghyang Tapak inscription context to West Java and removed the unsupported claim that it established the kingdom.
- Put 1024 Song paper notes in Sichuan context, distinguishing earlier private notes from state issue, and corrected Cairo and Constantinople references.

Moved **19 hazard entries** out of the hazard pack to indexed, non-damaging context notes: 16 eruption estimates without an established exact year, and three problematic earthquake/seawave records. GVP's date ranges and methods are retained in the evidence notes; similarly named eruptions with overlapping uncertainty are not assumed identical. NOAA's 1039 entry combines reports concerning Smyrna and Constantinople, its related wave record is flagged and of uncertain validity, and its 1046–1047 record may conflate Diyarbakır and Ahlat. A NOAA casualty *amount order* is a category, not a literal count of three deaths.

**48 other hazards** starting in the interval were not moved in this pass and should not be assumed verified or safe. Indexed notes and coordinates are often partial city or regional proxies, not reconstructions of the complete historical footprint. Calendar endpoints vary between game readers and have not been validated in a saved world.

Associations do not establish simultaneous presence. These are research leads only: they are **not spawnable VIPs** and do not implement natural spawning, historical behavior, or alternate-timeline branching.
