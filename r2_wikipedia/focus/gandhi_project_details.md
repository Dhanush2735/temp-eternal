# Mahatma Gandhi: details currently in the project

This report assumes you mean **Mahatma Gandhi**, not Indira or Rajiv Gandhi. It shows what the project has saved, not a newly checked or complete biography.

## Is Gandhi a working VIP?

**No.** The project has an **unreviewed, non-spawnable VIP research candidate**, but no Gandhi entry in `vips/foundation_vips.json`. Natural VIP spawning and historical behavior are not implemented. The draft scenario uses the trigger `vip:mohandas_karamchand_gandhi`, but that is not proof it can spawn him or run correctly.

## Personal details in the saved research biography

- **Full name:** Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi.
- **Also called:** Mahatma, Bapu and Rashtrapita.
- **Born:** 2 October 1869, Porbandar, Kathiawar Agency, British Raj.
- **Died:** 30 January 1948, New Delhi, India. The biography says he was assassinated by gunshot.
- **Occupation:** lawyer, activist and politician. Known for the campaign for Indian independence and nonviolent resistance.
- **Years active:** 1893–1948, as listed in the saved infobox.
- **Political party:** Indian National Congress, 1920–1934, as listed in the infobox.
- **Congress office:** president from December 1924 to April 1925, according to the infobox.
- **Education:** Samaldas Arts College (did not graduate); University College London (informal auditing student, 1888–1891); Inns of Court School of Law.
- **Spouse:** Kasturba Gandhi; married in 1883, she died in 1944.
- **Children listed:** Harilal, Manilal, Ramdas and Devdas.
- **Parents listed:** Karamchand Gandhi and Putlibai Gandhi.
- **Memorials listed:** Raj Ghat and Gandhi Smriti, Delhi.

These facts come from the **research copy**, not from an active VIP profile. The saved [Wikipedia biography revision](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Mahatma_Gandhi&oldid=1381836151) and [Wikidata revision](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Q1001&oldid=2559532451) were retrieved on 28 October 2026. The saved Wikidata claims contain additional raw details, references and qualifiers, not all interpreted or verified.

## Other project material

- A research-only 1931 people lead says Gandhi signed the Gandhi–Irwin Pact; it links to the pact article.
- The saved research candidate includes source revisions, discovery links, raw infobox fields and structured Wikidata statements. It is marked `unreviewed_not_spawnable`.
- The project contains a draft alternate-history scenario, `scenarios/what_if_gandhi_1948.json`, triggered by preventing the assassination. Its eight entries imagine consequences in 1948–1951, including political effects, a different trial and a hypothetical natural death in 1951. **That 1951 death is fiction, not Gandhi's historical death.** The file itself says it requires expert review; its factual and causal claims have not been verified in this report.
- The saved biography and scenario differ on when Gandhi's January 1948 fast began. That disagreement needs checking rather than silently choosing one.
