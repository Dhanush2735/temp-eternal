# 301–341 CE: partial research and indexed game data

**Focus years:** 301, 311, 321, 331 and 341 CE. All five were partly worked, not fully collected; intervening years remain for later. Some nearby 300, 320 and 330 records were corrected without claiming complete research for those years.

## Indexed history JSON

17 existing records updated and two new records added across three indexed packs. IDs of existing records were retained and no records were moved between packs. Exact before/after images, additions, source URLs and uncertainty are in `reviews/301_341_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `0fb1affa3409ff8babc72e34fd697257d2cddd97d148edd29f766b81feeb1ea4`. Writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs.

- Fixed the San Marino traditional-foundation marker, previously in southeastern Italy, to a San Marino regional point and labeled it legend, not a proven independent republic in 301. Armenian Christianization and the earliest Echmiadzin church retain the traditional 301 reference while noting uncertain chronology.
- Moved the Sima Lun reference to Luoyang, retaining its political effect; qualified disagreements about the end day of his usurpation. Kept the Luoyang sack political record active, describing reported mass killing without asserting an exact verified death toll.
- Added the 311 Edict of Toleration as a non-disruptive historical note: the Wikipedia article says Serdica, but its quoted text says the edict was published at Nicomedia; the Serdica coordinate is a named reference, not a claim both cities are the same.
- The 321 Constantine/Goths record now uses the Trajan bridge area as a reference rather than the Danube delta; the claim of bridge repair is uncertain, and the marker is not a proven battle site. Cologne's 321 decree is labeled evidence of a community, not the date Jews first arrived there.
- Corrected the Church of the Holy Apostles location and marked its date approximate; qualified Constantine temple policy and the 50 scripture copies. The city of Rome is only a policy reference and the account of Eusebius is the surviving attestation for the commission. Moved the Gogugwon accession marker to the Gungnae capital region, not a confirmed coronation site.
- Added the 341 Council of Dedication in Antioch as a cultural note. The Sasanian Christian persecution record no longer treats coastal Seleucia Pieria in Turkey as Seleucia in Mesopotamia; it uses the Seleucia-Ctesiphon region in Iraq as church context, without asserting an exact execution site or thousands killed in one event. Samudragupta's campaigns and Allahabad inscription are no longer dated definitively to 341.
- Qualified the year-300 claim about compass navigation, the attribution and date of Aksum's Meroë campaign, Frumentius's date, and the dating and identity of the people behind Nydam Bog offerings. Their single-year slots are references, not proof of exact dates. The Nydam site article itself has citation warnings.

The active 341 NOAA tsunami record `hz_d78ecb8e17` says Cyprus but is centered near the Turkish/Syrian coast; it remains **active and unreviewed**, as do other hazard rows. `wpr_85232e4f89` still misplaces Zenobia's Asia Minor campaign in Greece (previous batch).
