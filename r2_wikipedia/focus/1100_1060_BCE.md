# 1100–1060 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked:** 1100, 1090, 1080, 1070 and 1060 BCE. The years between them remain for later. None of these years is fully collected.

## Changes made to the game

The indexed game history JSON now has **15 corrected records, 16 uncertain volcano records and one Lake Crescent landslide-wave record moved from active disasters to history notes, one war record moved to history, and seven new notes**. Seven corrections concern nearby years: 1104, two in 1089, 1082, 1079, 1078 and 1069 BCE. Existing IDs were kept.

- Corrected or qualified records about **Mycenaean Greece**, the Greek postpalatial period, **Proto-Villanovan culture**, the **Shang dynasty** and Egypt’s New Kingdom. The linked articles describe broader or conflicting chronologies, not single-year transitions. Corrected the Mycenaean regional point from Crete to mainland Greece.
- Marked the **Dorian invasion** as a traditional narrative and a discredited archaeological hypothesis rather than a confirmed invasion. The **Kurukshetra War** is an epic tradition whose historical basis and dating are disputed; two related notes may repeat. The surviving 1100 slots do not establish battles in that year.
- Qualified the traditional **1104 BCE foundation of Cádiz** against later archaeological evidence, and corrected misleading nearby records for Zhou kings **Cheng and Kang**, Egyptian chronology and a Babylonian famine. These years are retained as references to disputed claims, not verified dates.
- Added cautious reign-context notes for **Ramesses XI, Herihor, Smendes, Di Yi, Di Xin, Marduk-shapik-zeri and Ashur-bel-kala**. Locations are regional references, not proof of personal presence or a particular action in those years.
- Moved **16 volcanic records** to non-disruptive notes: Smithsonian’s Global Volcanism Program confirms eruptive periods but gives approximate dates. The nearby Belknap record had begun in 1071 but appeared in 1070 because the disaster loader treats end years inclusively. Hekla 3’s date and proposed environmental connections remain debated.
- Moved the **Lake Crescent** record to an uncertain note. Evidence supports an ancient landslide and displacement wave; the exact date and the game’s 60-km impact radius are not established. These changes do not undo disasters already applied to saved worlds.
