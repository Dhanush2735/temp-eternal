# History collection: 200–160 BCE

## Progress

Worked on **200, 190, 180, 170 and 160 BCE** using English Wikipedia year pages, selected linked articles and Smithsonian volcano records. This is partial work, not complete collection.

**Total: 294 years partly worked; 0 years fully collected.** The years between these ten-year steps still need their own work.

## Changes in the game files

- Updated **25 existing records**, preserving their IDs. Corrected the years of several battles, including Elasa and Myonessus; fixed Baideng's location from Chang'an to the Datong area and the Eurymedon naval battle's location from Iraq to off Side. These supported battles keep their military effects.
- Avoided a duplicate Eurymedon effect. Other claims, including the founding date of Tiwanaku, early Philippine barangays, and some Bactrian and Parthian chronologies, were changed to non-damaging notes where an exact date or location was not supported.
- Moved **24 volcanic eruption records** from the damaging hazard pack into historical notes. Smithsonian describes each eruption as confirmed but gives an uncertain estimated date; that does not establish a damaging event in the exact year selected by the game. Their preserved game-year slots are references, not proven dates.
- Added **one cultural note** for the Winged Victory of Samothrace, with its date marked uncertain.
- Collected **13 possible people leads** for future R3 VIP research, including Apollonia Senmothis, Hipparchus, Judas Maccabeus and Emperor Wen. They are research notes, **not spawnable VIPs**; missing details were not invented. VIP behaviour and alternate-timeline features are not implemented by this batch.
