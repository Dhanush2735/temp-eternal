Two cross-pack hazard moves (`reviews/339_290_bce_hazards_2026-10.json`, canonical SHA-256 `59b9836589b722517ac4e4cc417d05ed495913293b49182bff05dccc65bc404b`): the 326 BCE Kutch earthquake and tsunami rows were moved from the always-loaded damaging hazard pack into regional notes. NOAA links its two records to the **same proposed sequence**, not two independent confirmations. Its tsunami catalog records opposing interpretations: one analysis reads an underlying account as tides, while other researchers argue a tsunami remains possible. The event's exact location, date, magnitude and mechanism are not established. The coarse catalog point is not a verified epicenter; moving these rows does not prove no earthquake or tsunami occurred. The previously reviewed 330 BCE Aegean earthquake and 300 BCE Vulcano eruption remain active, with their inherited footprint estimates unverified.
