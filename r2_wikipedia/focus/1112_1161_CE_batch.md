# R2 preliminary pass: 1112–1161 CE

**Status: partial, not fully collected.** All 50 distinct annual Wikipedia titles and their raw revisions were archived. A scan found 822 indexed rows starting in the interval, including 66 hazards; it excludes earlier-starting events overlapping these years. Only selected rows and linked articles were reviewed. Raw wikitext does not expand transclusions; most linked articles, references and indexed rows remain unexamined.

## Indexed gameplay changes

Applied **25 guarded updates across six indexed packs**, retaining IDs:

- Corrected a 1124 Aleppo governor entry to **1024**: Thu'ban ibn Muhammad served under a caliph who died in 1036. Corrected a 1150 White Ship entry to the actual **1120** sinking and qualified two duplicate White Ship notes.
- Corrected the 1124–1125 siege of Aleppo: it was lifted, not captured, and its prior point was in northern Israel. Corrected locations for the Concordat of Worms, the Zamora recognition, the Wallingford/Winchester settlement, the Wendish Crusade, a Bordeaux marriage and the Westminster coronation. City and regional coordinates are proxies, not verified activity footprints.
- Distinguished Suryavarman II's 1113 enthronement from the formal coronation cited in 1119; Angkor Wat was constructed over decades. The 1122 and 1150 rows are approximate context, not exact start or completion claims.
- Moved a Khamag Mongol context point from the North Caucasus to a broad Mongolian regional reference and qualified chronology: the linked article itself flags citation problems. Corrected Kaifeng and Hangzhou references for Song events; retained the Didgori and Caishi battle effects with uncertainty notes.
- Changed the 1130 Chaco drought from a single-year 400 km disruption to a contextual note, and qualified the 1140 “collapse” claim: Chacoan changes and migration took time and descendant Pueblo communities continue.
- Corrected a NOAA death **amount order** incorrectly written as four deaths in the 1114 earthquake record. Removed the 230,000-casualty certainty from the 1138 Aleppo earthquake, which Wikipedia and NOAA say is likely conflated with other earthquakes. Both triggers remain provisionally active with unvalidated radii.

Moved **24 hazard entries** from the damaging hazard pack to indexed, non-damaging notes: **22 volcanological dates or eruptions that GVP marks approximate, uncertain, or in-or-after**, and two 1114 earthquake/sea-wave reports with uncertain source, location or overlap. Smithsonian date methods and ranges are recorded per entry; overlapping uncertainty ranges do not prove separate catalogue entries are the same eruption. NOAA marks the 1114 potential tsunami's validity as 2 and describes uncertainty about both the source event and wave.

**42 hazards from the scan remain in the hazard pack**, including the two context-corrected triggers; many have not been individually reviewed. A separate Aleppo earthquake row in the world pack still carries a lockdown effect alongside the hazard trigger and needs duplicate/effect review; the 1157 Hama fatality figures also vary by source. Retained effects and radii are not verified reconstructions. Changing a hazard into a note does not claim the event never occurred.
