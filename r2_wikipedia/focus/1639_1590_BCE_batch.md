## Indexed game JSON

**17 existing records were qualified or enriched, one context note was added, and two uncertain-date eruptions were moved out of the destructive hazard pack.** Existing record IDs and stored time slots were preserved. The new note uses an approximate reference interval, not a confirmed lifespan or dated daily presence.

- Babylonian succession records for Samsu-Ditana and Assyrian succession records for Lullaya, Shu-Ninua and Sharma-Adad II are qualified. Duplicates were retained rather than merged because identity and save effects require further review. Three inherited Assyrian points were corrected to Assur as regional references, not proven succession sites.
- Two overlapping Aleppo attack claims are now qualified notes. The new Yamhad note adds Hammurabi III, his possible relationship to Yarim-Lim III, and disputed chronology. The linked [Sarra-El article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarra-El) says whether Sarra-El was king and who restored Aleppo are debated; the city did not simply disappear.
- Two Egyptian dynasty and Hyksos-transition records are qualified rather than treated as exact founding or invasion dates. The Venus omen note distinguishes its uncertain earlier tradition from the [British Museum's K.160](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_K-160), a Neo-Assyrian copy excavated at Nineveh, not a 1609 BCE original found at Babylon.
- A duplicate 1595 Babylon raid record no longer triggers military presence, and a generic war note's inherited Anatolian map pin was corrected to Babylon as a regional reference. The 1595 date is one middle-chronology estimate; alternative chronologies differ. Existing related raid records remain and may overlap.
- A 1627 tree-ring climate record and a 1618 Chinese frost/famine claim are non-disruptive notes. [Pearson et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgac048) attributes a 1628 BCE ice-core signal to Aniakchak II; this does not establish Thera as the cause of every climate effect. The Chinese tradition's precise wording, dating and volcanic attribution need further verification.

### Volcanic hazards

The [GVP Ata](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=282070) and [St. Helens](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=321050) profiles list confirmed eruptions at **1610 BCE (?)**, using tephrochronology and calibrated radiocarbon respectively. A question mark is not a numerical uncertainty range. Both inherited records were moved to indexed non-disruptive history notes; this does not deny eruptions or damage. Their nominal dates, coordinates and radii do not establish exact dates, vents or damage footprints.
