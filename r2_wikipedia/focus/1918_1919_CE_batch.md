# R2 preliminary continuation: 1918–1919 CE

**Status: two years partly worked; neither is fully collected.** The English Wikipedia year titles and raw pinned revisions are archived in `year_titles_1918_1918_CE.json`, `year_wikitext_1918_1918_CE.json.xz` and the corresponding `1919_1919` files. Raw wikitext does not expand transclusions. Only selected linked articles were inspected; the years and their linked histories remain far from exhaustive.

Pre-update inventories found **435 indexed starts in 1918**, including **66 hazards**, and **330 indexed starts in 1919**, including **71 hazards**. These counts exclude earlier events overlapping the years. The hazard rows were not reviewed or moved.

## Indexed gameplay updates

Nine existing records were corrected with full before-images, pinned sources and stable IDs. Changes remain subject to the game engine's existing year-level representation.

**1918 — four updates:**
- Moved the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk signing from an unrelated Russian-region coordinate to a Brest **city proxy**. Its political effect is retained. [Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Brest-Litovsk), [Brest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brest,_Belarus).
- Moved the Armistice of Mudros to a Moudros **town proxy** and removed the unverified assertion that the armistice itself granted Yemen independence; this is not a determination about Yemeni statehood. The signing was aboard a ship in the harbor. [Armistice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armistice_of_Mudros), [Moudros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moudros).
- Referenced the Villa Giusti armistice at its signing-site coordinates rather than an unrelated regional point. A separate signing record overlaps; Wikipedia flags insufficient inline citations in the armistice article, so further verification is desirable. [Armistice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armistice_of_Villa_Giusti), [Villa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Giusti).
- Qualified the Haskell County influenza record: an early outbreak is a proposed connection, not a proven pandemic origin. Its existing disruption effect remains pending gameplay review. [1918 flu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_flu), [Haskell County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haskell_County,_Kansas).

**1919 — five updates:**
- Moved the US Senate's Versailles ratification vote from France to a US Capitol **building proxy**. Two Versailles summaries now distinguish the 1919 signing from the treaty entering force in 1920; they remain overlapping records. [Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Versailles), [Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol).
- Moved two Irish War of Independence summaries from England to the **Soloheadbeg starting-event proxy** in Ireland. This is one starting point, not the whole war's geography; the summaries overlap and retain their earlier effects. [War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_War_of_Independence), [Soloheadbeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soloheadbeg_ambush).
