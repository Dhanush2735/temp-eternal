# 700–660 BCE: five partly researched years

**Focus years:** 700, 690, 680, 670 and 660 BCE. Intervening years are still waiting. This is a partial collection, not an exhaustive reading of Wikipedia or a complete history of these years.

## What changed in the game data

The indexed history JSON packs were replaced with updated versions: **25 existing records corrected, 31 records moved, and four contextual records added**. Their original IDs and before-images are retained in the reversible manifest. Existing political gameplay effects were retained for the documented 700 Babylonian campaign and succession and the 681 Zhou accession; their pins are qualified regional references. Three city sieges retain active military effects but now occur in 701 BCE, not 700.

- **Babylon and China:** Corrected the Babylonian succession and campaign descriptions and locations; distinguished King Xi of Zhou's accession in 681 from his reign in 680. The regional map points do not prove a ceremony or person's exact position.
- **Places and culture:** Placed the Great Temple at Yeha rather than the previous Ethiopian country-centre pin, while retaining the approximate date. Qualified broad South Asian urbanisation and the relationship between Villanovan and Etruscan culture.
- **Assyria and Egypt:** Moved the 701 Jerusalem, Lachish and Azekah siege records to the regional Wikipedia pack with **military effects preserved** and corrected years. The separate Jerusalem and campaign context can repeat them; the outcome and specifics are not all agreed. Corrected the date of Esarhaddon's capture of Memphis from 670 to 671, and removed the impossible implication that Ashurbanipal led an Egyptian campaign in 671, before he became king. Separate Esarhaddon records may repeat.
- **Legend and natural evidence:** Marked Jimmu and Macha as legendary traditions, not verified political history. Qualified the Kaali impact's disputed date; the impact is real but the 660–680 dating is not established. Moved the former c.660 solar-isotope reference to a 664 BCE slot, with 664–663 and possible cross-dating uncertainty described in its evidence. The Greenland point is a sampling reference, not the event's location.
- **Hazards and wars:** Removed 20 uncertain-date eruption entries from the active disaster pack and kept them as non-disruptive regional notes. Smithsonian's Global Volcanism Program reports eruptive periods, but the reported date uncertainties do not justify automatic damage in one exact year. Five long or uncertain war entries and three other uncertain battle entries were also converted to notes; this does not prove no conflict occurred. Nearby estimates or entries can overlap.
- **New context:** Added caveated notes for King Xi, Taharqa, early Demotic writing and Hesiod. These are reference slots, not verified actions on those exact dates.

## People for future VIP research

Collected **13 research-only leads** in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_700_660_bce.json`. They include Sennacherib, Bel-ibni, Aššur-nādin-šumi, Taharqa, Esarhaddon, Ashurbanipal, Psamtik I, Hesiod and the legendary Jimmu and Macha. Some repeat earlier batches. None was added as a playable VIP; missing dates, locations and behavior have not been invented. Historical VIP behavior and player-triggered alternate timelines are not implemented by this batch.

## Evidence and verification

- Manifest: `r2_wikipedia/reviews/700_660_bce_context_2026-10.json.gz`; canonical SHA-256 `1af23652d49411a10f2a425b1be045cb1c9dcd490a38ac96a5a549d5f56ace79`.
- Reversible guarded installer: `r2_wikipedia/apply_700_660_context.py`. It validates exact before-images and indexed IDs; writes are atomic per pack, **not** across packs.
- **12 installer tests** passed; staged and installed 60-record headless Gazetteer/NewsIndex/Disasters probes passed, as did the previous batch's 55-record regression. **624 grouped Python tests** passed. All 60 installed after-images were checked against every indexed pack.
- An inclusive scan found no remaining entries in the active hazards or wars packs overlapping the five selected years. This is only a pack scan, not a complete gameplay audit. Other unreviewed records and duplicates may remain.
- 36 Wikipedia article title/revision pairs were checked against the Wikipedia API; selected linked articles were read manually. Each revised record cites its evidence in the manifest and its indexed JSON entry. The crawler was not run because of the workspace storage guard; linked articles have not been recursively exhausted.
- **No full Forge/Minecraft or saved-world test** was run. Existing save effects, suppression and spawned entities can differ; moved records preserve IDs but may behave differently in existing saves. Gazetteer uses exclusive end years; NewsIndex converts the three Wikipedia packs to inclusive end years. Moving a war/world/Wikidata row can therefore change how its end year appears as news. Three moved siege rows use −701..−700 in the Wikipedia pack, yielding gameplay and source-event news for 701 in the headless probe; the normal news-after-effect still applies.
- Known previous issue: five locked 950–910 evidence notes incorrectly describe Wikipedia-pack end years in NewsIndex. The prior report flags this; a separate guarded metadata correction is still needed.
