# 2300 to 2260 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2300, 2290, 2280, 2270 and 2260 BCE**. These years are **not fully collected**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **three entries** and added **five** in the indexed game history files:

- **Central Europe:** corrected the Únětice entry to describe an archaeological culture, not a state founded in one exact year.
- **Egypt:** corrected the location of the Faiyum waterway reference. Sources disagree on when the canal was enlarged, so the entry no longer claims it was created in 2300 BCE.
- **South Asia and Peru:** extended approximate references to Mature Harappan society and Caral. These describe periods, not events proven to have happened every year.
- **Sudan:** added an Early Kerma cultural reference.
- **Mesopotamia:** added approximate reign references for Rimush and Manishtushu and a reference to Enheduanna’s inscribed disk from Ur. The kings’ dates and order vary between sources.

Changing old dates and locations may affect saved games. Two old Akkadian war entries and twelve hazard entries remain unreviewed. Period notes may appear repeatedly in game news; that does not prove an event occurred every year.

## People for later

I saved research leads for **Rimush, Manishtushu, Naram-Sin and Shu-Enlil**. They are **not active VIP NPCs**. The leads preserve uncertainty and do not invent birth or death locations.
