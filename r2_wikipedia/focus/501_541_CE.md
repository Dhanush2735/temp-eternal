Coordinates labeled city, capital or region are gameplay references; they do not prove precise event locations or footprints. Some one-year slots represent uncertain dates rather than exact claims. World, war and Wikipedia packs have different end-boundary behavior across readers, and moves or effect changes may affect saved games. NewsIndex reads event kinds independently of effects; a note does not guarantee the record is invisible in news.
