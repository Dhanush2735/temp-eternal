## Gameplay JSON

Thirteen existing indexed records were corrected with stable IDs:

- Changed the Albanian Republic's start from 1924 to 1925, and corrected the Bauhaus move from Berlin to a Dessau city proxy, distinguishing the 1925 move from construction of the Gropius-designed building in 1925–1926.
- Moved a Portuguese Carmona/coup summary from Cameroon to a Lisbon capital proxy; moved two overlapping Shanghai massacre summaries to a Shanghai city proxy. The latter include a separate Autumn Harvest Uprising, which did not happen in Shanghai; the city point represents only the massacre. Overlapping summaries remain.
- Replaced the 1927 Iraq independence claim with the 1927 agreement concerning future independence, distinguishing it from the end of the British mandate in 1932. The existing 1932 Iraq independence record `wpy_9f040e5f2a` was found and left unchanged; the 1927 Baghdad coordinate is a capital proxy, not a verified signing site. The [Springer history](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1057/9780230270602_44) identifies a 14 December 1927 agreement; [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/globalconnections/mideast/timeline/text/time3.html) dates formal mandate termination to 1932. The annual Wikipedia page's contrary 1927 formulation was not treated as conclusive.
- Relocated the 1928 Terengganu resistance associated with Haji Abdul Rahman Limbong to a Kuala Berang proxy and the 1929–1930 Women's War to a Bende district proxy in southeastern Nigeria, not England or the Arctic. Neither proxy covers the whole movement. The latter is classified as political protest, while retaining its note effect; British repression caused deaths.
- Corrected three Salt March records: Sabarmati Ashram as the departure proxy and Dandi as the law-breaking proxy. The march began 12 March 1930 and Gandhi broke the law on 6 April; the approximately 240-mile route was not represented as a single exact site. Existing overlapping start summaries were not deleted.
- Relocated the Musha Incident to a Ren'ai township proxy in Taiwan and the Saya San rebellion to a Tharrawaddy regional proxy in Myanmar, not Japan or the Arctic. The Saya San article contains conflicting death dates, so none were inferred.

All radii are gameplay approximations, not verified event boundaries. Dates in the game remain year-level; no exact day was fabricated. The sources include Wikipedia articles that request additional citations, notably those on the Albanian Republic, Limbong and the Dessau building. Apart from the Women's War kind correction, existing kinds and effects were retained. None of the 749 inventoried hazards were individually reviewed or moved.
