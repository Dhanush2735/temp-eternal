# 2890 BCE — partial ten-year step

## History improved in the game

I corrected **three existing entries** in the indexed game JSON:

- **Egypt:** the change from Qa’a’s First Dynasty to Hotepsekhemwy’s Second Dynasty is now marked as **around 2890 BCE**, with the date and succession uncertain.
- **Akkadian names:** I removed the unsupported 2890 date. A research-based entry now refers to early written words and names around **2600–2500 BCE**; that is not necessarily the first date anyone spoke Akkadian.
- **Akkadian texts:** I corrected another entry to distinguish later texts written fully in Akkadian from earlier words and names.

The dates used by the game are reference points, not proof that each development happened in one exact year. The older IDs were kept, but changes to them have not been tested in an existing saved world.

Sources include Wikipedia’s pages on [Qa’a](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Qa%27a&oldid=1381183472), [Hotepsekhemwy](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hotepsekhemwy&oldid=1361247478), and [Akkadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Akkadian_language&oldid=1378219700), as well as [Oxford research on early Akkadian writing](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:332bae64-5f87-4cf8-8ebd-649dd15fa3d5).
