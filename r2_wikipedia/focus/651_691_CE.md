Decade pages reuse year-page content and are not independent confirmation. Wikipedia pages were current when consulted and generally not revision-pinned.

## Indexed game JSON

Installed **38 updates and three additions** across three indexed packs. Existing IDs and start/end fields were preserved. The additions are Perctarit's return in 671, a cultural note on the Twelfth Council of Toledo in 681, and Ilterish's 682 khaganate. The 682 addition and changes to nearby 650, 682 and 683 records address chronological conflicts or likely duplicates discovered in this batch. A targeted name scan found no other matching additions; it cannot prove semantic uniqueness.

The before/after manifest is `reviews/651_691_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `cfb9fe0b19ca3152d5eeb838db5206f6bb22e72ff95f6e45bef3e463d6dfcf24`. `apply_651_691_ce_context.py` stages, applies or rolls back after exact before/after checks. Writes are atomic per pack, not across all packs. Source URLs and reasons are in each after-image.

- **651:** Yazdegerd III's death moved from central Iran to a Merv reference; the competing 650 duplicate is a note. The Tang embassy account distinguishes the reported embassy from the debated attribution to Sa’d. Oswine's death is not a battle, and its Gilling location is disputed. Œthelwald was Oswiu's nephew, not necessarily Oswine's. Hôtel-Dieu's founding in 651 is a tradition rather than an unqualified record. Balthild's marriage and Khorasan campaign dating are qualified.
- **661:** The Posentesbyrg battlefield's site, opponents and victor differ in linked accounts, so the point is a regional note. Tenji's formal accession was 668, after governing from 661; his entry and Saimei's Kyushu journey no longer point to Korea or call travel to Kyushu an invasion. Moved Ali's assassination reference from Sinai to Kufa; Hasan's subsequent agreement is not asserted to have happened there. Silla's succession point now references Gyeongju. Corbie and Æthelwealh's baptism/gift are contextual notes.
- **671:** The Two Rivers battle site is unknown. Silla's former-Baekje event now points to the Sabi area rather than Gyeongju. Yijing's journey and the Ōtsu water clock are cultural notes; the journey continued beyond 671. The 683 Srivijaya founding claim conflicts with Yijing's 671 visit and was changed to a note. The eclipse point is now NASA's calculated location of greatest eclipse, not Washington DC; it does **not** represent the full eclipse track or visibility. Perctarit's return is added using Pavia only as a royal-capital reference.
- **681:** Chrysopolis lies across the Bosporus, not the Hellespont; the revolt entry is a note while the linked suppression remains active. Bulgar recognition is retained in one 681 political record; a duplicate and the incorrect 650 claim are notes. Ilterish's 681 founding entry is a note and the 682 account is added with a broad regional reference. Jarrow's 681/682 reports conflict with the abbey article's 684–685 dating and both are notes; the Selsey site was moved from York. The Asuka code began compilation in 681 and was promulgated in 689. Agatho's death is no longer treated as proof of a plague disaster. The Constantinople council point was corrected, and a separate Twelfth Council of Toledo note was added.
- **691:** Clovis IV's accession is qualified as 690 or 691, with Compiègne only a court reference. The Dome of the Rock dates to about 691–692 and is among the earliest **surviving** Islamic monuments, not the first ever. The categorical 691 Buddhism state-religion claim needs more evidence. The existing Battle of Maskin is in Iraq and remains active; its linked article places subsequent Hejaz recovery in 692.

Coordinates labeled as site, city, capital or region are gameplay references, not proof of exact event sites or territorial footprints. Different Java readers use different end-boundary conventions; notes may still appear in news and a `note` field does not guarantee every reader ignores an entry. Saved-world behavior and the effect of moved records have not been tested.
