# 300–260 BCE: partial collection and game-data update

**Focus years:** 300, 290, 280, 270 and 260 BCE. These are five ten-year steps; the years between them still need work. None of these five years is fully collected.

## What changed in the project

The indexed game-history packs now contain **18 corrected records, 24 moved records and two new records**. All 42 existing records kept their IDs.

- Corrected the years of Ipsus (301 BCE), Corupedium (281), Agrigentum (262), and Heraclea (280). Extended the Changping campaign to 262–260 BCE. The ancient battlefield positions and casualty figures are not certain. Corrected the broad Third Samnite War marker to the article's 298–290 range; its large game radius is an approximation, not proof of fighting at its center every year.
- Added active naval battles at **Lipari and Mylae in 260 BCE**. Their coordinates are approximate harbor/coastal references. Name and Wikidata-ID searches found no matching existing indexed entries, but this does not prove semantic uniqueness.
- Corrected the Segesta relief marker from Rome to Sicily. Corrected the Thermae battle to 259 BCE and to the Termini Imerese area, rather than Mylae. The removed Macella part of the Segesta entry needs separate location review.
- Made the Pella Stag Hunt mosaic a cultural note, not a political event; its date and the interpretation of Gnosis's signature are uncertain. Qualified the Colossus location, the Kalinga War date and battlefield, and Callimachus's alleged appointment as chief librarian. The Kalinga war now has two non-damaging references, not active war effects.
- Corrected the Berenice entry: the linked port article gives foundation by Ptolemy II in 275 BCE, not by Ptolemy I in 290. The year-page proclamation claim remains a qualified note at Alexandria as regional context. Two diplomatic claims were moved from erroneous Iraq coordinates to a broad Macedonian reference and made notes; this does not identify signing sites. Pyrrhus's campaign was moved from Tuscany to southern Italian context without asserting an exact landing or motive.
- Moved **24 uncertain-dated volcano entries** out of the damaging disaster pack into non-damaging notes. Smithsonian GVP lists confirmed eruptions, but the dates carry question marks or ranges and the end dates are unknown; their one-year slots are references, not fixed-year predictions. The reported **Vulcano** eruption at 300 BCE remains active because GVP gives a reported observation and no uncertainty marker for its start; its duration and effects remain uncertain.
- Collected **15 research-only people leads**, including Euclid, Gnosis, Berenice I, Pyrrhus, Bai Qi, Lian Po, Ashoka and Hannibal Gisco. They are not spawnable VIPs. See `history/sources/wikipedia_300_260_bce.json`. Lian Po's article gives conflicting death dates; Hannibal Gisco is not Hannibal Barca, and Hamilcar of Drepanum should not be confused with Hamilcar Barca.
