# R2 preliminary pass: 1712–1761 CE

Reviewed 2026-10-31. This is a **partial pass**, not complete collection of any year or every linked article.

## Coverage

- Resolved 50 annual titles and saved their pinned raw wikitext. Transclusions are not expanded. Before updates, a scan found 2,267 indexed records starting in the interval, including 523 disaster-pack rows. This is an inventory, not a review of all records.
- The initial hazard inventory comprised 171 volcano, 168 earthquake, 85 plague, 73 tsunami, 18 famine, six epidemic and two flood records. Only selected entries were investigated.
- A selective research graph contains **50 people, 19 relationships and 50 event-role associations**, spanning 15 indexed event IDs. These are research leads, not complete biographies, proof of co-presence or spawnable NPCs.
- The title audit recognizes **4,750 distinct annual titles**: 2,989 BCE and 1,761 CE. Eleven early BCE entries remain outside that recognized inventory, although evidence exists elsewhere. Title resolutions are not completed histories. **Fully collected years established: 0.** The older count of 3,168 partly worked years is unreconciled and should not be added to the title count.

## Indexed gameplay changes

Twenty-one existing records were updated with stable IDs and source evidence. Many overlapping or wide-area records are nondisruptive notes; this does not comprehensively deduplicate them.

- Corrected Sikh confederacy reorganization references to the 1748 gathering at Amritsar rather than 1716 or 1750. Political confederacy formation was a longer process. Corrected the location of Banda Singh Bahadur's execution reference to Delhi, without asserting an uncertain prisoner count.
- Corrected three references to the 1720 Qing and Tibetan allied expedition expelling Dzungar forces from Lhasa; one had been placed in 1750, and the other locations were in Xinjiang. Lhasa is a reference for a wider expedition. Corrected the siege of Isfahan to Isfahan and avoided saying all Iran was handed over at once.
- Corrected the Tulip Period's end and Istanbul reference, and located the Patrona Halil revolt in Istanbul. Extended the First Great Awakening reference to the 1740s and corrected its oversimplified Whitefield description; Boston and Philadelphia are examples, not claims that the movement was confined there.
- Corrected the Carnatic wars' South Indian reference locations and the Second and Third War intervals; for the First War the record explicitly notes competing 1744 and 1746 start dates. Chennai is one reference point, not the full theater.
- Corrected the Jacobite rising's reference from Wales to Glenfinnan, and its duration through 1746. Corrected Asaf Jah I's 1748 death reference from Hyderabad to Burhanpur and the exclusive end year, preserving the original 1909 gazetteer quotation and source. Corrected the account and Satara reference for Shahu I's death and Rajaram II's succession.
- Corrected reference points and durations for the French and Indian War and forced expulsion of Acadians; Pittsburgh and Grand-Pré represent wider regional events. Corrected an oversimplified Plassey outcome without adding a second active battle effect. London is a reference for the 1752 British calendar reform, not its only location.

Twenty-one selected hazards were moved from the disaster pack to indexed regional notes: nine volcanic records where GVP gives a broad or uncertain date/status, and twelve tsunami records with specific NOAA-documented doubts or competing explanations. This is not a claim that the underlying observations never occurred. Some confirmed eruptions, such as Cerro Bravo and Witori, are real events but cannot safely be triggered as precisely dated hazards on the catalog's central year. NOAA reports include uncertainty over storm waves, river flooding, dates, event origin and coastal subsidence. **502** of the original 523 scanned hazards remain in the disaster pack; most have not been reviewed. Confirmed neighboring records were not moved merely because an eruption's duration is unknown or because a NOAA validity field is low.
