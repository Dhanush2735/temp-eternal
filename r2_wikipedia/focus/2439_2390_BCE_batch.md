Archaeological proximity does **not** prove the men met or were contemporaries. The displayed c.2400 slot is a reference for Ebih-Il’s statue, not an exact statue production, event or royal reign date. The statues are different objects.

The c.2400 slot and Girsu pin are references, not a dated signing or proof the two met there. The Sumerian King List’s 120-year reign figure is not installed as literal chronology. This updates the pre-existing note rather than adding a duplicate event.
