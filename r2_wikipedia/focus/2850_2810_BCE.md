# 2850 to 2810 BCE — five partial steps

These steps cover **2850, 2840, 2830, 2820 and 2810 BCE**. Wikipedia redirects all five year titles to the same century page, which does not list a separate event for any of those exact years.

## Game data improved

- **Iran:** Extended the existing Shahr-e Sukhteh note to cover an approximate archaeological period that includes all five steps. The dates are estimates, not proof of what happened in every individual year.
- **China:** Added a regional note for the Miaodigou II archaeological phase, also covering all five steps. Its classification is debated.
- **California:** Corrected an existing Methuselah tree entry at **2832 BCE** to say its age is disputed. Its map point is only a broad regional reference.
- **China:** Corrected an existing **2852 BCE** entry so it identifies the Three Sovereigns as a legend, not a verified political event.

The last two are corrections to nearby years, not extra completed year passes. The two long-period notes may appear across several years; they are not events that happened again each year.
