# 2350 to 2310 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2350, 2340, 2330, 2320 and 2310 BCE**. I followed some linked Wikipedia articles and other sources. These years are **not fully collected**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **nine entries** and added **three** in the game’s indexed history files:

- **Peru and South Asia:** extended the game’s coverage of Caral and the Mature Harappan period into these years. These are approximate period references, not separate events every year.
- **Mesopotamia and Syria:** made entries about Mari, Lugal-zage-si and Sargon more careful about uncertain dates. An existing entry now covers Sargon’s reign.
- **Egypt:** expanded the Sixth Dynasty entry and added a reference for Teti’s reign. Their dates follow one proposed timeline and may differ in other sources.
- **Ireland:** replaced an unproven claim of a Middle Eastern asteroid impact with a note about narrow Irish oak tree rings. The cause of the reduced growth is uncertain.
- **Ur:** corrected an unsupported birth year for Enheduanna, retaining an approximate reference to her priesthood instead. Her authorship of the works attributed to her is debated.

Some old entries changed their date or location, which may affect saved games. A separate Hekla eruption hazard remains at 2310 BCE, although its date is disputed. Other old volcano and war entries still need review.

## People for later

I saved **12 people as research leads**, including Sargon, Enheduanna, Teti and Iput I. They are **not active VIP NPCs**. I also recorded the Korean Dangun founding story as a **legend**, not a proven event or person.
