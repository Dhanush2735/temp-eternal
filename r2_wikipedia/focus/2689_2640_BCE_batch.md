# 2689–2640 BCE — preliminary consecutive batch

## Coverage

All 50 English Wikipedia year titles resolve to the [27th-century page](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=27th_century_BC&oldid=1371895855). This is a title check, not separate evidence for each year or a complete traversal of the linked articles. This pass followed selected sources on Egypt, Kalibangan and early Mesopotamia, and checked the catalogued volcanoes in the game's stored interval. Older broad archaeological context is not proof of activity in every year.

## Game history

* Updated `wpr_2c2d0e0c96`, the existing indexed Third Dynasty note, to name Djoser, Sekhemkhet and Sanakht while retaining its existing ID, approximate range and regional reference. Their order, identities and absolute chronology are qualified. The [dynasty article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Third_Dynasty_of_Egypt&oldid=1377187091) gives alternatives that differ by more than a century; the rulers must not be treated as simultaneously present throughout the stored interval. The association of Sanakht with Nebka is disputed.
* Added `r2_sekhemkhet_buried_pyramid_reference_c2645`, an indexed cultural note at the [Buried Pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Buried_Pyramid&oldid=1362493597) in Saqqara. Its c.2645 date is one approximate reference, not a confirmed construction or completion year. The structure is unfinished; the 20 km game radius is not its footprint. NewsIndex may also include the stored end year. The article on [Imhotep](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Imhotep&oldid=1382264995) reports a graffito at the enclosure, but his precise architectural responsibility is not established by that reference.
* Existing Kalibangan, Gilgamesh, Dholavira, Djoser pyramid and Third Dynasty notes already contain relevant qualifications; no unsupported duplicate was added. For Kalibangan, the century article's 2650 BCE mature-settlement abandonment claim conflicts with site chronology; the existing Early Harappan note does not assert that date or cause.
