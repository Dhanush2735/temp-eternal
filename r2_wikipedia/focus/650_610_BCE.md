# 650–610 BCE: five partly researched years

**Focus years:** 650, 640, 630, 620 and 610 BCE. The intervening years remain for later. Collection is partial, not complete.

## Changes made in the game

Updated indexed game history packs with **15 corrections, 28 moves and 4 context additions**. The manifest keeps original IDs and exact before/after images.

- Corrected the fall of Babylon from 651 to **648 BCE** and removed the claim that the king's suicide is certain; military effects remain. A separate siege reference is placed at approximately 650, not claimed as an exact opening date.
- Corrected the dates of the **Battle of Chengpu (632 BCE)** and **fall of Nineveh (612 BCE)**, preserving military effects. Chengpu's exact site is disputed. Harran's 610–609 BCE military record was also preserved and moved to a pack with consistent news end-year handling.
- Moved the Abdera and Carthage references from incorrect places to their actual regions. Abdera's political effect is retained; the supposed exact date of Carthage's independence is not established.
- Marked Ancus Marcius as a legendary Roman figure, qualified competing Assyrian–Elamite campaign dates, corrected the Sadyattes accession slot, and clarified Draco's law was an Athenian tradition. Both Cyrene founding references can still repeat each other.
- Moved **22 uncertain-date volcano records** from the active disaster pack into non-disruptive notes. Smithsonian GVP reports eruptive periods with question marks or date ranges, not exact-year proof of uniform game damage. Three broad war records were also moved to contextual notes. The 610 and 620 Redoubt estimates overlap; that does not prove two separately dated game impacts.
- Added caveated contexts for King Xiang of Zhou, Sappho, Anaximander and the law attributed to Draco. These are references, not proof of exact-day activities.

## People

Saved **10 research-only leads** in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_650_610_bce.json`, including Sappho, Anaximander, Necho II and legendary Ancus Marcius. None is a playable VIP; names and details can overlap earlier leads. Historical NPC behaviour and alternate timelines remain unimplemented.

- Ten installer tests passed; staged and installed 47-record headless Gazetteer/NewsIndex/Disasters probes passed, as did the preceding 60-record regression. All 47 installed after-images were checked against the indexed packs.
- An inclusive scan found no remaining hazards or war-pack entries overlapping these five selected years; it does **not** cover every effect, intervening year, related duplicate or saved state. Roman/Messenian and Teispes-related rows remain to investigate.
- The year and decade articles and selected linked articles were checked manually. Wikipedia titles/revisions in evidence were retrieved through its API; the whole graph was not crawled due to the storage guard. Different sources disagree on some dates.
