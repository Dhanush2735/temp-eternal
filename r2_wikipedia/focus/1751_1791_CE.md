# Wikipedia history pass: 1751–1791 CE

**Sampled years:** 1751, 1761, 1771, 1781 and 1791. This is a partial pass, not complete collection of any of these years. Many linked pages and intervening years remain.

## What went into the project

Updated **30 existing records** and added **two records** in four indexed game-history packs. The additions describe Lomonosov's observation of the 1761 transit of Venus and Herschel's 1781 observation of Uranus. The edits include corrected locations or descriptions for Pondicherry, Panipat, Jacinto Canek's revolt, the Moscow plague riot, the Zong massacre, Túpac Amaru II and Micaela Bastidas, Yorktown, Big Bottom, Pillnitz, Varennes and the Polish–Lithuanian constitution. Coordinates marked as proxies are approximate references, not assertions of an exact site.

Examples of source conflicts and qualifications:

- The year page calls Pennsylvania Hospital the first colonial hospital, while its linked article describes it as second after Bellevue; the edited record avoids a first-place claim.
- The 1761 Portugal year page says slavery was abolished. An [independent reference](https://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/africa-portuguese-colonies) describes a ban on transport from Africa to Portugal and continuing slavery. The linked [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavery_in_Portugal) has serious accuracy and other warnings. The corrected record does not claim universal abolition.
- The 1781 year page implies the capture of Túpac Amaru II ended the rebellion; the [linked rebellion article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebellion_of_T%C3%BApac_Amaru_II) gives a longer period through 1783. Túpac Amaru II and Micaela Bastidas were executed in Cusco, not Lima.
- The Zong massacre happened at sea; Black River, Jamaica is used only as the ship's arrival-port proxy, not as the massacre site. An indexed memorial already exists there. An exact sea location has not been established in this pass.
- The [Vrouw Maria article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrouw_Maria) disagrees with itself about the October sinking day and lacks inline citations; no day is asserted.
- The 1791 year page and [Olympe de Gouges's declaration article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_the_Rights_of_Woman_and_of_the_Female_Citizen) disagree on the publication day; only the year is used. The First Bank's linked article distinguishes February 8 House passage from February 25 enactment.
- The present San Gabriel mission location is a proxy for an institution originally founded elsewhere and later moved; it is not represented as the exact 1771 site.

All edited records and additions, prior values, source links and reasons are in `reviews/1751_1791_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`. The canonical manifest SHA-256 is `ee060fe5142bf832869e39b0f849226479c7fef2ac5d69bfb782b493d5117f18`. Installer: `apply_1751_1791_ce_context.py`; do not apply again to already-updated packs.

## People and hazards

**37 people leads** were recorded in the research-only file `history/sources/wikipedia_1751_1791_ce.json`. They are leads, not complete biographies or spawnable VIP profiles. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.

An inclusive stored-range scan found **162 hazard-pack rows** overlapping at least one sampled year. This is an inventory, not proof of activation. The rows remain **unchanged and unreviewed**, in `reviews/1751_1791_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`.

## Checks and remaining work

- Thirteen installer tests passed before and after installation. Four actual staged packs were checked against the expected transform and reversed logically. All 32 installed after-images were found exactly once across indexed packs.
- Targeted all-index name scans do not prove semantic uniqueness. Known overlapping siege, battle, massacre and decade records remain; readers can interpret stored end boundaries differently.
- The active `deccan_events.json.gz` record `gaz_dec_1761` states that Salabat Jung was dethroned in 1761. Both linked biographies instead give 1762 for the succession. It was left unchanged for further source/loader review; the 1751 year page and Salabat Jung biography also differ by a day on Muzaffar Jung's death. The Andean rebellion war record `wpw_77fd9bc118` remains wrongly located in Central America. The 1791 Constitutional Act year-page dating conflicts with its linked act page; that record remains unchanged. The 1761 Lisbon earthquake's magnitude scale and epicentre are uncertain in the linked earthquake article; hazard and other earthquake records were not edited.
- The Torghut migration article has a Wikipedia warning about suspected AI-generated text; its existing event was not enhanced from that page. Lhasa riot and New Granada revolt articles request more citations; their claims require further checking. The guessed `Cisteil` page does not exist. The 1770s overview has an essay-style warning.
- This was not a complete traversal of the Wikipedia graph, and not all year-page chunks, births, deaths or linked articles were read. No Java, Forge, Minecraft, saved-world or `/ee check` test was run. The game integrity manifest was not regenerated and the existing jar is stale; these source edits are not a newly built game release.
