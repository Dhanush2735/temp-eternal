# 1800–1760 BCE: five history steps, partly worked

**Years worked on:** 1800, 1790, 1780, 1770 and 1760 BCE.  
**Overall: 134 focus years partly worked on; 0 fully collected.** The years between these ten-year steps still need work. The full game review comes later.

## What changed in the game

The indexed game JSON now has **9 corrected records and 9 new historical notes**:

- **Mesopotamia:** corrected the existing Hammurabi and Zimri-Lim records into approximate reign notes. Added notes for Sin-Muballit, Damiq-ilishu, Shamshi-Adad I, Queen Shibtu and the Mari palace mural. Different historical chronologies give different dates.
- **India:** corrected the Adichanallur record’s location and changed its disputed 1800 BCE claim to a reference to later reported sample dates. Another older Adichanallur record remains and needs separate review. The Indus Valley transition is now described as a gradual regional process.
- **Egypt and Scandinavia:** qualified the Nile Delta settlement and Nordic Bronze Age records. The later Hyksos kingdom must not be assumed to exist in 1800 BCE.
- **Troy and Babylon:** qualified the date of Troy VI and treated Babylon’s supposed world population ranking as an uncertain estimate, not an exact political event.
- **Peru, Anatolia and Ecuador:** added bounded archaeological and cultural context for Sechin Alto, Kültepe and Valdivia. A separate early Maya-region reference explains that early settlement dates are debated.

Some records outside the five focus years were corrected because the linked evidence was relevant. Dates and map points are approximate; a long note does not prove that something happened every year. News can repeat these notes. Changed records may affect saved games.
