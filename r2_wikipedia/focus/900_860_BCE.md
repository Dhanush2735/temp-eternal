# 900, 890, 880, 870 and 860 BCE — partial collection

**Progress: 224 focus years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** These years are not complete, and intervening years remain for later. Next: 850, 840, 830, 820 and 810 BCE.

## What changed in the project

Installed **29 corrections, ten volcano-record moves and five additions** in four indexed game history packs. The corrections include all 16 pre-change non-hazard records overlapping one or more focus years under an inclusive scan and 13 nearby records. Some rows ending at a focus year are not actually active there in the Wikipedia-pack loaders, which treat ends as exclusive. Existing IDs and reversible before-images are preserved.

- **900:** Corrected the Adichanallur point to the archaeological site; reported samples span 905–696 BCE, not an exact 900 event. Anuradhapura has early settlement evidence, but a single city foundation in 900 is unproven. Removed the unsupported San Lorenzo destruction-by-migrants claim, corrected its map point to Veracruz, and qualified the gradual prominence of La Venta. Corrected La Venta's pyramid and head references from a country/museum position to the archaeological-site region; individual manufacture dates and exact locations remain uncertain. Villanovan II is dated around 800 in the linked article, not 900. The Greek Dark Ages endpoint is a periodization disagreement: decade c.900 versus linked article c.800. Nubian Kerma and the later Napatan Kushite kingdom are not simply interchangeable.
- **890:** The 890 Naples foundation claim is not established by the cited overview; the city article describes later Greek settlement. Tukulti-Ninurta II's accession is placed at 891 by the decade page but 890 in his biography. Added cautious Tukulti-Ninurta and Zhou King Yih reign context, not exact-year actions.
- **880:** Kalhu's development as Assyrian capital is linked to Ashurnasirpal II; a French Ministry of Culture account dates inauguration and banquet to 879. Existing 880 and 879 notes may repeat the same development. The Banquet Stele's figure of 69,574 guests is an inscriptional claim, not an independently verified count. Added Omri reign context; Samaria is a regional reference, not proof of personal presence in 880.
- **870:** Added Ashurnasirpal II reign context. Asa's death is given as 871 by the decade and c.870 by his biography, with biblical chronological differences. The two 872 Luxor flood entries were corrected to non-disruptive, uncertain claims, and the wrongly placed Delta pin moved to Luxor; they may duplicate each other. Nearby Chinese and Egyptian succession estimates conflict between the decade pages and biographies.
- **860:** The Urartu article describes state emergence in the mid-ninth century, not proof of a single unification act exactly in 860. Added Osorkon II reign context. For nearby 865, two Til Barsip conquest claims conflict: one attributes it to Ashurnasirpal II and another to Shalmaneser III. The linked site article and independent archaeological project give Shalmaneser III's conquest as 856; both wrong pins were corrected to the site, while the original 865 slots remain explicitly qualified and may duplicate one another.

The additions use one-year slots to make broadly dated context available; those slots and region pins do **not** assert annual events, individual locations or personal behavior.
