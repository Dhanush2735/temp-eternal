## Indexed game resources

**14 existing records corrected or qualified, one Hunefer papyrus note added, and two uncertain-date eruptions moved from destructive hazards to historical notes.** IDs and inherited lookup slots remain stable. Changes are in project resources but have not been rebuilt into the jar or tested in Minecraft.

- **Kadesh and diplomacy:** The [Battle of Kadesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kadesh) is well attested in Egyptian sources and usually placed around 1274 BCE under the low Egyptian chronology, but alternative chronology, the outcome and forces remain debated. The inherited fixed-year military effect was converted to a note; this does **not** deny the battle. The later [Egyptian–Hittite treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian%E2%80%93Hittite_peace_treaty) is separate from the battle; 1259 and 1263 records may refer to the **same agreement**, not two treaties. The treaty's surviving versions were found in Egypt and Hattusa; neither the existing point nor the archives prove its signing venue. It is among the oldest surviving peace treaties, not necessarily the earliest diplomatic treaty ever.
- **Germany:** [Tollense valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tollense_valley_battlefield) archaeological remains indicate violent conflict around 1250 BCE, but the exact year, groups and outcome are not established. The fixed-year military effect is now a note. Other 1300 and 1200 reference rows may describe the same evidence, not three battles.
- **Egypt:** Corrected the [Abu Simbel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Simbel) record's point from Sudan to the modern relocated temple site; the temples were moved in the 1960s. Nefertari's tomb and painting are at a different site. [Luxor Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxor_Temple) had building phases before and during Ramesses II rather than beginning wholly in 1279. [Pi-Ramesses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi-Ramesses) is a site reference for the Ramesside capital, not proof of a coronation location or exact completion year. Qualified campaign chronology and the Assyrian succession of [Shalmaneser I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalmaneser_I), whose biography lists alternative reign estimates.
- **Troy and traditions:** The [Troy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy) fortifications span phases; there is no definitive proof of a Greek attack at Troy VI or of a 1279 destruction. The [Exodus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exodus) entry now identifies 1263 BCE as a proposed traditional date, not an independently established event. The connection of a catalogued eclipse to [Heracles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heracles)' legendary birth remains speculation, not historical evidence.
- **Hunefer:** Added an indexed note for his [funerary papyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunefer). The decade suggests c.1285 BCE, while linked pages give c.1275; the [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA9901-3) identifies the 19th Dynasty and findspot only as Egypt. The broad Egypt pin is not a verified workshop, ancient discovery place or the modern London museum. Hunefer is a documented scribe; the represented gods are not historical VIPs. The museum lists his wife Nasha and notes that Hunefer himself writing the papyrus is only a possibility.

Inherited coordinates and radii are lookup references, not proof of event footprints or individual presence. Other duplicate and unreviewed records remain. Earlier Bourewa, Shang, San Lorenzo, Ugarit, Mycenae and other context remains in the resources; this batch does not certify it as comprehensive.

## Volcano evidence

The GVP entries were individually matched; primary scientific studies were not independently inspected. Moving to notes avoids assuming fixed-year damage, but does not deny eruptions or effects. GVP's “confirmed eruption” is not an exact date or footprint.

- [Aguilera](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=358062), Chile: **1253 BCE ±126**, calibrated radiocarbon, A1 tephra, VEI 5. The GVP table separately labels its VEI row ±123 and quotes Stern 2008's estimate as ±136; those distinct figures are not interchangeable.
- [Nevado del Ruiz](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=351020), Colombia: **1245 BCE ±150**, uncalibrated radiocarbon. Its proposed ENE flank/Alto la Piramide location is marked uncertain.
