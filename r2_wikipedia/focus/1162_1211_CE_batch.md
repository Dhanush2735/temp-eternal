# 1162–1211 CE — preliminary R2 pass

**Status: partial, not 50 completed histories.** Archived 50 distinct annual page titles and raw pinned revisions; templates/transclusions were not expanded. Scanned 848 indexed records starting in the interval, including 82 hazards; records that started earlier and overlapped the interval were excluded. Most rows and linked articles remain unchecked.

## Changes in active indexed game data

- Staged and applied **21 guarded updates** in five indexed packs, preserving IDs and before-images. Examples: Windsor treaty moved from Normandy to a Windsor reference; the Peace of Constance's date and location corrected; the Fourth Crusade's 1202 Zara and 1204 Constantinople events separated; the duplicate Tarain military effect neutralized; Shawar described as vizier rather than caliph; Temüjin's birth and Natchez construction remain approximate. Political or military effects are replaced with notes where the original certainty, footprint or duplication was not supported.
- The Grand Village's construction is attributed to prehistoric Plaquemine builders around 1200; historic Natchez use is much later. The Pandyan conflict is represented as a changing, multi-party war, not an assumed personal invasion by the Sri Lankan king. Bakhtiyar Khalji's eastern campaigns and associated religious changes are not treated as an instantaneous 1204 event.
- **22 hazards moved to indexed non-damaging notes:** 17 uncertain-dated geological eruptions, two overlapping 1202 earthquake records, one duplicate 1170 Dutch flood located in southern Germany, one 1196 Dutch flood wrongly located in southern Germany, and one uncertain 1172 wave linked to an existing quake. A separate 1202 earthquake effect remains; NOAA says the 1.1 million estimate included famine and epidemic, while even 30,000 is uncertain. This does not validate the retained effect's radius or severity. **60 scanned hazards were not moved**, most not reviewed; this is not a complete hazard audit.
- GVP confirmed eruptions are not treated as exact-year proof: examples include Machín 1180 ±150, Northern East Pacific Rise 1200 ±300, and Kie Matubu in or before 1210 ±26. Marine and regional notes are not physical simulations.

## People research for future R3

`eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1162_1211_ce_people_links.json` contains **44 people, 25 relationships and 50 event-role links across 15 existing event IDs**. It includes multiple opposing or allied figures for the Pandyan war, Tarain battles, Hattin, the Fourth Crusade, the Genpei War and Mongol–Jin campaigns. It is research-only: roles do not prove everyone was present together or establish complete biographies. **These records do not make anyone naturally spawnable**, and historical VIP behavior/branching is not implemented.

Canonical SHA-256 respectively `9f22d6f2b083c9b93cc7bde1218b00807f589d6761a0b4a98c2632fc3722abec` and `70a327a5b97225b44e8a9347e3918265f3fa7613b3584da9dff6aa006389429a`. - **30 tests passed:** 11 batch tests plus 19 resolver/scope/pipeline tests. They include in-memory reverse/reapply of this and the preceding batch in dependency order, manifest tamper detection, after-image uniqueness, title archiving and graph structure. Compression maps `reviews/complex_manifest_compression_1162_2026-10.json` and `reviews/complex_manifest_compression_extra_1162_2026-10.json` record original-byte hashes and checks. Both compression scripts stopped on later candidates; the maps were reconstructed and existing converted files validated. No gameplay record was deleted by this housekeeping.

## What remains

The title inventory now recognizes **4,200 distinct chronological year-page resolutions** (2,989 BCE, 1,211 CE), not completed years; another 11 early BCE resolutions are outside the recognized files. The old **3,168 partly worked years** figure remains unreconciled. **Fully collected years established: 0.** Extensive year-section, cross-link, citation, event and people review remains for all intervals. Neither a live game, loader, save, Java probe nor rebuilt jar was tested for this pass. Natural ordinary/VIP spawning behavior and alternate-timeline logic still require implementation and testing. R1 is deferred as requested. The next preliminary R2 interval is 1212–1261 CE, without implying earlier intervals are finished.
