# 1200–1160 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked:** 1200, 1190, 1180, 1170 and 1160 BCE. The years between them remain for later. None of these years is fully collected.

## Changes made to the game

The indexed game history JSON now has **20 corrected records, 19 uncertain volcano records moved to history notes, and five new notes**. Three corrections concern nearby years: 1182, 1177 and 1162 BCE. Existing IDs were kept.

- Corrected the **Pylos palace** and **Tollense battlefield** locations. The Dorian invasion explanation for Pylos is a discredited hypothesis; Tollense's exact date and participants are uncertain, and other records may describe the same battle.
- Qualified several accounts of **Hattusa**: its abandonment and subsequent destruction were not established as one exact-year event, and their causes remain debated.
- Corrected or qualified the **Olmec and San Lorenzo** records; the archaeological evidence covers more than a single year. Other records may repeat similar information.
- Qualified claims about **Nubia's independence**, **Phoenician Lisbon**, **Arameans and Chaldeans**, the **Cimmerians**, the **Srubnaya culture**, and the **end of Kassite rule** where linked articles give different or broader dates.
- Corrected the regional reference for **Ammurapi's correspondence** to Ugarit in Syria. Its date and the attackers' identities remain uncertain. The nearby Ramesses III and Marduk-statue records were also qualified.
- Added cautious notes about the emergence of **Ancestral Pueblo peoples**, the **Yajurveda**, **Tausret** and **Ramesses III**. Their displayed years are references, not verified dates of a particular action or presence.
- Moved **19 volcano records** from the active disaster pack to non-disruptive history notes. Smithsonian's Global Volcanism Program records confirmed eruptive periods, but the dates are estimates, not proof of eruptions or effects in the exact game years. Nearby entries for some volcanoes may refer to related evidence rather than separate events.
