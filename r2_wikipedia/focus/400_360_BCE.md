# 400–360 BCE: partial collection and game-data update

**Focus years:** 400, 390, 380, 370 and 360 BCE. These are five ten-year steps, not a review of every year between them. Each remains partly worked, not fully collected.

## What changed in the project

The indexed history packs now contain **23 corrected records, 23 moved records and one new record**. The original IDs were retained for the 46 existing records. The new entry is a non-damaging note about Tollund Man in Denmark, with an archaeological death range rather than an invented exact date.

- Corrected the game years for Cunaxa, Lechaeum, Leuctra and Mantinea to match their linked battle articles. The active military effect remains. Veii's long active *battle* range was narrowed to an approximate 396 BCE reference. Allia uses a single approximate 387 BCE active slot; the traditional 390 BCE dating is recorded as an alternative, not a second battle.
- Corrected the Delphi Tholos location and moved the La Venta reference to the archaeological site in Mexico. La Venta and San Lorenzo are described as approximate archaeological developments, not exact-year political changes. Qualified the Mahabharata's textual chronology and the regionally variable Jōmon/Yayoi transition.
- Qualified Egyptian succession records where Wikipedia pages disagree: Nepherites II and Nectanebo I around 380/379–378 BCE, and Nectanebo II around 360/358 BCE. Replaced Agesilaus II's Sparta death pin with a broad Cyrenaica reference and a disputed 360/59 BCE date. The Nectanebo II and Agesilaus entries are non-damaging notes, not precisely dated political effects.
- Changed 23 GVP volcano records from damaging disaster entries into historical notes. GVP describes confirmed eruptive periods, but their dates are uncertain, ranging from question-mark estimates to margins of ±210 years. This change does **not** mean the eruptions did not happen; it avoids automatically damaging the world in an unsupported exact year. The three Redoubt entries remain separate catalogue leads and may need further reconciliation.
- NOAA's catalogue lists the Rey/Eivan-e-Kay earthquake as event 19 in 400 BCE, with magnitude 7.6. Its active disaster entry is retained but labelled uncertain. The date, historical magnitude and game's 50 km radius require further review; another study mentions a Rey earthquake dated 312–280 BCE, but it has not been established that this is the same event.
- The linked Praxiteles, Xenophon, Amyrtaeus, Agesilaus II, Nectanebo II, Nepherites II, Cleombrotus I, Jason of Pherae, King An of Zhou, Tollund Man, Olympias and Buddha articles yielded **12 research-only people leads**. They are not new spawnable VIPs. Their source links and uncertainties are in `history/sources/wikipedia_400_360_bce.json`.
