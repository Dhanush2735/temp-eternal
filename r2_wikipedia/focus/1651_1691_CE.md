# Wikipedia history pass: 1651–1691 CE

**Sampled years:** 1651, 1661, 1671, 1681 and 1691. This is a partial pass, not a complete collection of these years or their linked articles.

## Changes in the game resources

Updated **27 existing records** and added **four** in five indexed history packs. The four new entries cover the succession of Guru Har Krishan, the sack of Panama, the sack of Burhanpur and the Treaty of Limerick. Existing IDs were preserved.

Examples of corrections:

- Charles II's 1661 Westminster coronation was moved from Scotland to Westminster Abbey; Kösem Sultan's assassination was moved from Sulawesi to the Topkapı Palace area.
- The execution of Murad Bakhsh was moved to Gwalior Fort; the location of Shah Shuja's death is represented by a broad Arakan capital proxy, with conflicting accounts noted.
- The 1681 Oxford Parliament, 1691 siege of Mons, and Battle of Leuze were moved to appropriate city or town proxies. Existing military records for Mons and Leuze remain separate; the edited entries are contextual notes.
- The Salher record was moved to a fort-area proxy and its disputed year noted; the related 1672 world battle record was also moved to the fort-area proxy. The 1671 year page dates the battle to 1671, whereas other articles say 1672. Both records remain.
- The Guangzhou massacre record was moved from 1651 to 1650, and to a Guangzhou proxy: the 1651 year page conflicts with the Guangzhou and Ming–Qing transition articles. The death toll is an estimate. This is a related correction outside the five sampled years.
- The alleged Three Hundred and Thirty Five Years' War is now explicitly identified as disputed and located in the Isles of Scilly. **Its existing stored end of 1712 is unsupported and remains unchanged**; the source describes a hypothetical war and a 1986 ceremonial peace, not confirmed fighting throughout a 61-year period. A separate safe range migration is still needed.
- The attempted Crown Jewels theft no longer repeats claims that Blood was too drunk to run, sentenced to death or exiled; these were not corroborated by his linked biography.

Approximate coordinates are labeled as proxies in the records; they do not prove the exact position of an event or cover an entire campaign. The 1661 siege of Fort Zeelandia already has a separate indexed battle; its stored end is 1662 and different readers may treat the boundary differently. Panama's new city-sack note overlaps the indexed battle of Mata Asnillos. New treaty and political records may affect gameplay; saved-world effects have not been tested.

The 1681 Pennsylvania charter record still has a coordinate in the sea; the 1651 San José wreck still has a Costa Rica-area point although its linked article describes southern Chile. The dodo's exact extinction year is not established by simply accepting the 1681 overview claim. Existing duplicates and incorrect positions elsewhere remain. Searches by name do not prove semantic uniqueness.
