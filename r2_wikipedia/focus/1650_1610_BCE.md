## What changed in the game JSON

I made **10 corrections**, moved **5 existing records** to more suitable history notes, and added **10 new notes**.

- **Egypt:** qualified uncertain dates for the Hyksos and Egyptian dynasties, and corrected the location of the proposed Abydos Dynasty. Its existence is still debated.
- **Greece and Switzerland:** corrected claims about Mycenae and prehistoric Lake Zurich crossings. They are not proven first appearances in 1650 BCE.
- **Thera:** the eruption happened, but its exact year is debated. The fixed-year disaster entry and three related hazard entries are now qualified notes; they do not represent separate eruptions. An Arenal eruption record was also moved to a note because its date is uncertain.
- **Hittites and Mesopotamia:** corrected a Hittite ruler record and added approximate information about rulers of Assur and Babylon. Different historians use different chronologies.
- Added regional context for **Kerma, Erlitou, Sechin Alto, Valdivia and Bronze Age Britain**. Long periods are not proof of a particular event every year.

Existing related records can still show the same story more than once. Changes may affect saved games.
