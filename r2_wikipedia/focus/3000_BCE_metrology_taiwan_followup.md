# 3000 BCE: metrology and Taiwan follow-up

This revisits one partly worked year; it does **not** complete that year or a ten-year step.

## Indexed gameplay changes

- **Mesopotamian measurements:** The [century page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30th_century_BC) asserts the shekel was introduced in 3000 BCE, but the linked [Shekel article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shekel) dates word attestation around 2150 BCE, not its invention. The record now notes uncertain dating and broader [Late Uruk measurement systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Mesopotamian_units_of_measurement), using an Uruk proxy instead of southeastern Turkey. Its ID and note effect remain; date and radius are approximate.
- **Dapenkeng culture:** Added a [Dapenkeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dapenkeng_culture) cultural note for coastal Taiwan, with a type-site-area proxy. Its 3000–2500 BCE interval is approximate and clipped to the game’s starting limit, not an origin or proof of continuous occupation. The commonly proposed link to early Austronesian speakers is not certain. The note may appear as background news; differently named overlaps remain possible.
