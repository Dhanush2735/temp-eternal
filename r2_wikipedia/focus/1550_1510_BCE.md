# 1550–1510 BCE: five history steps, partly worked

**Years worked on:** 1550, 1540, 1530, 1520 and 1510 BCE.  
**Overall: 159 focus years partly worked on; 0 fully collected.** The years between the ten-year steps still need work. The full game review comes later.

## What changed in the game JSON

I made **8 corrections**, moved **23 existing records** to history notes, and added **10 new notes** in the indexed game files.

- **Egypt:** corrected the locations and uncertain dates of several New Kingdom and dynasty-boundary records. Added a note about the Rhind mathematical papyrus and its scribe, Ahmes.
- **Babylon:** marked the transition to Kassite rule as dependent on the historical dating system used.
- **Greece:** corrected a claim that Mycenae ruled all Achaea in 1550 BCE; added a note about Mycenaean inlaid daggers.
- Added regional context for **Kerma, Erlitou, Erligang, Sechin Alto, Valdivia, early communities in the Gulf lowlands of Mexico, and Bronze Age Britain**. These are not proof of a separate event every year.
- Moved **22 volcano records** with geological dates out of the active disaster loader and into qualified historical notes. Their exact years are not established observations. One war record about Ahmose I was also moved to a note because the date and site are uncertain.

Some related entries may still repeat the same story. Changes may affect saved games.
