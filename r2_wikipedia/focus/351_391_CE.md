# 351–391 CE: partial Wikipedia research and indexed history updates

**Focus years:** 351, 361, 371, 381 and 391 CE. All five were partly worked, not fully collected. Intervening years remain for later; corrections were also made to some nearby 380, 385 and 389 records.

## Game history JSON

Updated 23 existing indexed records and added two references across `events_wikipedia.json.gz`, `events_wikipedia_regional.json.gz` and `events_wars.json.gz`. Existing IDs were retained; no records were moved between packs. Exact before/after images, additions, links, caveats and reasons are in `reviews/351_391_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `98203da7c0c66015b0666efbbecc3922b8b952e17e08450f60350a56462b2b4e`. Apply or reverse with `r2_wikipedia/apply_351_391_ce_context.py`; writes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs.

- **351:** Corrected the Jewish revolt's regional war center from southern Italy to Sepphoris and adjusted its local reference. The 90 km radius is a gameplay approximation, not a historically verified boundary; the war record remains in the war pack and its active consequences should be tested in-game. Corrected Shi Zhi's location to the modern Xingtai area and qualified the account of his killing. Changed Former Qin's foundation reference to the Chang'an region, distinguishing the 351 Heavenly King title from the 352 imperial title. The 351 sugar-processing invention claim is marked unverified, not established. Added Gallus's elevation at Sirmium as an indexed political reference.
- **361:** Moved Eastern Jin succession to the Jiankang region, Julian's sole-reign reference from Rome to Constantinople, and the physician licensing claim from England to Constantinople. City centers are proxies, not proven sites for each event. Qualified the Liberius/Julian and Saint Anthony monastery claims; the monastery article contains conflicting founding information.
- **371:** Changed the reported Gogugwon death/Baekje attack reference from southern Korea to the modern Pyongyang region, and made it a note because the exact fortress and chronology need verification. The Pyongyang Castle article gives a different year. Moved Sosurim's succession to the Gungnae region, Sirmium's frontier reference from the Danube delta to Sirmium, and the Mosella poem reference from the Rhine mouth to the Moselle region. Bagavan is a reference for a 371 conflict, not a proven location of all Roman–Sasanian fighting; the existing Battle of Bagavan row remains separate.
- **381:** Corrected Cularo/Gratianopolis to Grenoble, qualified a bundled Gratian policy claim and added the First Council of Constantinople at Hagia Irene as a note. The dedicated council article dates it 381; the year page also reports that some authorities give 383. The present church building is not asserted to be unchanged from 381.
- **391 and nearby:** Qualified the 391 Roman worship claim: the Edict of Thessalonica was issued in 380, and the linked Vesta article places extinction of the fire in the early 390s, likely 394. Corrected the bundled Symmachus claims and Gwanggaeto succession reference. Moved the 385 Serapeum reference from Italy to Alexandria and changed the questionable 389 all-temples-and-library-fire record to a note; chronology and accounts differ, and the two existing records may refer to the same developments. The 380 Rapa Nui settlement statement is now labeled disputed; the exact settlement date is not known, and later radiocarbon evidence supports much later estimates.
