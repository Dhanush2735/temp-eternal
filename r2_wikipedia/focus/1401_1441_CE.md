# Wikipedia history pass: 1401–1441 CE

**Five years partly worked:** 1401, 1411, 1421, 1431 and 1441. Many linked articles, later sections of decade pages, and intervening years still need work. None is fully collected.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

**59 existing records updated and four added across five indexed packs.** This includes related 1409 and 1419 legacy slots. Examples:

- **1401:** moved Conwy Castle's capture from Anglesey to Conwy and several Jingnan campaign entries from Shandong to their appropriate Beiping or Dezhou proxies. Added the execution of Lollard priest William Sawtrey. Clarified the Schaffhausen massacre's baseless accusation and forced confessions, and corrected Hồ succession and Malwa locations.
- **1411:** added the First Peace of Thorn; changed the unsourced 1419 Toruń claim to a conflict note without changing its ID or year range. Relocated the Ming–Kotte War from China to a Sri Lankan regional proxy, and Zheng He's return and the Malaccan court visit to Nanjing. The war is variously dated 1410 or 1411; a 1409 legacy claim is now a dated-conflict note.
- **1421:** corrected the Mamluk deposition of the child Ahmad by his regent and stepfather Tatar, at Damascus. The St. Elizabeth's flood now uses the Dordrecht area rather than a point far north and no longer claims a certain death toll. The fleet event now describes the **sixth** voyage, not the start of all the voyages in 1421. The Larabanga Mosque date remains explicitly traditional. The Franco-Scottish victory at Baugé is clarified.
- **1431:** Angkor's conventional capture date is qualified, not treated as an instant abandonment. Moved the Hussite invitation to Basel and the Treaty of Medina del Campo to its town proxy; corrected the Hexamilion location. The Po battle's duplicate summary is a note rather than another battle effect.
- **1441:** corrected Novo Brdo's location and retained its 1441 surrender as a note alongside the existing siege, to reduce duplicate military effects. Corrected Bordeaux's university entry, the Crimean Khanate's location and the overly broad claim about Mayapan. Portuguese voyages no longer claim the first Africans ever brought to Europe. Added the assassination of Ashikaga Yoshinori and Isidore of Kiev's imprisonment.

Each changed record has source links and a rationale. The review manifest preserves before- and after-images. Coordinates marked as city or regional proxies do **not** claim exact event sites. Existing IDs and stored date ranges were retained; notes can still appear as news.

## Important limits

- **Possible duplicates and date conflicts remain.** The separate 1410–1411 war and 1409 note concern the Ming–Kotte conflict. The 1419 treaty claim and 1411 treaty may be confused versions of one event, but their identity is not proven. The 1441 Luchuan event overlaps a longer campaign, and the two Portuguese captivity summaries overlap. The 1440–1441 Novo Brdo siege has different end-boundary behavior across readers; changing the 1441 surrender row to a note avoids another military effect but does not prove how it appears in game. The St. Elizabeth's flood may also overlap hazard records. No broad semantic deduplication was performed.
- Angkor's 1431 date is conventional; archaeological research indicates a more prolonged transformation, and chronicle evidence is contested. The Sarangpur campaign chronology is internally conflicting in the linked biography. Ouagadougou's specific 1441 capital claim and the 1421 reach of Cape Bojador need more verification. These are retained as qualified notes, not settled facts.
- **130 hazard records** overlap at least one sampled year under an inclusive start/end scan. They remain active, unchanged and unreviewed. Overlap alone does not prove a game reader triggers the row or that records are distinct events. See `reviews/1401_1441_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`. Earlier hazard overlaps remain.
- **52 source-linked people leads** are in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1401_1441_ce.json`. These are research leads, not completed biographies or spawnable VIPs. Natural VIP spawning, historical VIP behavior and player-triggered alternate timelines are not implemented.
- No Java/Forge, game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was performed for this batch. The previously built jar is stale and does not contain these changes. Existing in-game diagnostic failures have not been resolved.
