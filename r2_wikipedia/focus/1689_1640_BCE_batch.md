### Volcanic hazards

**Seventeen uncertain-date records** were moved from the destructive hazard pack to indexed non-disruptive notes. Sixteen were matched to individual GVP records: Craters of the Moon (1680 BCE ±150), St. Helens (1680 ?), Furnas (1670 ±1460), Katla (1670 ?), Nemrut Dagi (1662 ?), Asosan (1650 ?), Azufral (1650 ±150), Campi Flegrei (1650 ?), Hekla (1650 ±2500), Iwatesan (1650 ?), Kilauea (1650 ?), Krasheninnikov (1650 ?), Mageik (1650 ?), Sheveluch (1650 ?), Tenerife (1650 ?), and Katla again (1640 ?). Individual profile links, vent descriptions and dating methods are in the hazard manifest. GVP lists the two Katla entries separately; whether they are distinct needs further study. A question mark is not a numerical uncertainty interval.

The seventeenth record, Aniakchak, matches [NOAA event 4645](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/volcanoes/4645) and the [GVP profile](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=312090), which report 1645 BCE ±10. NOAA's search response marked the record `publish=false` while its detail response returned `publish=true`; the reason is unresolved. These sources are not assumed independent. A [2022 ice-core study](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgac048) attributes the Aniakchak II signal to 1628/1627 BCE, outside the older stated interval. The relationship between the older and newer date assignments requires further review; neither is converted into an exact destructive game event here.

These moves do not deny real eruptions or damage. Inherited nominal slots, coordinates and radii are not precise eruption dates, verified vent locations or measured damage footprints.
