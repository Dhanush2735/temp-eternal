# History collection: 51–91 CE

## Progress

Partly examined **51, 61, 71, 81 and 91 CE** using English Wikipedia and selected linked articles. The 51 CE title redirects to the 50s page; the other four are year pages. This is a limited pass, not a complete collection of the years or all linked articles. **Total: 319 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** Years between the ten-year steps remain for later.

## Changes to indexed game JSON

Updated **11 existing records** across four indexed packs, keeping their IDs:

- **Caratacus:** Cefn Carnedd is a proposed site of his last battle, not an established battlefield. Sources differ between 50 and 51 CE. Its record is now a qualified note rather than a fixed-site military effect.
- **Boudica:** three records about her final battle had different specific locations despite the site being unknown. They now use a clearly labelled regional reference, with no military-presence effect. The duplicate references remain because their IDs may be used by saved worlds; they are not three separate battles. The year is uncertain between 60 and 61 CE.
- **Stanwick and Trier:** qualified the claimed Stanwick battle because its occurrence at that site is doubted. A Trier battle record had been pinned in central France; it is now a qualified regional note because the 71 CE page conflicts with the linked 69–70 CE rebellion account.
- **Agrippa II:** corrected two entries that put the Syrian tetrarchy of Chalcis in Greece. They now use a Beqaa Valley regional marker, **not a claimed location of the ancient capital**. The linked biography dates his departure to 53 CE; the date he received the tetrarchy differs between the biography and the decade page.
- **Goguryeo and Nabataea:** corrected the traditional date of Taejo's accession to 53 CE; clarified that Rabbel II was already reigning in 71 CE rather than implying that his reign began that year.
- **Rome:** marked the date of the Arch of Titus as approximate, matching its linked article.

Some changes remove effects from records whose exact location or date is unsupported. This is **not a claim that the underlying historical events did not happen**. The marker coordinates and radii are gameplay references, not verified sites or boundaries.

Ten people leads, including Boudica, Shaqilath II, Deng Sui and Ban Chao, were saved in `history/sources/wikipedia_51_91_ce.json`. These are research notes, not spawnable VIPs. No NPC spawning or VIP behavior was implemented.

## Remaining work and limits

- Many other records in these years, including war, disaster and decade-start records, were not reviewed. A name/ID search cannot prove that all semantic duplicates were found.
- The news index treats end years for the world and Wikidata packs differently from the Wikipedia packs and the history gazetteer. The changed world/Wikidata references can therefore remain in news one extra year. This loader discrepancy was **not fixed**.
- The Uruk test's duplicate NPC UUID warnings, missing natural VIP spawning, manifest mismatches and disaster-count failures were not fixed.
- The project JSON was updated, but **no new mod jar, Java loader test, Minecraft test or saved-world test** was performed. Cross-pack writes are not transactional.
