## Game data: Uruk literary account

The indexed Wikidata-derived `q3204978` had been scheduled as a siege with a military-presence effect in the reference slot −2701 to −2700. [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Q3204978&oldid=2511581905) gives only 27th-century start and end, both without references; it does not provide the exact 2701 year. The Sumerian poem [Gilgamesh and Aga](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Gilgamesh_and_Aga&oldid=1370621357) portrays such a siege; the article says the historicity is open and notes a proposed c.2600 BCE date. The [Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/gilgamesh) says no contemporary information on Gilgamesh survives. The [ETCSL translation](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/section1/tr1811.htm) is the narrative, not proof the events happened as described.

Changed the existing record in place to a clearly uncertain `cultural`/`note`, preserving its ID, pack, inherited dates and location as an arbitrary reference. This does not assert that the siege never happened or that the poem was composed in 2701 BCE. The point and radius are not evidence for a battlefield. The `events_wikidata_more` pack is treated differently by some readers at its end boundary: NewsIndex can include −2700, so the note may appear in an additional year. This is a reference, not proof of an event in either year. Existing qualified notes on Gilgamesh and Sumerian poems were not duplicated.
