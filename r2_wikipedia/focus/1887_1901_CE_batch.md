# R2 preliminary batch: 1887–1901 CE

Reviewed 2026-11-01. **These 15 years are partly worked, not fully collected.** Their Wikipedia year-title identities and raw pinned revisions are archived in `year_titles_1887_1901_CE.json` and `year_wikitext_1887_1901_CE.json.xz`. Year-page transclusions are not expanded, and linked articles and their references were sampled, not exhaustively traversed. This continuation reaches 1901; **1902–1911 have not been newly collected in the 1862–1911 batch**. Some isolated work on later years existed previously.

## Indexed game data

A pre-update inventory found **2,780 indexed records starting in this interval**, including **572 hazards**: 230 volcanoes, 220 earthquakes, 104 tsunamis, seven famines, five plagues, four epidemics and two floods. `indexed_start_inventory_1887_1901_CE.json.xz` contains pack, ID, start and kind; it excludes earlier-starting overlapping events. Hazards were **not reviewed or moved**. Many other events and duplicate effects remain unchecked.

Applied **12 guarded before/after updates across two indexed packs**. Examples: corrected the 1893 Chicago fair and 1898 Belgian language law; corrected the date and location of the 1895 Treaty of Shimonoseki; moved the Cuban independence war reference from central Europe to a Cuban proxy; clarified that the Philippine Revolution began in 1896; distinguished Cuba's 1898 end of Spanish sovereignty from the republic in 1902; and corrected the Boxer Rebellion chronology. Regional points are reference locations, not precise boundaries. A Revolution of the Park summary no longer has a duplicate political effect, but other overlapping political records remain active. Numerous 1900 Boxer entries still need individual review.

## Storage and recovery

To make room, **116 already-compressed non-indexed research sources and 95 already-compressed research graphs** were consolidated as six solid archives, `research_people_solid_01.tar.xz` through `06`, in `focus/`. The original manifests retain raw and gzip hashes; `research_people_solid_2026-11.json` lists each group's members and archive hash. `research_source_archive.py` and `people_graph_archive.py` load prior JSON paths through `research_solid_archive.py`. Each raw JSON remains byte-identical, and all 211 original gzip files were verified as exactly reproducible in the current environment; the restore helper checks their original hashes and fails closed if a future gzip implementation cannot reproduce those bytes. No indexed gameplay pack was consolidated.

Two more recent people-graph JSON files (1812–1861 and 1862–1886) were compressed separately, with exact raw and compressed hashes in `people_graph_gzip_1862_2026-11.json`; old `.json` paths load through `people_graph_archive.py`. An initial attempt to update the large older map was refused by the guard while both originals remained; a small separate map was registered and verified before originals were removed. Recovering raw or original gzip files increases workspace use; reserve enough space first. The crawl and people SQLite databases remain losslessly parked, so normal recursive and people collectors remain paused. The storage guard was not raised; headroom remains extremely tight.
