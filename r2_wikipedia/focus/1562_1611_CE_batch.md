# R2 preliminary pass: 1562–1611 CE

Review date: 2026-10-31. This is a **partial pass**, not completion of these years or of their linked Wikipedia articles.

## Coverage

- Resolved and saved all 50 annual title revisions and their wikitext. Archived an inventory of 3,509 indexed records whose start falls in the interval; this scan is not a review of each record.
- The starting scan contained 1,433 disaster-pack records: 1,152 plague, 129 volcano, 91 earthquake, 36 tsunami, 17 famine, 7 flood and one epidemic. Only the selected records described below were reviewed or moved; the other records are not validated.
- A selective graph contains 40 people, 18 relationships and 36 event-role associations across 12 indexed event IDs. It is research only; association does not establish co-presence, complete biography, spawn dates or natural NPC spawning. Other associated people and opposing sides still need investigation.
- Worldwide annual titles recognized by the current inventory: 4,600 (2,989 BCE and 1,611 CE), **not 4,600 completed histories**. Eleven other early BCE title entries remain outside that inventory. Fully collected years established: **0**.

## Gameplay-facing changes

Thirteen guarded updates to existing indexed records retain their stable IDs. Examples:

- Corrected the Malta siege records from points near Cyprus or Istanbul to Malta; the existing world battle remains active. Corrected the Talikota decade point and converted a duplicate Deccan battle effect into a sourced note while preserving the gazetteer quotation and the world battle.
- Corrected the Safavid capital-move point from Qazvin to Isfahan; the Janszoon landfall reference from Java to the Pennefather River area of Australia; and the English and Dutch company references to London and Amsterdam. The locations are references, not proof of exact charter-signing or landing coordinates. Indigenous Australians already inhabited the continent.
- Reframed the Kashmir record as the 1585–1589 Mughal campaign, not an annexation completed in 1585. Reframed the invasions of Korea as a 1592–1598 war, rather than a one-year outcome placed in China. The broader points and remaining active war effects need independent validation.
- Changed an asserted 1585 Aleutian earthquake-and-tsunami narrative to a note because the source and link to historical accounts are disputed. Qualified the 1611 Sanriku casualty wording; estimates and whether a historical tally included horses vary.

Nineteen disaster records were moved out of the disaster pack into the indexed regional notes pack. This is necessary because the disaster loader can treat other kinds in the disaster pack as damaging. They comprise eight uncertain-dated volcanic rows, eight doubtful tsunami rows, two rows for the hypothetical 1585 Aleutian earthquake, and one badly geolocated duplicate of the 1604 Arica earthquake. The retained Arica earthquake (`hz_c8ae613de5`) and world event remain; their game effects and radii have **not** been validated. Other potential duplicates and effects remain unresolved. The starting scan's 1,414 other disaster rows remain, including all 1,152 plague rows.

GVP's *confirmed eruption* status does not imply an exact year: in particular Iskut-Unuk 1590 ± 50, Taranaki 1590 ± 40 and Savai‘i 1610 ± 200. For the eight tsunami records NOAA assigns validity 0 or 1 and its descriptions offer competing interpretations; moving them does not claim the historical observations never happened. Sources and before/after images are in the manifests.

## Outstanding limitations

Most linked pages, year content, plague and other hazards, duplicate battles, geography, game radii and side effects remain unchecked. Natural VIP spawning, historically faithful behavior, ordinary/VIP interaction, and alternate-timeline branching have not been implemented or tested in this pass. The game-wide review and Requirement 1 remain deferred. Prior 1462–1511 and 1512–1561 review layers record 2026-10-31 although they were created under a previously supplied 2026-10-30 review date; they remain hash-locked and have not been silently changed.
