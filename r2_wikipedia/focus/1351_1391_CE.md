# Wikipedia history pass: 1351–1391 CE

**Five years partly worked:** 1351, 1361, 1371, 1381 and 1391. Many linked articles, later sections of decade pages, and intervening years remain. None is fully collected.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

**42 existing records updated and two added across five indexed packs**, including the related 1350 Ayutthaya slot. Examples:

- **1351:** distinguished Firuz Shah's succession from the Samma polity in Sindh. Corrected the Goryeo and Ayutthaya locations, and added detail to the existing Statute of Labourers record. The 1350 and 1351 Ayutthaya slots refer to the same foundation but different dates; the earlier one now flags the disagreement.
- **1361:** moved the Red Turban capture of Goryeo's capital to modern Kaesong, North Korea. Corrected the locations of Westminster and Windsor records. Philip of Rouvres' death now records uncertainty over its cause; Pavia's university entry distinguishes its 1361 establishment as a studium generale from earlier teaching.
- **1371:** corrected the Westminster Parliament's king to Edward III and moved both Westminster records out of France. Corrected the 1371 Battle of Maritsa's location to the Chernomen/Ormenio area; the two existing entries may still duplicate one another. Moved the John of Gaunt and Constance marriage to an approximate Roquefort town location. Qualified the uncorroborated Java mission and removed its Sichuan pin. The playing-card reference now distinguishes early **European** evidence from earlier cards elsewhere.
- **1381:** moved the Blackheath revolt gathering to London, corrected the Treaty of Turin's location and classification, and made the duplicate year-page treaty a note rather than a second political effect. Changed the Koratty church entry to a traditional, unproven foundation date; its dedicated article says its proven existence dates to the 18th century. Clarified the Yunnan campaign and Sarbadar submission, corrected the king in the Foix entry, and added the naval Battle of Saltes Island.
- **1391:** moved the Kondurcha battle from the Caucasus to a broad Kondurcha River area proxy, corrected Zafar Khan's 1391 Gujarat appointment, moved Sophia of Lithuania's marriage to Moscow, and added Manuel II's Byzantine accession. The Iberian anti-Jewish violence entry now locates its starting point at Seville and describes the broader Castilian and Aragonese wave without asserting attacks throughout Portugal.

Each changed record includes source URLs and an explanation. The review manifest retains the exact before and after images. Coordinates marked as city, regional or river proxies do **not** claim exact event sites. Existing IDs and stored date ranges were retained.

## Important limits

- **Duplicates remain:** Gaegyeong's capture is recorded twice, and Maritsa and Kondurcha each have two battle entries. The Peasants' Revolt's broad war and decade rows overlap its Blackheath gathering, while the Turin treaty remains in two packs. Retaining IDs avoids an untested saved-world migration, but may produce repeated news or other effects. The 1350 and 1351 Ayutthaya rows are conflicting slots for the same foundation. The Ming Yunnan year summary overlaps the longer campaign.
- **Other unresolved records:** the 1371 Möng Mao succession conflicts with the linked polity's ruler chronology; it remains unchanged. The 1391 parliament's meeting location is contradictory on the year page and remains unchanged. The First War of the Guelderian Succession's two records remain pinned in France. The 1391 Constantinople siege record is a separately identified Wikidata event from the 1394–1402 siege; it was not changed merely because the latter article uses a different date. Other scanned entries and alleged sites still need review.
- **186 hazard rows** overlap at least one sampled year under an inclusive start/end scan. They remain active, unchanged and unreviewed; an overlap does not prove a particular game reader triggers a row or that the rows are distinct historical events. See `reviews/1351_1391_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`. Earlier hazard overlaps also remain.
- **51 people leads** are in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1351_1391_ce.json`. They are source-linked research leads, not completed biographies or spawnable VIPs. Natural VIP spawning, historical VIP behavior and player-triggered alternate timelines are not implemented.
- No Java/Forge, game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was performed for this batch. The previously built jar is stale and does not contain these changes. Existing in-game diagnostic failures have not been resolved.
