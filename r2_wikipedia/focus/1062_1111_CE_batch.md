# R2 preliminary pass: 1062–1111 CE

**Status: partial.** Fifty distinct annual Wikipedia pages were resolved and their raw revisions archived. A start-year scan found 774 indexed rows, including 69 hazards; it excludes events starting earlier but overlapping this interval. Only selected rows and linked articles were reviewed. Raw wikitext does not expand transclusions; most linked articles and references remain unexamined. No year is established as fully collected.

## Indexed gameplay changes

Applied **23 guarded updates across seven indexed packs**, preserving event IDs. Among them:

- Corrected Canossa from Germany to Canossa Castle in Italy, the Stuhna battle reference from Belarus to near Trypillia, and Ertsukhi from Iran to a **regional** proxy in Georgia, not a proven battlefield point.
- Replaced four overlapping, definite Ghana/Almoravid conquest and sack assertions with qualified, non-damaging notes: historians dispute the traditional conquest narrative. Koumbi Saleh is a site reference, not proof of the alleged siege.
- Qualified the dates of Oxford teaching, Bologna's origins, the Venetian Arsenal and Gelati; the existing Song–Đại Việt war's 1078 end is an exclusive endpoint for a war ending in 1077. Hanoi is only a partial geographical proxy.
- Kept existing battle effects at Hastings, Stamford Bridge, Manzikert, Ani and Jerusalem with explicit historical and footprint caveats. NOAA's Guangdong and Hebei casualty *amount order* 3 is a category, not three deaths; corrected the names while retaining the hazard triggers provisionally.
- Qualified the 1068 Near East casualty claim, preserving one provisional earthquake trigger while moving the overlapping NOAA earthquake row and a contested sea-wave row to notes. NOAA describes different March and May accounts and flags the tsunami's location and effects; the remaining 150 km disruption is not validated.

Moved **17 hazard rows** from the damaging hazard pack to indexed non-damaging notes: 15 eruptions with approximate or uncertain dates, plus the two 1068 earthquake/sea-wave records above. Smithsonian GVP date ranges and methods are recorded per entry. The Sunset Crater catalogue estimate differs from the later range described in GVP's summary. These are not claims that the eruptions or historical earthquakes did not occur; the fixed-year game disruptions and footprints are unsupported. **52 hazards remain in the hazard pack** from this start-year scan, including the three context-corrected rows; many remain unreviewed. The 1099 flood record's Bavaria-area coordinate appears inconsistent with its North Sea description and remains for review. Spatial proxies, remaining military effects and reader endpoint behavior have not been validated in game.
