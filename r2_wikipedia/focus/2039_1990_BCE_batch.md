Five of these 50 years—2030, 2020, 2010, 2000 and 1990 BCE—were already counted in earlier endpoint work, so the batch adds 45. Every preceding batch remains incomplete.

## Indexed game data

- Corrected the Ur fall reference from a location in modern Iran to the existing Ur point in Iraq, changed the destructive one-year siege into a non-disruptive note, and qualified the date: approximately 2004 BCE is one chronology, while the [Ibbi-Sin article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ibbi-Sin&oldid=1363803359) also gives 1940 BCE. The separate war note was similarly relocated and qualified, but retained under its existing ID; it may duplicate the event note. The date is a reference slot, not a proven exact year.
- Replaced an unqualified Ethiopia-to-Kenya migration and political action with a contextual note. The [Pastoral Neolithic article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastoral_Neolithic) and [ancient DNA study](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aaw6275) discuss earlier livestock around Lake Turkana, multiple possible routes, and interaction with foragers. They do not prove a single farmer migration in 2000 BCE. The inherited Ethiopia point is a broad reference, not a route or settlement.
- Enriched existing Ishbi-Erra and Amar-Sin notes with connections to officials Puzur-Numushda and Zariqum, preserving the uncertainty of literary correspondence and distinguishing the Assur governor from a contemporary Susa namesake. Ranges and inherited pins are not proof of annual personal presence.
- Moved **20 Smithsonian GVP eruption rows** from the destructive hazard pack into indexed, non-disruptive historical notes, preserving IDs and nominal slots. Each exact volcano profile and evidence method is recorded in the manifest; confirmed eruptions do not imply exact dates. Ata is in Japan. The GVP profile and regional listing disagree about Jebel Marra's VEI; Tolima's VEI is itself uncertain. Vaiyots-Sar's nominal date is both questioned and ±1,000 years. Numerical uncertainties are not invented for entries marked only `(?)`.
- Moved **three directly matched NOAA rows** into non-disruptive notes. [Earthquake 2](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/2) and [tsunami 1](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/tsunamis/events/1) are linked, not independent corroboration; NOAA's tsunami entry has validity 1 and its comments describe second-millennium and much later proposed dates. [Earthquake 3](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/3) describes Ak-Tepe archaeological evidence and an approximately inferred depth; its exact date, magnitude and casualties need further study. These changes do not deny that damage may have occurred.

The inherited one-year slots and radii are references, not verified durations or damage footprints. Other records may still be inaccurate. News and other indexed readers have differing endpoint behavior; notes can recur. Moving an event changes gameplay and may affect existing saves.
