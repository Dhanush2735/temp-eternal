# History collection: 100–60 BCE

## Progress

Worked on **100, 90, 80, 70 and 60 BCE**, using English Wikipedia year pages and selected linked articles, plus NOAA and Smithsonian hazard catalogs. This is partial work, not a complete collection of those years or their linked articles.

**Total: 304 years partly worked; 0 years fully collected.** The years between these ten-year steps still need their own work.

## Changes in the game files

- Updated **24 existing records**, keeping their IDs. Corrected dates for Vercellae, the Silarius River battle and the siege of Mytilene. Fixed map locations including the San Bartolo Maya site in Guatemala and Caesar's Iberian campaign, which had been placed in New York. Qualified uncertain historical claims about Tigranes, Huo Guang, Mathura, the Western Regions and others.
- Added **two records**: an active military event for the Battle of the Baetis River in 80 BCE and a non-damaging historical note about the First Triumvirate. Their precise locations or timing remain uncertain; the battle marker is a broad estuary reference.
- Moved **24 volcano records** and **three reports of a possible earthquake/tsunami** out of the damaging hazard pack into non-damaging history notes. Smithsonian lists uncertain volcanic dates; the Etna 61 BCE eruption is itself uncertain. NOAA's 60 BCE Portuguese earthquake and tsunami reports depend on much later sources; their occurrence, dating and magnitude are doubtful, and the three records may refer to a single alleged event.
- Kept the **70 BCE Shandong earthquake** active, while marking its magnitude and reported deaths uncertain. NOAA event 32 reports magnitude 7 and more than 6,000 deaths; independent research discusses the ancient earthquake but uncertainties remain.
- Recorded **12 possible people leads** for later R3 VIP research, including Maues, Huo Guang, Zheng Ji, Tigranes, Ptolemy XII and Meleager. These are research notes, **not spawnable VIPs**. No VIP behavior or alternate-timeline feature was implemented here.

## Sources and limitations

- [100 BCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_BC), [90 BCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/90_BC), [80 BCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/80_BC), [70 BCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70_BC), [60 BCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60_BC), and selected decade and century pages. The 70 BCE article's calendar table incorrectly shows 100 BCE.
- Linked topics include [Battle of the Baetis River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Baetis_River), [San Bartolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bartolo_(Maya_site)), [Protectorate of the Western Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectorate_of_the_Western_Regions), [Maues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maues), [Meleager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meleager_of_Gadara) and [First Triumvirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Triumvirate). Individual sources and reasons are saved with each changed record in the manifest.
- [NOAA earthquake 9734](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/9734) and [tsunami 4263](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/tsunamis/events/4263) link the Portuguese reports; tsunami validity is 2, questionable. NOAA includes conflicting magnitude estimates and discussion of late sources with no identified ancient source. The two tsunami markers are not independent confirmation.
- The GVP listing gives Okmok II 100 BCE ±50 years; a [2020 ice-core and tephra study](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2002722117) dates Okmok II to 43 BCE. The record was moved to a note, not automatically converted to a new 43 BCE disaster; possible duplicate/correlation needs review.
- Conservative overlap scan found **12 war entries needing further review**, including `q1764124`, `q194378`, `q1991765`, `q677316`, `wpw_03eb1fac49`, `wpw_ee74f46ffc`, `wpw_a56ce1e417`, `wpw_51b25605d4`, `wpw_18f4598c05`, `wpw_1684ce2409`, `wpw_939d7ae7e3`, `wpw_88145ba824`. Their inclusion does not establish actual overlap under every loader. Two Ischia and Vulcano hazards (`hz_a06ab1a611`, `hz_cb11e41ad9`) have confirmed GVP-reported 91 BCE starts and unknown ends: they were left active, not automatically moved just because a stored end overlaps 90 BCE. Their duration and impacts still need review.
- Name and ID scans did not find a Baetis duplicate, but do not prove semantic uniqueness. Other existing records remain unreviewed. Wikipedia revisions were not independently pinned. The recursive crawler remains blocked by the storage guard, so linked articles were not exhaustively followed. This is not a claim of worldwide completeness.
