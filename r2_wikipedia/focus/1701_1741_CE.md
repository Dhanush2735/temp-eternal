# Wikipedia history pass: 1701–1741 CE

**Sampled years:** 1701, 1711, 1721, 1731 and 1741. These years were partly worked, not completely collected. The intervening years and many linked articles remain for later.

## Changes in the game resources

Updated **22 existing records** and added **two** across two indexed history packs. Existing IDs were preserved. The additions are the 1701 English Act of Settlement and the 1731 Ashburnham House fire, which damaged the Cotton library's manuscripts.

Examples of corrections:

- Moved the Great Peace of Montreal from Pennsylvania to a Montreal city reference point; corrected Dartsedo to a Kangding reference point, the Düna crossing to the Riga area, and the Battle of Carpi to Carpi near Legnago. Separate military records remain for the battles; edited entries are contextual notes.
- Corrected the location of the Battle of Cartagena de Indias from Madrid to Cartagena, Colombia, and Vitus Bering's death from a generic Siberian point to a Bering Island reference point. The Cartagena military record remains separate.
- Corrected the 1721 Boston inoculation entry: Boylston used **variolation**, not a vaccine. It now mentions Onesimus's contribution and removes an anachronistic Harvard Medical School claim.
- Moved Patrona Halil's death to 1730 and Laura Bassi's university appointment to 1732, following their linked biographies; the 1731 year page lists both in 1731. Moved Halil to Constantinople and removed the unsupported anatomy claim from Bassi's appointment.
- Corrected the 1731 Treaty of Vienna entry: Spain joined later in the year. Jacques I of Monaco's biography says his reign ended in 1733, not at his death in 1751. The 1741 New York conspiracy entry now makes clear that historians dispute whether a plot existed.
- Corrected the Clipperton Island location and qualified earlier European identification, and corrected the location of the 1721 Caracas university decree to a Lerma, Spain reference point. The capital of the university and the decree-signing town are different.

Coordinates described as proxies are approximate reference points, not proof of precise historical sites or complete geographic extent. Some edited battle and treaty records overlap existing military/political records. Changing effects or coordinates can change gameplay; saved-world effects are untested.

## Source trail and unresolved issues

Year pages [1701](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1701), [1711](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1711), [1721](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1721), [1731](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1731) and [1741](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1741), plus their decade pages, were sampled. The 1731 page has a citation warning. Decade transclusions are not independent evidence. Per-record linked sources and full before/after images are in the manifest.

The Basra source pages disagree on the day of the 1701 withdrawal; the revised record retains only the year. The 1731 Cotton library and year pages disagree on the number of manuscripts destroyed, so the new entry does not state a count. The 1741 year page places the Kampo tsunami on August 23 and claims at least 2,000 deaths, while its linked eruption article says August 29 and gives a range of estimates. The existing Japan-located tsunami note is still pinned far from Hokkaido and was **not corrected** in this pass; any associated hazard records remain unreviewed.

The indexed Paraguay Comuneros revolt `wpw_6e09d3bd22` still has an incorrect Central American coordinate, and its single stored 1721–1736 range obscures separate phases described by the linked article. The 1711 Zuytdorp departure note remains pinned at the later Australian wreck site; the 1721 Portuguese galleon capture remains pinned near Goa rather than Réunion. The older Feyiase entry `wpy_05843fc46d` still has a wrong 1750 year and location alongside the corrected 1701 entry `wpd_342c3bc78d`. Other duplicates, imprecise or incorrect locations and date conflicts remain; a name scan cannot prove semantic uniqueness. Different readers also interpret stored end-year boundaries differently.
