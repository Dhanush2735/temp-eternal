2. **Jeroboam–Nadab:** Updated `wpr_19cfeb67c6` from an unqualified political effect to a contextual note. The decade offers 909 or 910, while [Jeroboam](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jeroboam&oldid=1379985813) and [Nadab](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Nadab_of_Israel&oldid=1358960926) describe different chronological reconstructions, including an approximately nine-year difference. 3. **Shoshenq campaign:** Enriched `wpy_dd522d9aa9`, retaining its prior evidence. The Egyptian campaign has inscriptional evidence, but [Shishak](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shishak&oldid=1354627579) distinguishes the biblical Jerusalem episode from the [Bubastite Portal](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bubastite_Portal&oldid=1380737843) place list, which does not name Jerusalem. Explanations and historicity are debated. The approximate date, route and overlapping existing notes remain qualified.
4. **Zhou succession:** Enriched `r2_yih_zhou_890_context`, retaining existing evidence. [King Yih](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=King_Yih_of_Zhou&oldid=1369889997) and [King Xiao](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=King_Xiao_of_Zhou&oldid=1359828604) give conflicting reign dates; Xiao's article says a supposed usurpation is unclear. Yih's article also contains internally inconsistent succession language. The 890 slot is a context reference, not a claim that one specific king definitely ruled then.

These are four updates, **no additions**. Names and note effects are in indexed packs; the changes do not create new military or political triggers. Runtime behavior and saved-world effects were not tested.

## Other source checks

- The 920s Wikipedia page lists a Venus transit in **920 BCE**, citing [NASA's catalog](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/transit/catalog/VenusCatalog.html). NASA lists astronomical year **−920**, which is **921 BCE** in the historical BCE convention, not 920 BCE; see [NASA's dating explanation](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEhelp/dates.html). The catalog entry is a grazing geocentric prediction. No localized event was added: historical observation and a visibility footprint were not established. The project's BCE conventions require care before importing astronomical rows.
- The 930s page's reference to Samaria with the 931 BCE division should not establish the city as the contemporary capital: [Samaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaria_(ancient_city)) links its establishment as capital to Omri in the ninth century BCE. Earlier agricultural use of the hill is a separate question.
- [Adichanallur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adichanallur) contains inconsistent age claims. Existing game notes already qualify sample dates rather than setting a founding year. [San Lorenzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Lorenzo_Tenochtitl%C3%A1n) describes a decline in pre-eminence, not established destruction by northern migrants; the existing note already flags the cause.
- [Adad-nirari II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adad-nirari_II), [Shamash-mudammiq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamash-mudammiq) and [Nabu-shuma-ukin I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabu-shuma-ukin_I) provide opponents and diplomatic leads. Campaign dates and the identity of one marriage partner are uncertain. Assyrian annals are royal claims, not neutral independent confirmation.
