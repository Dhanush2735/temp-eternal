## What changed in the game

I replaced the indexed game JSON with **five corrections and eleven added historical notes**. They are non-disruptive cultural notes, not disasters or forced political actions. Their dates and places are estimates where the evidence requires it.

- **Iran:** corrected Shahr-i Sokhta's map pin from England to the actual site. Whether its later occupation continued around 2100–2000 BCE, and when abandonment occurred, is disputed.
- **Britain:** qualified Stonehenge's bluestone rearrangements; the evidence does not establish a single event exactly in 2100 BCE.
- **China:** identified Yu and the Xia foundation as tradition, not a proven event or a proven historical person.
- **Southern Urals:** changed the asserted exact beginning of Sintashta into an approximate cultural context, with the named archaeological site as a regional reference.
- **Crete:** corrected the Middle Minoan IA chronology without assigning inventions to one year.
- **Mesopotamia and Egypt:** added approximate Shulgi and Intef III reign contexts and references to the Ur law collection and ziggurat. Chronologies, legal authorship and building dates vary.
- Added bounded continuity notes for Únětice, Kerma, Caral, the Indus region, Shimao, Taosi and Egypt. These are context, **not proof of an event in every year**. Some appear in yearly news while a range is active.
