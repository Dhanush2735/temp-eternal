# 1412–1461 CE — preliminary R2 pass

Reviewed 2026-10-31. This is a **partial pass, not completion of these years**. `year_titles_1412_1461_CE.json` resolves all 50 annual titles; `year_wikitext_1412_1461_CE.json.xz` preserves the raw revisions. Raw wikitext does not expand transcluded pages. `indexed_start_scan_1412_1461_CE.txt.xz` is a snapshot of 1,911 indexed records whose start falls in the interval; earlier-starting overlaps were not scanned. Most records, article links and references remain unexamined.

Pachacuti's 1438 is conventional chronology; source histories of Majapahit succession are imperfect. A point is not evidence of a whole affected territory.

## Hazards still needing review

The original start-year scan counted 491 hazard rows: 378 plague, 62 volcano, 28 earthquake, 18 famine, four tsunami and one flood. **480 were not moved**, including all 378 plague rows; most have not been source-checked. Other packs can also produce event effects. Some might be inaccurate, duplicated, or incorrectly located. The surviving world-pack 1428 Catalonia and 1456 Italy lockdown effects and the 1436 nature flood are not validated simulations. The five 1416–1453 Yellow River/Yangtze entries all share a generic upstream point and 300 km radius; no better precise location was asserted. There may be other duplicates, including earlier-starting overlapping records. The separate NOAA Kuwae 1430 row remains unreviewed; its relationship to GVP's 1425 ±5 estimate and the later ice-core peak is unresolved.

Sources include [NOAA earthquake 593](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/593), [earthquake 611](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/611), [tsunami 135](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/tsunamis/events/135), [tsunami 3482](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/tsunamis/events/3482), and Smithsonian GVP for [Changbaishan](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=305060), [Haleakala](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=332060), [Taveuni](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=245010), [Kikai](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=282060) and [Kuwae](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=257070). NOAA rates the two moved tsunamis very doubtful; the Inchon record mentions storm waves. GVP reports Haleakala 1420 ±100, Taveuni 1420 ±20, Kikai 1430 ±75, Kuwae 1425 ±5 and a questionable Changbaishan 1413 eruption. These are not fixed-year certainties. Confirmed eruption occurrence and uncertainty in its year are distinct questions.

## People and validation

`history/sources/wikipedia_1412_1461_ce_people_links.json` contains 40 research-only people, 23 qualified relationships and 42 event-role links across 14 IDs. It includes participants from opposite sides and people associated with successive phases, not just principal figures. The graph is **not spawnable VIP data**, and an event association does not prove a person's presence throughout an event.

Thirty Python tests passed: manifest guards, indexed uniqueness, in-memory current and preceding-layer reverse/reapply, graph references, year-title revisions, resolver and pipeline tests. Fifty-six Wikipedia title/revision pairs used by the context and graph were independently checked against the Wikipedia revision API. The check validates revision/title identity, not every claim or source quality. Both layers were actually rolled back and reapplied on disk to correct a review-note radius. No live game, Java build, saved-world behavior, natural VIP spawn or branching test was performed.

The inventory script finds 4,450 recognized distinct annual title resolutions, not collected histories; 11 early BCE titles are outside recognized title files. The legacy 3,168 partly-worked-year count remains unreconciled. **Zero years have been established as fully collected.**

## Reversibility and storage

Installers `../apply_1412_1461_ce_context.py` and `../apply_1412_1461_ce_hazards.py` support `stage`, `apply`, and `rollback` with hash and before-image guards. To reverse, undo any later dependent layers first, then this batch's hazards, then context. Changes are atomic per pack, not transactional across packs; saved-world side effects are untested. Earlier archive conversion maps are `archive_compression_1412_2026-10.json`, `archive_compression_extra_1412_2026-10.json`, and `../reviews/ce_manifest_compression_1412_2026-10.json`; old reports may name original gzip paths.
