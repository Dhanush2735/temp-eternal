# 551–591 CE: partial research and game history updates

**Partly worked:** 551, 561, 571, 581 and 591 CE. The five year pages, initial decade sections and selected linked articles were checked. Many links remain unchecked; decade pages reuse year-page material and are not independent confirmation. Wikipedia pages are current and not revision-pinned.

## Indexed game JSON

Installed **33 updates and three additions** across four indexed packs. Existing IDs were retained. The additions are a 551 Getica note, a 561 First Council of Braga note, and a 552 First Turkic Khaganate event, the last added to correct conflicting 551 founding dates. Name scans in nearby years found no other matching Braga or Getica entry, but cannot prove semantic uniqueness. The 552 event's Altai coordinates are a broad regional reference, not an exact coronation site.

The before/after manifest is `reviews/551_591_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `1463f4c50afafd0421254fba649dcd58dca61f7972cebb470c7e957921767629`. `apply_551_591_ce_context.py` stages, applies or rolls back, checking exact before/after images. Writes are atomic per pack, not across all packs.

- **551:** Corrected Xiao Dong's relationship to Emperor Jianwen. Two 551 Turkic founding effects are now notes: the year page says 551, while Bumin and the khaganate articles say 552. Added Getica as a cultural note; the linked biography places composition in Constantinople around 551, while the book article allows shortly afterward. The Sena Gallica city point represents an offshore naval battle.
- **561:** Qualified the Battle of Cúl Dreimhne to 555–561 and converted the second active battle to a note. Corrected Gao Zhan's accession point to Jinyang. Qualified Sky Witness's accession and seat, Chlothar's death and Jñānagupta's multi-year translation activity. Added a Braga council note; the exact assembly building is not asserted.
- **571:** Corrected the unsupported location of Biedcanford. The point is a broad Aylesbury-area reference for the Chronicle campaign, not the unidentified battle site; [British History Online](https://www.british-history.ac.uk/vch/oxon/vol1/pp346-372) rejects its identification with Bedford. Qualified Wuffa's historicity, Benevento's foundation date and Liuvigild's Córdoba chronology. Ælla's conflicting 581 reference is a note; his traditional 560 accession was recategorized from treaty to political event.
- **581:** Moved Monte Cassino from near Vienna to the abbey, and made it a note because the abbey article gives different dates, including around 570, 580 and 581. Corrected the failed Byzantine–Ghassanid campaign toward Ctesiphon and al-Mundhir's arrest: he was accused but not tried. Qualified the Mâcon synod's 581/582 date and Cherson's disputed commander. Emperor Jing yielded the throne before being killed; the duplicate Sui founding effect became a note. Constantina is dated 581 or 582; a likely duplicate at 582 was relocated from Iran and changed to a note.
- **591:** Corrected Chinkultic's ballcourt coordinates and made the dedication cultural. Clarified Agilulf's formal Milan investiture versus his earlier assumption of title, and that Arechis was only possibly Zotto's nephew. Ctesiphon is a capital reference for Khosrow's restoration, not Blarathon's battlefield. The northern Italy locust point is an approximate regional reference. The Jñānagupta/Vimalakīrti attribution requires further verification. Balkan campaigns span 582–602; the 591 point is regional context rather than a claim of a single conquest.

City, capital and region points are gameplay references, not proof of exact locations or geographic footprints. Some one-year entries are modeling slots, not verified dates. Readers differ in end-boundary semantics. NewsIndex reads kinds independently of effects, so a note is not guaranteed to be invisible. Moving coordinates or changing effects may influence saved games.
