# 2880 BCE — partial ten-year step

## History improved in the game

I corrected **two existing entries** about **Prometheus**, an ancient bristlecone pine in what is now Nevada, USA.

One entry uses an older possible date; the other uses **around 2880 BCE**. Both now say they refer to **the same tree**, and that its germination date is uncertain. Tree-ring counts do not establish its exact first year.

I kept both old IDs to avoid silently removing saved-game references. That means the game may still show two notes in different years; they have not yet been fully combined. The changes have not been tested in an existing save.

Sources: [Wikipedia’s Prometheus article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Prometheus_(tree)&oldid=1367341525) and the [US National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/grba/learn/historyculture/the-prometheus-story.htm).

## VIP research

I saved **Donald Rusk Currey**, a modern geographer connected to the tree’s research, as a VIP candidate. He did **not** live in 2880 BCE and is not an active VIP NPC. I also saved a limited research lead for Donald Graybill, who is credited with a later ring count.
