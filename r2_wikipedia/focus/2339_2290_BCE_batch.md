**Partial work, not 50 completed histories.** All 50 year titles were checked: 39 redirect to the 24th-century article (revision 1371895851) and 11 to the 23rd-century article (revision 1371895841). Redirects are not year-specific research. Selected linked biographies, Korean traditions, cultures and Smithsonian eruption records were checked; many links and regions remain uncollected.

## Indexed game history

- Added `r2_dangun_gojoseon_tradition_2333`, a cultural **mythology reference**, not a political founding event. Dangun's traditional 2333 BCE date is a later calculation, not an archaeologically proven event or lifespan. The earliest extant Dangun narrative is in the 13th-century *Samguk yusa*. The Pyongyang-area pin is only a regional reference; the traditional Asadal's location is disputed. The linked [Dangun](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Tan%27gun&oldid=1380481574), [Gojoseon](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Old_Chos%C5%8Fn&oldid=1374403964) and [Samguk yusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Samguk_yusa&oldid=1381756217) articles make the distinction clear.
- Updated `wpr_36dd7c7369` to name Enheduanna as Sargon's daughter as well as priestess at Ur. The disk inscription supports the relationship; her birth date and the attribution of later surviving literary works are uncertain. The separate artifact note remains.
- Updated `wpy_1ca9938dd7` to reflect that the separate Hekla hazard was moved to a note. The 2360 overview, Smithsonian 2310 ±20 and recent ice-core 2375 ±8 BCE estimates refer to alternative dating of H4, not proven separate eruptions. Both notes remain pending deduplication.
