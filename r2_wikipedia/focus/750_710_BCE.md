# 750–710 BCE: partial research and game-data changes

**Focus years:** 750, 740, 730, 720 and 710 BCE. These are ten-year steps; intervening years remain for later.

**Overall progress: 239 focus years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** Selected linked pages were checked, not the full Wikipedia link network.

## Changes installed in the game

The indexed game JSON now has **24 corrected records**, **27 moved records** and **4 new context notes**. Moved records include 16 volcano records, 6 broad or uncertain war records, and 5 Wikidata battle records. Existing IDs were kept. The four new entries are notes, not proof that an event or person appeared in the exact slot year.

- **750 BCE:** Qualified the claim about Urartian defenses against Cimmerians. Cumae and Naples no longer share an asserted single 750 founding. El-Kurru pyramids remain a note with disputed timing.
- **740 BCE:** Arpad’s siege duration differs in the decade and site articles. Ahaz’s accession has competing reconstructions. Added a broad Piye reign-context note, not an assertion of where he was in 740.
- **730 BCE:** Piye’s exact accession is disputed between decade pages, and Osorkon IV’s later rule shows why a complete sudden end to Libyan rule in northern Egypt is misleading.
- **720 BCE:** A Tyre siege note was moved from an incorrect Assyrian location to the Tyre area. The Chicago museum location of a Dur-Sharrukin guardian figure was replaced with the ancient site as a historical reference; its precise creation date is unverified. The fall of Samaria is attributed differently in sources.
- **710 BCE:** The Medes’ political development is a broad context rather than a precisely dated unification. The historical details of a 710 Babylon battle need more work.

Nearby, Biskupin’s two differently dated records are qualified and one mislocated pin corrected. A **713 BCE Olmec founding of Monte Albán** claim is explicitly marked contradicted: the linked site article gives approximately 500 BCE for the city’s foundation. Other records may still repeat these events.
