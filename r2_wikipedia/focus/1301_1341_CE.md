# Wikipedia history pass: 1301–1341 CE

**Five years partly worked:** 1301, 1311, 1321, 1331 and 1341. Many linked articles and intervening years remain; none is fully collected.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

**40 existing records updated and five added across six indexed packs.** Examples:

- **1301:** corrected locations for Edward I's arrival at Glasgow, the Genoese taking of Monaco, Verona's succession and the Japanese imperial succession. Added Andrew III's death in Buda and Charles of Valois's entry into Florence.
- **1311:** corrected the locations of events in Algeciras, Kamakura, Milan and Venice. Updated the Dwarasamudra siege, the Maestà altarpiece and the disputed height of Lincoln Cathedral's former spire. Added the Milan uprising and the Council of Vienne.
- **1321:** corrected the siege of Leeds Castle to **Kent**, not the city of Leeds. Corrected Gračanica Monastery's location. Clarified that the French lepers' plot was an accusation, not established fact. Corrected Spitakavor's 1321 record to refer to its church rather than its whole complex.
- **1331:** labeled the proposed Hebei Black Death identification as unproven. Clarified that the Kamakura shogunate fell in 1333, not 1331. Added Nicaea's fall, which may overlap the existing siege row in some readers.
- **1341:** moved Petrarch's coronation from Australia to Rome and Queen's College to Oxford. Made the Periyar flood a qualified, non-disruptive note because both the event and its effects are debated. Removed a premature marriage from the Margaret of Tyrol entry.

Each changed record contains source URLs and a reason, while the review manifest retains exact before and after images. Coordinates may be city or regional proxies, not exact event sites. Existing IDs and stored ranges were retained.

## Important limits

- **Warangal chronology:** two 1909 gazetteer rows presented the final capture as a 1321 battle. They are now source notes explaining that later histories commonly date it to 1323. The separately indexed 1323 siege was updated; the old quotations, 1321 slots and IDs remain. The older date is a historical source disagreement, not something resolved by erasing the source. The 1321 first-attack chronology also varies. The gazetteer has equal stored start/end years; HistoryGazetteer clamps these to one year, but other readers handle ends differently. Two notes may still be repeated as news. `gaz_auto_012` refers to a different district-centre entry and remains unreviewed; no identity or date was assumed.
- **Unverified Georgian claim:** the old 1341 Tbilisi/Jerusalem assertion is now an explicit note requiring verification, not a political event. One history institute dates the transfer of the episcopal see to 1329 and describes religious-site diplomacy, not a 1341 military reconquest of Jerusalem.
- **More uncertainty and overlap:** Halmyros's exact site is disputed; its coordinate is only a broad proxy. Ibn Battuta's Kilwa date varies between accounts. The two 1311 Ordinances entries remain overlapping, as do Genkō's war/start/summary entries. Nicaea's 1331 capture may overlap its siege entry; the siege's old stored end can be excluded by some readers. The Breton war's end range and other inherited war ranges were not changed. 1331's combined Cividale/Alicante row and other scanned entries remain unreviewed.
- **60 hazard rows** overlap at least one sampled year under an inclusive range scan. They were not reviewed or changed, and this scan does not establish whether a particular reader triggers them. See `reviews/1301_1341_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`. Previous batches' unresolved overlaps also remain.
- **52 people leads** are saved in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1301_1341_ce.json`. These are research notes only, not complete profiles or naturally spawning VIP NPCs.
- No Java/Forge, in-game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was done for this batch. The old jar does not include these new JSON changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
