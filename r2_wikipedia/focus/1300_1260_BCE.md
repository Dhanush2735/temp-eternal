# 1300–1260 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked:** 1300, 1290, 1280, 1270 and 1260 BCE. These are five ten-year steps; the years between them remain for later. None is fully collected.

## Changes made to the game

The indexed game history packs now contain **nine corrections, fifteen records moved to cautious notes, and two new notes**. Existing IDs were kept for the corrected and moved records.

- **Poverty Point, Louisiana:** corrected the location. Its earthworks were built over a long period; older mound complexes such as Watson Brake mean Poverty Point should not be called the earliest.
- **Pylos:** corrected the map point from modern Pylos to the Palace of Nestor archaeological site; its exact building date is uncertain.
- **Tollense Valley:** corrected the map point to northern Germany. The battle's exact year and estimates of participant numbers remain uncertain.
- **Fiji:** moved the Bourewa pottery point from New Caledonia to a broad Fiji reference. The century page's 1260 BCE date conflicts with a later radiocarbon study; two related game records may both appear.
- **Greece:** qualified the dates for the Warrior Vase and the Treasury of Atreus. The vase's point is its archaeological findspot, not a verified manufacturing site. The tomb's mythical names do not establish its occupant.
- **Central Europe:** changed the Lusatian culture from a political event to a cultural note; the linked article gives conflicting starting dates and carries a warning about potentially AI-generated content.
- **Torah:** replaced a confident 1280 BCE completion claim with a note distinguishing traditional attribution from scholarly views that the final form was largely completed later. The retained map point is not a proven place of composition.
- **Dapur and Alashiya:** moved two military-effect records to cautious regional history notes because dates, locations or active battle intervals were not sufficiently established. These preserve their IDs but change gameplay classification; the dates shown in the game are reference slots, not verified siege or battle years.
- **Disasters:** moved 12 volcano records and one Troad tsunami record out of the active disaster pack into notes. Smithsonian identifies the eruptions as confirmed eruptive periods, but their dates are estimates, often with wide uncertainties; NOAA's ancient Troad tsunami record has uncertain validity and cause. The two Katla estimates may be related rather than separate events.
- Added a **Seti I succession context** for 1290 and an **Alaksandu–Muwatalli treaty context** for 1280. Both dates are approximate, and the map points do not prove where the people were or where the treaty was signed.

## What is still unfinished

The two Tollense references at 1249 and 1200 BCE are also unreviewed and may refer to the same conflict; the two Bourewa entries may repeat. Alashiya's retained interval is only an uncertain reference and can recur as news, not evidence of annual battles. No active hazard rows now overlap these five exact years, but this is not an audit of other years or hazards already affecting saved worlds.
