# 2550 to 2510 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2550, 2540, 2530, 2520 and 2510 BCE**. Their Wikipedia year and decade titles lead to the same century page. I also followed some linked articles; this is **not a complete collection**.

## Game history updated

I corrected **four entries** and added **four** to the game's indexed JSON files:

- **Stonehenge, Britain:** corrected a later-only construction entry to cover an approximate period for the large stone settings. Stonehenge had earlier and later phases too.
- **Caral, Peru:** added early site context, with an approximate date rather than an exact founding year.
- **Ur, Iraq:** corrected a claim about the animals shown on the Standard of Ur—their identification is debated—and added a cautious reference to Puabi.
- **Egypt:** separated the Pyramid of Khafre and the Great Sphinx into distinct, approximate entries. Their dates and the Sphinx's identity are uncertain.
- **Mesopotamia:** added approximate references to Mesilim of Kish and Lugalshaengur of Lagash.

Long period notes can appear in multiple years; that does **not** mean a separate event happened every year.
