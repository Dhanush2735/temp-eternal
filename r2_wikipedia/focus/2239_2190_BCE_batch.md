## Indexed game-data changes

Two existing regional records were enriched, preserving their IDs, slots and coordinates:

- `r2_shar_kali_sharri_akkad_context` now records connections to queen Tuta-sar-libbis, Lagash governor Lugal-ushumgal and scribe Ibni-Sharrum. The seal attestations do not establish exact appointment years or the people's presence at the regional pin. The reign dates remain approximate and use one chronology.
- `wpr_2adae19fb6` identifies early Batanes archaeological sites and Taiwan-linked pottery. [Bellwood's synthesis](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/658181) discusses early ceramics at Reranum and Torongan Caves and proposes settlement around 2200 BCE. The note is not proof of a landing in that year or the language spoken by a particular individual; dating and interpretations remain approximate.

The existing Gutian-conflict note and Mawmluh climate-proxy record remain qualified: neither an exact destruction of Akkad nor an automatic worldwide drought or civilization collapse is established. Relevant linked articles include [Shar-Kali-Sharri](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shar-Kali-Sharri&oldid=1379603611), [Lugal-ushumgal](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lugal-ushumgal&oldid=1342423023), [Gutian rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Gutian_rule_in_Mesopotamia&oldid=1376539585) and [the 4.2-kiloyear event](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=4.2-kiloyear_event&oldid=1380005033).
