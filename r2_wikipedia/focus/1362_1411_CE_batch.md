# 1362–1411 CE — preliminary R2 pass

**Status: partial, not 50 completed histories.** Archived 50 distinct annual titles and their raw pinned revisions. Transclusions were not expanded. Scanned 1,903 indexed records starting in the interval, including 454 hazards. Earlier-starting records that overlap the interval were excluded. Most records, year sections and linked articles remain unchecked.

## Changes in indexed game data

- Staged and applied **29 guarded updates** across seven indexed packs, preserving IDs and before-images. Corrected or qualified records for Lake Poyang, the foundation of the Ming dynasty, Suzhou, the Kalmar Union, the Jingnan campaign, Yongle's accession and the first Ming treasure voyage. In particular, Beiping is modern Beijing, not Cambridge; the 1368 Ming foundation at Nanjing is distinct from the Yuan withdrawal from Dadu; and the Kalmar Union was a personal union, not the merger of three kingdoms.
- Moved the erroneous Vorskla battle marker to the article's Ukraine reference and made it a note alongside the separate active battle. Corrected the Tokhtamysh–Timur war's range to **1386–1395**, using 1396 as its exclusive end. Corrected the 1398 Delhi record: Mahmud Shah II was not the last ruler of the Delhi Sultanate. Clavijo's 1403 departure is now referenced to the Cádiz region, not his 1404 destination Samarkand. Single points do not represent a whole campaign or journey.
- Changed the redundant 1404 regional St. Elizabeth's flood record to a note. A separate nature-pack flood effect remains active. Moved the 1375 Yellow River flood reference from the upper river to Kaifeng, retaining a small disruption effect; neither that effect nor its radius has been validated. NOAA's death-order categories were removed from five earthquake names that incorrectly presented them as exact death counts. The earthquake effects remain active but unvalidated.
- **17 hazards were moved to indexed non-damaging notes:** 11 eruptions with uncertain dates, three possible or doubtful NOAA tsunami reports, two misplaced duplicates of the 1362 Saint Marcellus's flood, and a misplaced duplicate of the 1382 Dover Straits earthquake. Confirmed geological eruptions are not denied: for example, GVP gives Merapi **1380 ±300 years**, Aniakchak **1370 ±55**, and Rangitoto **1397 ±7**. The 1400 Arenal date is questionable. NOAA classifies the 1406 Shizuoka event as doubtful, 1407 Haeju as very doubtful, and the 1402 Syria wave as possible; the first two may have been storm waves.
- The 1362 flood remains represented by `hz_d597340413`, and the Dover earthquake by world record `q55600359`. Their existing effects, radii and casualty estimates are **not validated**; a single point cannot represent the flood's distributed impacts.
- **437 of the 454 scanned hazards were not moved**, including all **340 location-specific plague rows**. Most remain unreviewed: do not assume their dates, identities, locations, radii or effects are correct. Other volcanoes, earthquakes, floods and tsunami entries remain active without full assessment.
