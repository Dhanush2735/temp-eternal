# 2900 BCE — partial ten-year step

## History improved in the game

I corrected **five existing entries** in the indexed game JSON:

- **Mari:** its first city is dated to around 2900 BCE, not an exact year.
- **Early Dynastic Mesopotamia:** dates are approximate and scholars use different timelines.
- **Writing:** cuneiform developed gradually from earlier writing, not all in one year.
- **Wars:** the old entry no longer says a particular war happened in 2900 BCE.
- **Ur:** the entry now refers to early remains rather than claiming the city became wealthy in exactly 2900 BCE.

The period entry can appear across multiple years in the game; that does **not** mean an event happened every year. Changing older entries may affect existing saves; that has not been tested.

Sources include [Wikipedia’s Mari article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Mari,_Syria&oldid=1382056034), [Early Dynastic Mesopotamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Early_Dynastic_Period_(Mesopotamia)&oldid=1381348519), and [Cuneiform](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cuneiform&oldid=1381622586). The [French Ministry of Culture](https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/mari/en) also dates Mari’s founding to around 2900 BCE.
