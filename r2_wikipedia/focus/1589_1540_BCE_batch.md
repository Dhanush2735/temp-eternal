## Indexed game JSON

**Nine existing records were qualified or enriched, one historical context was added, and one uncertain-date hazard was moved out of the destructive hazard pack.** Existing IDs and stored time slots were preserved.

- Two Egyptian dynasty-transition records no longer imply that several dynasties all ended in exactly 1567 BCE. Their points now use Thebes as a regional reference. A 1552 BCE New Kingdom boundary is also a qualified note. These entries overlap other boundary notes; IDs were retained pending further reconciliation.

- The Ahmose I context now distinguishes his mother Ahhotep I, sister-wife Ahmose-Nefertari and [Ahmose, son of Ebana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmose,_son_of_Ebana), the soldier whose tomb biography describes military service. The soldier is not Pharaoh Ahmose I or the Rhind papyrus scribe Ahmes. No exact dates or annual itineraries are inferred.
- A Mycenae political-dominance claim and a Memphis world-population ranking became qualified notes, not precise annual political changes. A supposed 1558 BCE Shang founding treaty is a historical-chronology claim, not a verified treaty; its attribution and dating need checking against a primary edition of the Bamboo Annals.
- The 1556 BCE Cecrops/Deucalion entry is now a **mythology note**, rather than a disruptive flood. Its date and Athens pin do not establish a historical flood or ruler.

### Jericho earthquake record

[NOAA earthquake 8](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/8) lists a Jericho-area earthquake at nominal 1566 BCE, intensity 10, with no month, day or magnitude; its [reference](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/hazard-service/api/v1/earthquakes/8/references) is a 1985 regional seismicity catalogue. That catalogue was **not** inspected, so the evidence for the date, intensity and point is not independently established here. Interpretations of Middle Bronze Jericho destruction and seismic causation differ; this record is not assumed to be the same as an archaeological destruction layer. The entry was moved to an indexed non-disruptive note without denying possible earthquake damage; its nominal date, coordinates and radius are not a verified exact event or footprint.
