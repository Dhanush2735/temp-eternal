# 1239–1190 BCE: partial R2 collection

**Progress: 2,133 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** This batch covers the 50 intervening year titles, not just five endpoints. All 50 redirect to five decade articles; the linked articles were reviewed selectively, not exhaustively, and redirects do not supply individual annual histories. Under the existing broad shared-period counting convention, 1230, 1220, 1210, 1200 and 1190 BCE were previously counted, so this batch adds **45** years to the prior 2,088. Earlier years remain incomplete.

## Changes to indexed game history

Nine existing records were qualified or corrected, one note added and two uncertain volcanic hazards moved to historical notes. Existing IDs were preserved. In particular:

- The **Battle of Perire** is recorded as a contested-date, regional campaign note rather than an exact-place military trigger. The Egyptian account describes Merneptah and Meryey's coalition; the location of Perire and the date are uncertain. The separately reviewed 1221 campaign entry may overlap it.
- **Theseus** is labelled as myth, not a verified ruler. Two potentially duplicate **Wu Ding** death/succession claims were made non-disruptive and linked to Zu Geng with qualified chronology. Ramesses II's succession is also approximate, and the old map point does not prove his death location.
- The **Late Bronze Age collapse**, Assyrian–Babylonian conflict, late Egyptian 19th Dynasty disputes and Ugarit's destruction are presented as uncertain or multi-phase contexts, not universal exact-year changes. Ugarit's own article gives around 1185 BCE, while Ammurapi's biography mentions 1192–1190; neither fixes a conclusive date or attacker.
- A note for the **Tale of Two Brothers** distinguishes the literary characters from historical people. The date and attribution to scribe Ennana are approximate or qualified; the broad Egypt marker is not a proven place of writing or discovery.
- The Smithsonian GVP identifies eruptions at **Isla San Luis** (1212 BCE ±127 years, uncalibrated shell radiocarbon) and **Naolinco** (1202 BCE ±180 years, calibrated radiocarbon). They remain historical notes rather than exact-year damage events. Confirmation of an eruption is not proof of its exact date or destructive footprint.

The changes are in the indexed source resources used by the project, not just a separate research report. Note effects remain indexed references; this batch does not implement new world simulation or verify how these changes appear in a running game.
