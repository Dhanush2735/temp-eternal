# 1101–1141 CE: partial collection and project update

**Years partly worked:** 1101, 1111, 1121, 1131 and 1141 CE. English Wikipedia year pages, the beginnings of the corresponding decade pages and selected linked articles were consulted. The five years are **not fully collected**. Decade entries are often transcluded from year pages, not independent corroboration. The intervening years and many other linked articles remain to be researched.

## Installed changes

- Updated **36 existing records** and added **6** across five indexed game-data packs. The adjacent 1122, 1142 and 1150 legacy entries were also qualified when they conflicted with linked articles. Existing IDs and stored date ranges were preserved. Full before/after records, source URLs and reasons are in `r2_wikipedia/reviews/1101_1141_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `12405852dc7d61d9d5643d4382b34df094aa1502985d335a621b0fee599815ab`.
- Added **47 research-only people leads** in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1101_1141_ce.json`. They are not finished profiles or naturally spawnable VIPs.
- Thirteen guarded-installer tests passed before and after installation. All five staged documents were logically reversed against their original versions, and all 42 installed after-images were checked for uniqueness and exact match across the index. During verification, an earlier application was rolled back to correct the Alton reference, then the revised manifest was staged and installed. Writes are atomic per pack, not a transaction across all packs.

## Examples

- **1101:** Moved the account of the capture of Caesarea from Genoa to a coastal reference, Roger I's death from Palermo to Mileto, and Ranulf Flambard's escape from Durham to the Tower of London. Added the death of Song writer Su Shi at Changzhou. The Cilician fleet entry now describes the linked biography's approximate 1101–1102 date and uses a regional proxy.
- **1111:** Moved the account of Henry V's imperial coronation to Rome and the destruction of Laus Pompeia to Lodi Vecchio. Added al-Ghazali's death at Tus. Donglin Academy's 1111 foundation is distinguished from its much later restoration; the two 1111 founding records still overlap.
- **1121:** Corrected the locations of Reading Abbey, L'Aumône and the Prémontré accounts. The linked order and abbey articles describe a **1120 foundation**, whereas the year page reports vows in 1121. Added the assassination of Fatimid vizier al-Afdal; his article gives conflicting days in December and different accounts of responsibility. Fang La was captured in Qingxi and executed at Kaifeng; Hangzhou is only a broad campaign reference.
- **1131:** Moved Tintern Abbey to the Wye Valley and the joint coronation of Melisende and Fulk to Jerusalem. The Beisi Pagoda record now describes a Song-period design dated 1131–1162; the current building is later, according to the linked article. Added references to the deaths of Canute Lavard, Omar Khayyam and Ayn al-Quzat Hamadani.
- **1141:** Clarified Lincoln, Qatwan and the Rout of Winchester. Queen Matilda and Empress Matilda were different people: after Robert of Gloucester was captured, he was exchanged for **Stephen**, who returned to the throne. The Treaty of Shaoxing entry distinguishes the 1141 agreement from its 1142 formal ratification. The Ricasoli entry is limited to the estate's own account of a 1141 document about Brolio Castle; it is not proof that a modern company was founded then.

Selected sources: [Caesarea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Caesarea_Maritima_(1101)), [Eustathios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eustathios_Kymineianos), [Donglin Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donglin_Academy), [Didgori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Didgori), [Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Tbilisi_(1122)), [Fang La](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fang_La), [al-Afdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Afdal_Shahanshah), [Premonstratensians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premonstratensians), [Beisi Pagoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beisi_Pagoda), [Winchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rout_of_Winchester) and [Shaoxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Shaoxing). Other sources and their qualifications are attached to individual records.

## Important limits

- **27 hazard rows** have stored ranges that touch at least one sampled year when both endpoints are counted inclusively. This is not proof they all trigger in those years: readers interpret end boundaries differently. They are listed in `r2_wikipedia/reviews/1101_1141_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`; all remain **active and unreviewed**.
- Duplicate or overlapping records remain active: Caesarea year/siege, Santarém campaign/capture, Donglin year/decade, Prémontré year/decade, Tbilisi's several entries, and the 1141/1142 treaty references. Searches by name and year cannot prove semantic uniqueness.
- The 1121 year page claims Tbilisi was captured that year, but the linked siege article dates its capture to **1122** and also reports an earlier 1121 siege. The indexed 1121–1122 siege and a wrong-year 1150 legacy slot remain in place with qualifications. The latter is a legacy record, not a claim that the present 1150 page lists it. The 1122 vizier record also retains a disputed year. Preserving a legacy date does not establish its correctness.
- The war-pack entry for Henry of Lower Lorraine was a biography, not a single war. Its kind is now `political` and effect `note`, but it remains in the war pack with its old 1101–1116 range; downstream interpretation still needs review. Several other records changed kind/effect or location and may affect gameplay or existing saves. Regional points are approximations, not exact historical sites.
- Not every linked source, birth/death, place or event has been checked. No Java, Forge, in-game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration or jar rebuild was performed. The old jar **does not include** these source-data changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
