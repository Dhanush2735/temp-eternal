# 600–560 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked on:** 600, 590, 580, 570 and 560 BCE. This is a partial pass, not a complete collection of those years or of every linked article.

## Changes made to the game

The indexed game history JSON was updated: **16 records corrected and 29 records moved**; no new event was added, avoiding deliberate duplicates. The changes include:

- Corrected a claim that Cahokia's largest mound was begun in 600 BCE. The linked Monks Mound article dates the beginning around **900–955 CE**; the record now uses 900 CE as an approximate reference, not a verified year of beginning. It may overlap other Cahokia records.
- Removed the asserted 570 BCE founding of an Achaemenid Armenian satrapy and corrected its Babylon pin to a broad Armenian-highlands reference. Qualified the 600 BCE version too. The region's political transition is not an exact-year claim.
- Corrected the statue known as the Berlin Kore from Germany to a reference near its discovery in Attica, Greece. Its ancient workshop location is not proven by the findspot; it was displayed in Berlin much later.
- Corrected the Tyre siege context from Iraq to Tyre, and retained the separate active siege record with the commonly reconstructed **586–573 BCE**, while marking the dates and outcome uncertain.
- Qualified Massalia's approximate foundation, the uncertain Hanging Gardens, Urartu's disputed end and Indian regional history. Corrected the Corfu temple and Babylonian succession references. Preserved the political effects for the reviewed Cambyses, Croesus and Neriglissar records; Croesus's accession date remains disputed.
- Moved **23 volcano records** from destructive hazards to historical notes because the Smithsonian's dates are approximate, uncertain or bounded, not proof of destruction in a specific game year. Colli Albani's eruption itself is uncertain. Two broad war-pack records and three uncertain Wikidata conflict records also became notes rather than fixed-location military effects. Their source IDs were retained.

These moves do not erase knowledge of the events; they stop those particular entries from triggering unsupported exact-year disaster or military effects. Other records with related events may still overlap. Some changed records use a single year as a reference slot for a broader approximate period; it is not a claim of an exact year.

## People for later R3 work
