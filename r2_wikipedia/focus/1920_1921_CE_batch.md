Reviewed 1 November 2026. This is a small, selective pass, **not completed collection** of either year. The annual revision was archived for each year, and indexed starts were inventoried; most records, hazards, article links and people remain unreviewed. ## Changes applied to indexed gameplay JSON

**1920:** moved the Treaty of Tartu record from Siberia to a Tartu city proxy and clarified Soviet recognition of Estonia; moved the San Remo conference to a Sanremo city proxy; moved the Tambov rebellion to a regional proxy instead of Mongolia; moved the Iraqi revolt to a Samawah-front proxy instead of the Arctic; moved the U.S. Senate's Versailles vote to a Capitol proxy; and clarified that the League of Nations began in January and moved its headquarters to Geneva later that year. Neither a city proxy nor an arbitrary game radius describes a whole rebellion or proves its exact site. Existing political/note effects remain.

**1921:** moved Talaat Pasha's assassination to the article's reported Berlin site; moved the Treaty of Ankara signing from Paris to an Ankara city proxy; moved the Chinese Communist Party's first congress opening to a Shanghai city proxy (the meeting continued in Jiaxing); moved the Malabar rebellion to a Tirurangadi proxy for southern Malabar, not its full extent; and changed the Arlington unknown soldier interment record from treaty/political to cultural/note, with the reported memorial coordinates. The existing annual granularity does not place these events on their precise dates.

Records retain their IDs. Before and after states are in `reviews/{1920,1921}_ce_context_2026-11.json.gz`; installers `apply_1920_ce_context.py` and `apply_1921_ce_context.py` support guarded reversal. For each year all 26 indexed pack hashes were checked through apply, rollback and reapply; three packs changed per year. Atomicity is per pack, not across packs. No live game or saved-world test was run.

## Sources and people

Pinned English Wikipedia revisions are recorded on records and in the graphs and `focus/pin_verification_{1920,1921}_CE_context.json.xz`; 25 and 20 pins respectively were checked for page/revision identity, **not proof of every claim**. The U.S. Senate's treaty list independently records the March 1920 vote. Some Wikipedia articles have insufficient citations, including Tirurangadi and some individual biographies; the Tartu article flags its signatory sentence as needing a citation. Those leads require more verification.

`history/sources/wikipedia_1920_ce_people_links.json.gz` and `wikipedia_1921_ce_people_links.json.gz` contain selected event-connected people and qualified relationships, including people on different sides. They are research leads, not complete biographies or evidence of physical co-presence. They are not spawnable; natural VIP spawning, historical behavior and alternate-timeline branching remain unimplemented.

## Remaining limitations and storage

Overlaps and unreviewed hazards remain; the Georgian coup record was not changed because the attempted article titles were not verified. No recursive linked-article collection was completed. The parked crawl and people SQLite databases still prevent their collectors from running. In addition to seven previously archived snapshots, five more inactive review JSON files were losslessly compressed; `reviews/archived_review_reports_1920_2026-11.json` records hashes and `review_snapshot_archive.py` verifies and restores them subject to space. `place_candidates` and `person_timelines` staging exports were also converted losslessly to XZ; checksum-verified readers fall back to them and `restore_vip_staging.py places|timelines` can reconstruct the exact gzip when space allows. Restorations were not actually performed under the cap.

Selected automated checks: 40 tests passed across the two years and related suites, plus 3 archive tests. Archive tests verify hashes, exact reconstruction and guard refusal, not an actual restoration. Latest title audit recognizes 4,910 year titles: 2,989 BCE and 1,921 CE. Eleven earliest BCE titles have evidence elsewhere outside this recognized inventory. Title recognition is not a completed-year count. **Fully collected years established: 0.** The workspace is again close to its 125 MB persistence guard; 1922 and further years were not collected in this pass.
