# 1262–1311 CE — preliminary R2 pass

**Status: partial, not 50 completed histories.** Archived 50 distinct annual titles and their raw pinned revisions; transclusions were not expanded. Scanned 1,015 indexed records starting in the interval, including 115 hazards. Earlier-starting records that overlap the interval were excluded. Most records, year sections and linked articles remain unchecked.

## Changes in indexed game data

- Staged and applied **24 guarded updates** across six indexed packs, preserving IDs and before-images. Corrected the 1293 Mongol invasion of Java: Kertanagara had already died, and Raden Wijaya first allied with the Yuan against Jayakatwang before turning on them. Corrected the location and narrative of several related Majapahit records.
- Qualified the early Ottoman beylik's conventional c.1299 date, Samudera Pasai's traditional foundation, Cebu's disputed Sri Lumay foundation story, and the different 1292/1296 Lan Na milestones. Corrected place references for Venice's 1297 Serrata, Bilbao, Avignon, Sukhothai, Ruad and the Auld Alliance. These notes are not proof of political footprints, exact events or physical presence of all rulers.
- Adjusted the retained Xiangyang battle record's exclusive end to **1274**, including the 1273 surrender, and made its duplicate annual military record a note. The article's lead and infobox differ on the start; its military footprint remains unvalidated. Corrected four NOAA earthquake records that mistook death-order categories for exact numbers; the original counts are unspecified in NOAA.
- **27 hazards were moved to indexed non-damaging notes:** 22 uncertain-dated or uncertain-occurrence volcanic entries, one uncertain 1273 Durrës wave, one 1280 Dutch flood wrongly placed in inland Germany, two misplaced duplicates of the 1287 St. Lucia's flood, and one duplicate 1303 Crete earthquake hazard. Their historical evidence remains in the indexed notes. The separate St. Lucia flood in `events_nature.json.gz` and Crete earthquake world-pack lockdown remain active, but their game radii, duration and effects have not been validated.
- The 22 volcano entries include confirmed geological eruptions whose exact dates are uncertain, such as Aniakchak **1280 ±145**, Okataina's Kaharoa eruption beginning **1310 ±12**, and Osorno **1310 ±75**; Glacier Peak **1300 ±300** and the 1292 Harrat Rahat report are uncertain as eruptions too. GVP lists ongoing Yasur activity with an uncertain historical start, not an established one-year disaster. Quilotoa's eruption was large but its listed 1280 date is questionable. The notes do not deny that confirmed eruptions occurred.
- **88 of the 115 scanned hazards were not moved**, many unreviewed. For example, other catalogued eruptions, earthquakes, famines and floods remain active and need review. NOAA's 1273 Durrës wave is rated validity 2; the separate earthquake remains. The retained St. Lucia flood effect has not been shown to represent the full geographically dispersed storm surge, and reported death figures have weak sourcing.

## People research for future R3

`eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1262_1311_ce_people_links.json` contains **45 people, 21 qualified relationships and 50 event-role links across 19 existing IDs**. It includes multiple opposing or allied figures for the Java campaign, Xiangyang, Yamen, Bạch Đằng, Mongol invasions of Japan, Acre, Ruad, Stirling Bridge and the English baronial war. Roles are associations across stages, not proof of co-presence. These are **research-only**, not complete biographies or naturally spawnable VIPs; historical behavior and alternate-timeline branching are not implemented.
