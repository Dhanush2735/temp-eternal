# 550–510 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked on:** 550, 540, 530, 520 and 510 BCE. This is a partial pass, not a complete collection of those years or every linked article.

## Changes made to the game

Updated the indexed game history JSON: **27 records corrected, 35 moved, and one historical note added**. These include records in nearby years that overlap or duplicate the five focus years.

- Corrected Elea/Velia's founding reference from Greece or Albania to southern Italy, and Xanthos from incorrect locations to Lycia in present-day Türkiye. Each set had three entries; one political effect was retained and the others became notes. Elea's date is approximate, around 538–535 BCE.
- Corrected the Temple of Artemis location and the claim that its rebuilding was completed in 550 BCE: the linked article says rebuilding began around that time. Qualified exact-date claims about Buddhism, Colchis and Greek colonisation.
- Retained one political effect each for King Dao and the expulsion of Hippias, changing their duplicates to notes. Moved King Dao's point to a broad Luoyang capital-region reference, not a claimed assassination site. The Hippias biography calls him Pisistratus's eldest son, unlike the decade overview's 'second son'.
- Changed the duplicate 510 BCE end-of-monarchy entry to an approximate 509 BCE note, preserving the existing 509 political effect. The traditional Roman chronology and story are disputed. Qualified the accessions of Cleomenes and Amyntas, and kept the political effects; their single-year reference slots do not prove exact accession years.
- Added a note on the Peplos Kore, discovered on the Athenian Acropolis and dated approximately to 530 BCE. Its discovery site does not establish where it was made. A name scan found no matching indexed Peplos Kore record, though that cannot rule out all semantic duplicates.
- Moved **30 volcanic records** from the disaster pack to historical notes because the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program marks their dates as approximate or uncertain. This prevents those records from causing exact-year disaster effects; confirmed eruption evidence is not the same as a precisely known date. Moved three broad or uncertain war records and two uncertain battle records to notes as well. The Alalia record remains a note covering a contested approximate period, and separate 535 BCE battle entries may overlap.

Some records use one year as a reference for an approximate period; the year is **not** a claim of exact timing. Moving records between packs can affect saved-world state, which has not been tested.
