# 2700 to 2660 BCE — five partial steps

I worked on **2700, 2690, 2680, 2670 and 2660 BCE**. Their Wikipedia year titles lead to the same century page, so this was one shared research pass, not five separate discoveries.

## Game history updated

I corrected **seven existing entries**, including:

- **Kalibangan, India:** replaced an unsupported claim that the Mature Harappan settlement was abandoned in 2700 BCE with an approximate note about the earlier settlement. Sources disagree about dates and causes.
- **Egypt:** corrected the dating of the Third Dynasty and Djoser's step pyramid, and marked the dates as uncertain. Also corrected an overbroad claim about early Egyptian statues.
- **Mesopotamia:** marked the Early Dynastic period boundary as uncertain, and Gilgamesh as a legendary figure whose history and date are uncertain.
- **China:** marked the Yellow Emperor's traditional date as legend, not a verified political event.

Some entries cover long approximate periods and may appear in game history in multiple years. They do not prove something happened in every year. Two corrections use nearby dates, **2698 and 2650 BCE**, but those are not counted as extra completed steps.

## People for later

I saved research notes on **Djoser, Imhotep, Khasekhemwy and Sneferu**, as well as legendary figures and modern researchers. None was activated as a VIP NPC. Imhotep's role as the pyramid's architect is possible, not certain.

Sources include [the 27th-century page](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=27th_century_BC&oldid=1371895855), [Kalibangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kalibangan&oldid=1365143289), [Egypt's Third Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Third_Dynasty_of_Egypt&oldid=1377187091), and [ARCE on Imhotep](https://arce.org/resource/imhotep-sage-between-fiction-and-reality/).
