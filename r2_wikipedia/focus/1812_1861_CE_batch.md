## Indexed project data

A pre-addendum inventory of existing starts found **5,844 records**, including **1,240 hazards** (549 volcano, 377 earthquake, 217 tsunami, 53 plague, 18 epidemic, 13 famine and 13 flood). `indexed_start_inventory_1812_1861_CE.json.xz` records only pack, ID, start and kind, not full original records. Hazards were **not researched or moved** in this continuation. Many non-hazard events remain unchecked.

Applied **14 guarded updates** in three indexed packs, in addition to the three earlier partial corrections in `1812_1861_CE_partial.md`. Examples: corrected the misdated Massachusetts gerrymandering entry; distinguished Gerry's 1812 bill from the older practice; placed Treaty of Kiel at Kiel and qualified the unrealized Pomerania transfer; moved the Pernambucan revolt reference from Portugal to Recife; distinguished the 1826 poem *Casabianca* from the 1798 battle and removed an erroneous military effect; corrected the Battle of Konya's location; and clarified the French invasion of Algeria began in 1830. The Black War entry no longer implies Aboriginal Tasmanians or their descendants are extinct. Where separate records already exist, updated summaries are notes rather than additional political or military effects. Broad events use explicitly identified geographic reference points, not asserted precise borders.

## Storage and important operational limitation

- To recover space, the crawl and people **staging SQLite databases were parked**, not deleted: exact-byte compressed copies `r2_wikipedia/crawl.sqlite3.xz` and `r2_wikipedia/people.sqlite3.xz`. Both archives, original SHA-256 hashes, SQLite integrity and foreign keys were checked; manifest `focus/staging_databases_parked_1812_2026-11.json`. The collector commands deliberately refuse to create empty replacement databases while their archives exist.
- **The normal recursive crawler, people collector and SQLite-based audits are paused until the databases are restored.** Use `python r2_wikipedia/park_staging_databases.py restore crawl.sqlite3` and the equivalent for `people.sqlite3` only after making enough space. Restore retains the archive and refuses to overwrite; it increases workspace usage substantially. The existing collector reserve still applies and will block growth unless adequate free space is provisioned. Temporary `verify` checks restoration outside the workspace without permanently restoring the DBs.
- A further 116 non-indexed Wikipedia research source JSON files were losslessly compressed to `.json.gz`, saving 501,250 bytes before manifest overhead; raw and compressed hashes and original filenames are in `research_sources_gzip_1812_2026-11.json`. `research_source_archive.py` loads or restores exact original bytes without overwriting. Existing older reports referencing `.json` source paths require this loader or restoration. The 95 research people graphs compressed in the preceding turn use `people_graph_archive.py`.
- New `collect_ce_year_batch.py` checks the 125 MB guard before requests and before saving, and checks page identity and disambiguation. It only archives raw year revisions; it does not recursively crawl links or certify completion. The guard was not raised. Headroom is small and cannot sustain another comparable batch without more space.
