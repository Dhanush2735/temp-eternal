# Wikipedia history pass: 1201–1241 CE

**Five years partly worked:** 1201, 1211, 1221, 1231 and 1241. This is not a full collection of those years or of all linked Wikipedia articles. Intervening years are left for later.

## Installed in the project's indexed game history

- Updated **30 existing event records** and added **6** across five indexed packs. IDs and stored year ranges of existing rows were preserved. Each change includes article URLs and an explanation; the review manifest contains complete before- and after-images.
- **1201:** improved the Riga and Villeneuve Abbey entries and Theobald III's death location; added al-Tusi's birth and John Komnenos's coup. The coup may have been in 1200 rather than 1201; the game still uses a 1201 reference slot.
- **1211:** improved the accounts of Yehuling, Antioch on the Meander, Lavaur, Toulouse, Iltutmish, Reims and Santiago de Compostela; placed Frederick II's election at Nuremberg.
- **1221:** improved records for the Mongol campaign, Indus and Parwan battles, and Jōkyū War; added the Fifth Crusade defeat near Mansurah. The Nishapur entry now notes attacks in 1220 and capture in 1221, but its stored end may exclude 1221 in some readers. Chichen Itza's purported 1221 revolt is qualified: colonial accounts and archaeological chronology differ.
- **1231:** corrected the place of the Constitutions of Melfi and separated the unsupported claim that the Edict of Salerno was promulgated that year; the Frederick II article instead dates the edict to 1241, and its full legal chronology needs review. Corrected the Uiju invasion location, updated Kuju and Filangieri, and added the university bull *Parens scientiarum* and Anthony of Padua's death.
- **1241:** improved Legnica, Mohi and Giglio; corrected Lahore, Valladolid and Snorri Sturluson's locations; added Gregory IX's death. The Lahore siege article carries an AI-text warning; its commander and details still need independent verification. A separate existing world row also describes the siege.

## Important limits and remaining risks

- **Montségur:** `q12950532` still has a 1241–1242 game range even though the siege article gives 1243–1244; `q2750233` appears to describe the same siege in a later range. Both remain active. The early record was labeled as conflicting, but that does **not** stop its gameplay effects. Fixing the range and duplicate safely needs saved-world review.
- **Nishapur:** the Khorasan article distinguishes 1220 activity from Tolui's April 1221 siege. The existing `q5961259` range was not extended, so some consumers may not show the capture in 1221. The siege-specific article has an AI-text warning.
- Existing war and event records can overlap the updated or added entries. Duplicate-name scans do not prove semantic uniqueness. Wide-radius coordinates are area or institutional proxies, not precise sites of every action.
- **39 hazard entries** overlap at least one sampled year using `start <= year <= end`. This inclusive scan is not proof that every entry triggers in those years: readers use different end boundaries. All 39 are unchanged and unreviewed; see `reviews/1201_1241_ce_hazard_overlap_unreviewed.json`. The hazards and nature packs were not edited.
- **38 people leads** are in `eternal/src/main/resources/data/eternalengine/eternal/history/sources/wikipedia_1201_1241_ce.json`. They are research notes only, not finished or naturally spawning VIPs. Their details need further checking before becoming profiles.
- No Java, Forge, in-game or saved-world test, integrity-manifest regeneration, or jar rebuild was done for this batch. The old jar does not contain these JSON changes. Natural VIP spawning and historical VIP behavior remain unimplemented.
