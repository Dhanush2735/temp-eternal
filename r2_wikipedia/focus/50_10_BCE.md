# History collection: 50–10 BCE

## Progress

Partly worked on **50, 40, 30, 20 and 10 BCE**, using English Wikipedia period pages and selected linked articles, with checks against Smithsonian and NOAA catalogs. This is not a complete collection of these years or all their linked articles. Years between the ten-year steps still need work.

**Total: 309 years partly worked; 0 years fully collected.**

## Changes installed in the game files

- Updated **20 existing records**, keeping their IDs. These include active records: Uxellodunum is dated to 51 BCE, the Battle of the Bagradas to 49 BCE, and the Battle of Alexandria to 30 BCE. The Jerusalem and Roman–Parthian agreement markers are no longer placed in Iraq. The 30 BCE Egyptian political record is no longer classified as a treaty.
- Corrected or qualified other entries: Igodo and Igodomigodo's origins belong to much later traditions, not an established 40 BCE event; the date and succession of the Kanva dynasty remain uncertain; and Cleopatra's burial place is unknown. A claim that a Chinese primer proves a hydraulic-powered hammer in 40 BCE was replaced with a more cautious note.
- Added **two non-damaging history notes**: Cleopatra's death in Alexandria in 30 BCE and the Solarium Augusti in Rome in 10 BCE. The latter uses the ancient site, not the modern position of the relocated obelisk.
- Moved **29 volcano records** from the damaging hazards file to historical notes because their exact game-year timing is not established. Smithsonian confirms most of the eruptions, but dates them with uncertainty; the 10 BCE Vulcano eruption is itself marked uncertain. These changes do **not** mean the confirmed eruptions did not happen.
- Moved **four earthquake/tsunami records** to non-damaging notes. The Dyoscuria date is disputed between BCE and CE sources; the Bizone event's exact year and a possible tsunami are uncertain. NOAA's linked earthquake and tsunami entries are not independent confirmation.
- Saved **13 people leads** for future R3 research, including Shi You, Fuzhulei Ruodi, Souxie, Igodo, Cleopatra and Claudius. They are research notes, **not spawnable VIPs**. No VIP behavior or alternate-timeline feature was implemented in this batch.
