All four were moved from the separately loaded hazards pack to the indexed regional history pack as qualified `cultural` / `note` references. IDs, nominal year slots, coordinates and radii were retained, but the 40 km radii are game references, **not measured damage footprints**. The Hekla catalog episode names Vatnafjöll; the inherited Hekla point is not a precise vent. The catalog confirms eruptions, not those exact years; VEI 0 for Hualālai does not establish absence of local lava damage.

On a fresh load of the modified resources, `Disasters.java` no longer gets these four rows from its hazard pack. They remain regional notes at their nominal reference dates. No claim is made about existing saved-world effects or a running game, and other records may also describe related events. Ten other hazards start in the batch and one additional hazard overlaps at an inclusive stored boundary; they remain **unreviewed and unchanged**.
