# 1551, 1561, 1571, 1581 and 1591 CE — partial collection

**Progress: 469 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** These five ten-year steps are not complete histories. Intervening years, unread parts of year and decade pages, and many linked articles remain.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

- Updated **28 existing records** and added **one**, across four indexed packs. The addition describes the 1571 Battle of Bangkusay near Manila. A name search found no matching indexed battle, but this does not prove semantic uniqueness.
- Corrected misplaced records, including the 1551 siege of Tripoli in Libya (not Lebanon), Bairam Khan's death at Patan, the 1581 capture of Narva, Dmitry's death at Uglich, the naval battle off Flores, and the 1591 Portuguese invasion of Jaffna. Town and regional points are labeled as proxies, not exact historical sites.
- The Ajacán mission's site is unknown; the changed 1571 entries now use a broad Virginia Peninsula proxy instead of Rome or an asserted Jamestown site. The separate 1570 establishment entry still has an incorrect Rome location and remains to be reviewed.
- Corrected the Nuremberg 1561 record to describe a **broadsheet report**, not a verified aerial battle. Corrected Saint Basil's construction start and qualified the Gozo slavery estimates. The Treaty of Drohiczyn did not end the Livonian War; its Wikipedia article has limited sourcing.
- Corrected the 1591 Rouen siege context, the location of the New Spain Inquisition, the place of Bayinnaung's death, the Act of Abjuration's signing place, and the location of Charminar. The 1591 Hyderabad founding account remains in tension with an existing 1589 legacy row; that older row was not changed.
- Tondibi entries use nearby Gao as a broad proxy, not the battlefield itself. Several overlapping events were retained as contextual notes instead of being deleted; repeated news and interactions remain possible.
