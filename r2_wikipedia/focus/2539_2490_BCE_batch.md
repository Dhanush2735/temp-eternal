## Indexed gameplay edits

* Corrected `wpy_c21a706004`: its old name referred to Serteya II in western Russia while its coordinate was in central Siberia. The [2021 archaeological study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352409X21004314) gives site coordinates 55°37′53″N, 31°32′28″E and dates the pile-dwelling settlement approximately 2500–2200 calibrated BCE. The note now uses the site coordinates and is a cultural note, not a political event. It no longer asserts an unverified first-European N1c finding; the genetic claim and sample need primary-source review. The one-year slot is an approximate reference, not the duration of the site or proof of occupation specifically in 2500.
* Changed `wpy_403d82cc80` and `wpr_50ec0003ce` from political effects to cultural notes: [Hayk and Bel](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hayk&oldid=1370643977) belong to a legendary tradition, and 2492 BCE is traditional dating, not an independently established event. The battle note now uses the [Lake Van](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lake_Van&oldid=1380993106) centre as a **regional proxy**, since the [linked account](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Battle_of_Hayots_Dzor&oldid=1371169183) locates the legend east of the lake; it is not a verified battlefield. The other pin remains an approximate broad Armenian reference, not a verified founding location. These records may overlap semantically and remain separately identified pending deduplication. The later author Movses Khorenatsi is not a contemporary of the supposed event; his dating is contested.
* Added `r2_userkaf_pyramid_context_c2498`: the Saqqara [pyramid complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Pyramid_of_Userkaf&oldid=1361590222) is attributed to Userkaf. Its selected 2498–2491 BCE interval reflects one [Fifth Dynasty chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Fifth_Dynasty_of_Egypt&oldid=1351994729), not proof of construction in every year, exact completion, reign boundaries or personal presence. The 20 km radius is a gameplay setting. The same source discusses a separate queen's complex attributed to Neferhetepes, Sahure's mother and most likely Userkaf's wife. Another [Neferhetepes](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Neferhetepes&oldid=1365242903), Djedefre's daughter, is probably not the same woman. The long existing Fifth Dynasty regional note remains distinct; further semantic overlaps are possible.
* Corrected `wpr_5598bb46c7`: the 25th-century page's claim about the **earliest** sewn boat at c.2500 is contradicted by its linked [sewn boat article](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sewn_boat&oldid=1374360444), which describes earlier [Abydos boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Abydos_boats&oldid=1380649443). The note now describes the Khufu ship as an early example, while explicitly acknowledging earlier examples. The date remains approximate, and the old broad regional pin and 269 km radius are game settings, not the ship's verified construction location.

All retain stable existing IDs where applicable. Names and source evidence include uncertainty; evidence fields are not necessarily displayed by the game. Narrow indexed scans do not guarantee semantic uniqueness. The additional site/reign context can repeat in NewsIndex, and some readers may include stored end years.
