# 850–810 BCE: partial research and game-data changes

**Focus years:** 850, 840, 830, 820 and 810 BCE. This follows ten-year steps; intervening years are still to be collected.

**Overall progress: 229 focus years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** Reading selected pages is not a complete collection of every linked article or every historical event.

## What was installed

The indexed game JSON now contains **26 corrected records**, **16 moved records**, and **6 new context notes**. IDs were retained for all changed and moved records. Of the moves, 14 were uncertain-date volcanoes, one was an Assyrian war note, and one was a Qarqar battle record. The new notes are broad reign/period context, not proof of an action in the exact displayed year.

- **850:** The Adena date differs across sources: Wikipedia’s culture article gives about 500 BCE onward; other sources use about 1000 BCE. The entry is a regional context, not a proven first appearance in 850. Tagaung’s Abhiyaza origin is a later legendary account; no 850 foundation is proven. Takelot’s accession and the Middle Mumun boundary are qualified. Mesha’s stele was found at Dhiban, Jordan; dating estimates include about 850 and about 840.
- **840:** Gonghe regency leadership and start dates are interpreted differently. The precise 840 Gopala founding claim is not established; it is a later dynastic tradition. Tel Dan’s fragmentary inscription and biblical accounts do not establish the previously asserted choice of killer.
- **830:** Shalmaneser III’s reign is included as context, not an annual action. The Qarqar references may be duplicates: one references 854, the other 853, with differing dating and disputed outcome.
- **820:** Xiong Xun’s succession relies on later historical writing; Pygmalion’s precise 820 accession conflicts with alternative chronologies. Shamshi-Adad V is included as reign context only.
- **810:** Adad-nirari III’s accession is 811 in one eponym-list reconstruction, 810 in another. His earlier pin was in Syria; Kalhu is now an Assyrian regional reference, not proof of a ceremony site. King Xuan’s reign is included as context.

Nearby records were also qualified, including Dido and Carthage traditions, Egyptian rival kings and Zhou succession. Dido’s historicity is uncertain. Some related game entries can still repeat.

## Sources and traceability

The five year page titles redirect to their decade pages. Research used those five decades, the ninth-century page, linked biographies, archaeology and artifact articles, plus Smithsonian volcano records and an Indiana DNR educational source for one alternative Adena dating. Source links and qualifications are embedded in the reviewed JSON records; Wikipedia references use checked revision IDs. This was a bounded manual review, not traversal of every Wikipedia link.

The exact before/after records, source positions, and additions are in `reviews/850_810_bce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `030235f269e7f3bd451c18904dc79e294ba148b79891bd7a170ed1b9e6a73398`. `apply_850_810_context.py` stages, applies or rolls back with changed-record and shared-ID guards; writes are atomic per pack, **not across packs**. Rollback requires current after-images and must be coordinated with later edits.
