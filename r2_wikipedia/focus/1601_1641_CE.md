# 1601, 1611, 1621, 1631 and 1641 CE — partial collection

**Progress: 474 years partly worked; 0 fully collected.** These five ten-year steps are not complete histories. Intervening years, unread parts of period pages, and many linked articles remain.

## Implemented in indexed game JSON

- Updated **34 existing records** and added **three** across three indexed packs.
- Corrected locations for the 1601 sieges of Székesfehérvár and Nagykanizsa in Hungary, the 1611 capture of Novgorod in Russia, the 1621 Khotyn battle near modern Ukraine, the 1631 raid on Baltimore in Ireland, the 1631 killings at Mombasa in Kenya, and the 1641 Dutch trading post on Dejima in Japan. The locations are sites, town points or regional proxies, not always precise coordinates.
- Corrected the Ming Tianqi emperor to Zhu Youjiao; his father Zhu Changluo was the Taichang Emperor. John Winthrop was already governor before 1631. The 1631 Prague capture is attributed to Saxon forces; year-page day-level accounts conflict.
- Moved Charterhouse's 1611 foundation from the school's later Godalming campus to the London Charterhouse. Moved selected 1641 parliamentary events from Wales to a Westminster proxy. Corrected the English East India Company departure to an England proxy and the 1631 Battle of the Slaak fleet-loss wording.
- Added a 1621 Plymouth harvest gathering context note, without asserting it was the first thanksgiving or that an invitation is proven; a 1621 Banda conquest civilian-consequences note; and a 1641 Malacca siege completion note. The latter two overlap existing indexed conquest/siege records, but are notes rather than additional military effects. Name and interval searches cannot guarantee semantic uniqueness.
- Several overlapping records were retained as notes, not deleted. The siege of Nagykanizsa source needs additional citations; the Tamblot article is brief. Some duplicate news and other interactions may still occur. Khotyn's treaty location is a battle-area proxy, not a verified signing place. Hudson's James Bay point is a broad proxy; his fate and number of companions remain uncertain.
