- Moved **11 uncertain-date disaster records** out of the active disaster loader and into qualified historical notes. These include two related Thera eruption/tsunami entries; they are not evidence of two eruptions. Two uncertain war records were also moved to history notes.

Some related older entries may still repeat the same story. Changes may affect saved games.
