## Indexed gameplay corrections

Ten existing records were updated, retaining their IDs:

- **1932:** moved two overlapping Siamese revolution summaries to a Bangkok city proxy; they remain separate and retain political effects. Reframed Earhart's transatlantic landing around a Culmore village proxy in Northern Ireland. She departed Newfoundland, then a British dominion, not the United States; conflicting duration figures were omitted.
- **1934:** moved the Soviet invasion of Xinjiang from Russia to an Ürümqi city proxy. Corrected George V's Silver Jubilee from 1934 to **1935**, with London as a proxy for one centre of celebrations. The event article calls this the first British monarch's silver-jubilee celebration, while the broader silver-jubilee list includes earlier British royal anniversaries; the first-ever assertion was omitted pending further research. No other indexed jubilee record starting in 1935 was found.
- **1935:** corrected the Danish election summary: the Folketing election was in October in Denmark proper, and Thorvald Stauning had already served as prime minister since 1929, after an earlier term. The Faroese election took place in November. Completed a truncated Earhart solo-flight summary; its Hawaii point represents the departure island, not the route.
- **1936:** moved the Xi'an Incident from the Sea of Japan to a Xi'an city proxy and included both Zhang Xueliang and Yang Hucheng as the generals who detained Chiang Kai-shek.
- **1937:** moved the Xinjiang rebellion from Russia to a Kashgar regional proxy; and placed the Earhart–Noonan final flight at a **Lae departure proxy**, explicitly not an inferred crash or disappearance location. They disappeared over the Pacific and their exact fate remains uncertain.

Coordinates and radii are gameplay approximations, not exact incident sites or complete geographic footprints. Existing effects were retained, and game timing remains year-level. The 1933 annual page places an ambiguously described anti-monarchist incident in April, whereas the Boworadet rebellion was in October; those were **not merged or rewritten**. The Inamujandi Revolt title checked was missing, so that record was left alone. Several sources, including the Boworadet, Kashgar, 1937 Xinjiang and Fred Noonan articles, request additional citations; Noonan's article also has inconsistent disappearance days. None of the 649 inventoried hazards were individually reviewed or moved.
