# Preliminary historical work: 2289–2240 BCE

**Status: partly worked, not fully collected.** The 50 English Wikipedia year titles were checked, and all resolve to the 23rd-century BC article, revision 1371895841 (`year_titles_2289_2240.json`). This is title resolution, not 50 separate annual histories; the worldwide linked-article crawl is incomplete.

## Indexed game-data changes

Five existing contextual records were improved, without adding duplicates. The stele reference now names the Lullubi and cites Satuni and Sidur as named in the Wikipedia transcription, without presenting an exact battle year, location or carved-figure identification. Manishtushu's note references governors Eshpum and Ilshu-rabi; Pepi II's note identifies Ankhesenpepi II and her **probable**, not certain, regency. Points and time slots are contextual, not proof that people met there at those dates. The Louvre describes the victory stele as originating at Sippar and being taken to Susa in the 12th century BCE; that removal must not be placed in Naram-Sin's lifetime.

Two legacy war-pack notes (`wpw_4e118c2b56`, `wpw_ab7e1def57`) were relabeled as cultural context, not single continuous wars. Sargon's formerly misleading 2300–2240 slot was changed to the approximate 2334–2279 reign range used by one chronology, and its geographical point/radius changed to a broad Mesopotamian reference. Akkad's location is unknown. This overlaps existing `wpr_1a396908c8`; both are retained pending deduplication. The war pack's NewsIndex treats the stored end as inclusive and can continue reporting news for two years, so the end must not be interpreted as a precise death date. Date and point changes may affect saves.

## Volcano source checks

Of 19 overlapping GVP hazard rows, 18 confirmed but imprecisely dated eruptions were moved from deterministic hazard handling to indexed non-disruptive regional notes, preserving IDs and nominal slots: Pelée 2280 BCE (?); Raususan 2270 ±50; Snæfellsjökull 2270 ±300; Escanaba Segment 2260 (?); Haleakalā 2260 ±40; Arenal 2250 (?); Chyulu Hills 2250 ±110; Galunggung 2250 ±150; Gorely, Hakkodasan, Karymsky, Katla, Mauna Loa and Tenerife each 2250 (?); Cerro Hudson 2250 **in or before**; Machín 2240 ±300; Shisheika and Villarrica each 2240 (?). These are the source's dates and dating expressions, not proof of precise events. `?` is not a quantified uncertainty; “in or before” is not an exact date. Shisheika's descriptive 4190 ±70 BP estimate is retained separately from its listed 2240 BCE (?) catalogue row. Inherited points and game radii do not establish the impact footprint. Moving an eruption to a note does **not** prove that it caused no damage.

Cotopaxi 2250 BCE `hz_00a8df6fb5` remains unchanged and unreviewed for gameplay: the [GVP entry](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=352050) gives tephrochronology but no `?` or numerical range for this row. No uncertainty range has been invented. ## People and relationships

`history/sources/wikipedia_2289_2240_people_links.json` contains 23 sourced person/name references and 22 qualified connections, including Akkadian officials and Lullubi opponents, Egyptian royal relatives and Harkhuf. It cross-references earlier research and uses local identifiers for inscription-only people. Shutruk-Nakhunte is linked to the monument a millennium later, not treated as a contemporary of Naram-Sin. **This is research data, not spawnable VIP data.** No exact ancient births, deaths or meetings were invented. Enheduanna's literary exile/restoration reconstruction is not installed as an exact historical event; authorship is disputed.

## Verification and rollback

Twenty-one unit tests passed both before and after application. Both context packs and both hazard packs were staged and reversed against their actual inputs; 23 unique installed after-images and the unchanged Cotopaxi row were verified. Writes are atomic per pack, **not across packs**. The context manifest was applied first and the hazard manifest second; if rolling back this batch without intervening changes, use:

```
PYTHONPATH=r2_wikipedia python r2_wikipedia/apply_2289_2240_bce_hazards.py rollback
PYTHONPATH=r2_wikipedia python r2_wikipedia/apply_2289_2240_bce_context.py rollback
```

Both scripts validate exact before/after images and can fail safely if packs have changed. Later appended regional rows should be reversed first to preserve order. No live loader, Minecraft, save, integrity-manifest or jar test was performed; the existing jar is stale. Natural VIP spawning and alternate-timeline behavior remain unimplemented. The full crawler remains blocked by its separate storage guard.
