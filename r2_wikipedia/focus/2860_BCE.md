# 2860 BCE — partial ten-year step

## Added to the game data

I added a **Saqqara, Egypt, tomb reference** to the indexed history JSON. Wikipedia’s century page gives 2860 BCE for the start of a tomb, but its detailed articles do not confirm that exact date. The game entry therefore says the date and tomb’s owner are **uncertain**. It is a reference, not proof that construction began that year.

The map point is the general Saqqara area, not the exact tomb. The entry is a history note; it does not spawn a person.
