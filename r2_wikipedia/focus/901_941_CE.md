# 901–941 CE: partial Wikipedia research and installation

**Years sampled:** 901, 911, 921, 931 and 941 CE. These years are partly worked, not fully collected. The intervening years and many linked articles remain for later.

## Installed in the game project

Updated **36 existing records** and added **six records** across six indexed history packs. The guarded change manifest, with exact before- and after-images and source links, is `r2_wikipedia/reviews/901_941_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `17b8c33edacd2bb9f03ef94e4ae49142f3e29cc502a8fef22865b2881713e373`). The installer is `r2_wikipedia/apply_901_941_ce_context.py`; writes are atomic per pack, not across packs. Existing event IDs and stored time ranges were preserved.

- **901:** Corrected map references for the Fuzhou walls, Uxmal ballcourt, Nunnaminster, the Tang restoration, the Kutama mission and the Messina naval action. Clarified that Shrewsbury's mention is not its founding; the Nunnaminster was founded between 899 and 902; and Tula developed over centuries rather than necessarily being founded in 901. The Fuzhou wall date and the Sicily campaign still need more checking. Proxies for a city or broad region are not precise sites.
- **911:** Corrected the statement that the Fatimids began taking Sicily from the Aghlabids in 911; the Aghlabid conquest had begun in 827, and the Aghlabid state fell in 909. Clarified Rollo's land grant and Æthelred's succession. Replaced an unrelated Netherlands point for a multi-region Hungarian campaign with a broad Swabian reference. Added Conrad I's election at Forchheim.
- **921:** Added the Abbasid embassy's departure from Baghdad, including Ahmad ibn Fadlan, and a rival court at Koh Ker attributed to a dated inscription. Koh Ker became the capital of the whole Khmer empire later, according to its article. For Pegae, the linked place and battle articles disagree about the site; the new point is one proposed regional reference, not a confirmed battlefield. Clarified the Treaty of Bonn and Ludmila's murder.
- **931:** Removed the incorrect claim that Hugh of Italy received the imperial crown, marked his agreement with Rudolph as chronologically uncertain, and moved Mardavij's campaign reference from Baghdad to Isfahan. Clarified that the Nómina Leonesa is a medieval regnal list with a disputed date, not a modern publication. Replaced the claim that Castile only acquired its name after a 931 conquest of Burgos: the county existed earlier, and the claimed capture needs investigation. Added the death of former emperor Uda at Ninna-ji.
- **941:** Corrected Olaf Guthfrithson's misplaced location and qualified his death: a 941 raid on Tyninghame is recorded, but the biography does not establish that he was killed during it. Replaced Hugh's northern Italy location with a Rome campaign proxy. Distinguished the first Kaminarimon, near Komagata, from its later site and reconstruction. Clarified the Baghdad Green Dome collapse and the 941 Rus' attack. Added the Wuyue and Khmer successions, using city/capital references rather than asserting exact succession or death sites.

Examples of linked sources: [Uxmal ruler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chan_Chak_K%CA%BCak%CA%BCnal_Ajaw), [Kung Ye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kung_Ye), [Nunnaminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mary%27s_Abbey,_Winchester), [Muslim conquest of Sicily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_conquest_of_Sicily), [Conrad I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conrad_I_of_Germany), [Ahmad ibn Fadlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_ibn_Fadlan), [Jayavarman IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayavarman_IV), [Hugh of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_of_Italy), [Nómina Leonesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomina_regum_catolicorum_Legionensium), [Olaf Guthfrithson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olaf_Guthfrithson), [Kaminarimon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaminarimon), and [Qian Yuanguan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qian_Yuanguan). Wikipedia year and decade pages often repeat material and are not independent corroboration.

## Unresolved and active

- The [Battle of Sevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Sevan) article's heading gives 924, while its infobox and the year page give 921. Two active game records, `q2070062` for 921 and `wpw_98124d5daa` for 924, appear to describe the same battle. Both have been labeled as potentially duplicated and conflicting, but **both remain active** because separate readers and saved-world effects require further investigation.
- The inclusive scan found **33 hazard rows** whose stored ranges touch at least one sampled year. All remain active and unreviewed; overlap does not establish when each consumer triggers them. `Disasters.java` loads hazards independently of the edited history packs.
- The 941 Rus'–Byzantine war record represents the first attack, not a continuous 941–944 war; a later offensive occurred in 944. Its existing range is unchanged. The 901 Sicily campaign and several other entries may overlap across packs; name/time scans do not prove semantic uniqueness.
- Earlier unresolved: `wpy_65c3fd3d26` implies al-Ma'mun became caliph in 821, although his accession was 813. It was not changed here.

## People and checks

Thirteen installer tests passed before and after installation. All six staged pack transformations passed logical reversal, and all 42 installed after-images appeared exactly once in indexed packs. These are JSON-level checks, not proof of historical completeness, Java/Forge behavior, Minecraft gameplay or saved-world compatibility. The resource-integrity manifest was not regenerated and no jar was rebuilt; the old jar remains stale. Previous live-test failures remain unresolved.
