# 250–210 BCE: partial collection and game-data update

**Focus years:** 250, 240, 230, 220 and 210 BCE. These are five ten-year steps. The years between them remain for later; none of these years is fully collected.

## What changed in the project

The indexed history packs now contain **25 corrected records, 26 moved records and two new cultural records**. The 51 existing records keep their IDs.

- Corrected military dates for the two Capua and two Herdonia battles, the Bagradas River battle and the siege of Lilybaeum. The Upper Baetis battle is now 211 BCE at an upper Guadalquivir regional marker instead of 210 near Cádiz. Its linked article relies on one source, so details and the location remain uncertain. A potentially duplicate Capua siege is now a non-damaging cross-reference.
- The date of the siege of Medion differs across linked articles, so it is now a qualified note rather than a fixed-year military event. The Byzantium siege and Mantinea battle also became notes because their Wikidata dating is uncertain and no English article was found. Mercenary War is a broad, non-damaging 241–238/237 BCE context near Carthage.
- The Septuagint translation is now a cultural note, not a political treaty. Simuka's founding date is debated, and the Bhattiprolu Kubera claim needs better evidence; neither now triggers a fixed-year political event. The Terracotta Army is cultural, not political. Removed false disaster effects from the Roman food-relief and Hannibal campaign entries, and qualified the Molon, Hermeias, Great Wall, Ludovisi Gaul and Spanish-language records. Two Halley comet entries remain non-damaging cross-references; sources disagree on the day.
- Added a note about **Edfu temple construction beginning in 237 BCE**, and one about the **Lion Capital at Sarnath, approximately 250 BCE**. These are reference slots, not claims that construction or carving occupied just one year. The indexed Pleiades Edfu site has a later occupation range and a questionable `kush` hint; the event note does not fix or certify that separate site record.
- Moved **20 volcano records** from the damaging disaster pack to non-damaging notes. GVP dates carry uncertainties or, for Laguna del Maule, no reported error margin on an Ar/Ar date; none establishes the exact single damaging year used by the game. GVP describes Stromboli as 210 BCE ±10, but its associated narrative says early third century, which needs further checking. The 250 BCE Chiguchin earthquake remains active as a reported NOAA event, with date, magnitude and location uncertain.
- Moved the **210 BCE Gulf of Cádiz earthquake and two possible tsunami references** to non-damaging notes. NOAA links the earthquake to tsunami event 4281, whose comments say very improbable and no original sources are available. The two tsunami rows may describe the same report; an independent earthquake confirmation was not established.
- Added **12 research-only people leads**, including Agron, Teuta, Eratosthenes, Livius Andronicus, Simuka, Qin Shi Huang, Qin Er Shi and Hannibal. They are not spawnable VIPs: `history/sources/wikipedia_250_210_bce.json`.
