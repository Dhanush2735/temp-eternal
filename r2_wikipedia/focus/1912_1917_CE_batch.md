## Changes to indexed gameplay data

Six guarded updates in two indexed event packs (manifest `reviews/1912_1917_ce_context_2026-11.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `242d5220c480b2908010321524427b64b7c08b2854d4e487fbda8c847d7b30c9`). Original IDs and full before-images are retained for rollback:

- Corrected two *Lusitania* summaries to 1915 and the sinking location off Ireland; one had been filed under 1914, the other mapped to Berlin. They remain overlapping notes pending deduplication. [Sinking of RMS Lusitania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinking_of_RMS_Lusitania).
- Replaced a misleading First Balkan War claim that the Ottomans were pushed out of Europe entirely. Existing point is only a broad marker. [First Balkan War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Balkan_War).
- Moved the 1913 assassination of Greek king George I to a Thessaloniki **city proxy**, preserving the political effect. [George I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_I_of_Greece), [Thessaloniki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thessaloniki).
- Completed the truncated Panama Canal opening description, naming SS *Ancon*. [Panama Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_Canal), [SS Ancon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Ancon).
- Moved the 1917 Russian Revolution summary from Mongolia to a **Petrograd city proxy**, separating the February and later Bolshevik phases in the wording and retaining the political effect. The proxy does not represent the whole revolution's geography. [Russian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Revolution), [February Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_Revolution).

The indexed pack still contains other unreviewed and potentially duplicate records. The many events and linked people are not comprehensively covered.
