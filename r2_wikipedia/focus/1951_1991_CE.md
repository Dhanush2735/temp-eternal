# 1951–1991 CE: partial Wikipedia collection

**Years sampled:** 1951, 1961, 1971, 1981 and 1991. These years are started, not fully collected. Many sections and links remain unread, as do the intervening years.

## Added to the project

- Updated **17 existing events** and added **one** in three indexed game history packs. IDs and stored year ranges of the existing events were retained.
- Added a 1951 entry for the origin of the HeLa cell line from Henrietta Lacks's cells, including that they were used without her knowledge or consent. The hospital coordinate is a proxy; the name scan did not establish semantic uniqueness.
- Corrected selected locations, including the Seventeen Point Agreement in Beijing, the 1961 Goa operation, the 1971 Bangladesh surrender in Dhaka, the first Space Shuttle launch in Florida, the GCC's founding summit in Abu Dhabi, and the 1991 Belovezha Accords in Belarus.
- Corrected representative locations for the 1991 Benin and Cape Verde elections and the South Ossetia and Georgian civil wars. A capital or town is a **proxy**, not a claim that a nationwide event or war happened only there.
- The Algerian War record retains its old 1961–1963 stored range but now identifies the 1961–1962 phase; the war began in 1954. Stored end-year behavior differs between readers.

The exact prior and new records, source links, proxy qualifications and reasons are preserved in `../reviews/1951_1991_ce_context_2026-10.json.gz`. Canonical SHA-256: `eb40a01621f7b98fcd5d415c74fd587af8fd10a95c8471b479dae6b2ee43a53d`.
