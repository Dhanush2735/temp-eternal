# 1050–1010 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked:** 1050, 1040, 1030, 1020 and 1010 BCE. The years between these steps still need work. None of the five is fully collected.

## What changed in the game

I installed changes in the indexed history JSON: **16 corrected records, 39 records moved to history notes, and five new notes**. Existing IDs were kept. The corrections include nine nearby-year records outside the five steps.

- Qualified conflicting dates for the **Shang–Zhou transition**, Protogeometric Greece and Troy. The linked Troy article does not establish a Greek attack or a specific destruction at 1020. Mycenaean Greece is now referenced in mainland Greece rather than Crete.
- Marked **Saul, the Ark capture, Gilboa and the Jebus siege** as biblical traditions with uncertain history and dating. The Jebus and Gilboa records were moved out of military-effect categories to history notes; this does not establish that these accounts are false.
- Qualified Assyrian, Zhou and Egyptian succession claims, including nearby 1053 and 1044 claims that conflict with linked biographies. The 1057 Solomon's Temple claim is identified as an unreliable Josephus-based chronology rather than a construction fact. Rebellion of the Three Guards remains an approximate, geographically broad context.
- Added reign-context notes for **Psusennes I, King Cheng of Zhou, Shalmaneser II, Simbar-shipak and Ashur-rabi II**. Dates are uncertain and coordinates are regional references, not proof that a person was present or acted in the chosen year.
- Moved **36 volcano records** from active disasters to non-disruptive history notes. Smithsonian's Global Volcanism Program lists the respective eruptive periods but dates them approximately or marks them uncertain. In particular, Atitlán is 1020 BCE ±150 and St. Helens is 1010 BCE (?). Overlapping estimates do not prove distinct impacts. Volcano coordinates may represent the system, not an exact vent.
- Moved the **Timna-area earthquake catalogue record** to an uncertain note. NOAA event 13 lists 1050 BCE, magnitude 6.2 of unknown magnitude type and a damage order, citing Kovach (1988). That listing alone does not verify the exact year, location or the game's 50-km effect.

The exact before/after records and sources are in `reviews/1050_1010_bce_context_2026-10.json.gz` (canonical SHA-256 `1041ee796ea5752518777af18468cf85afba9a431182fb2ff5db7c258453c28a`). The guarded installer provides logical rollback and checks for duplicate IDs; writes are atomic per pack, not across all packs.
