# Preliminary historical work: 2139–2090 BCE

**Partly worked, not fully collected.** The 50 English Wikipedia year titles resolve to century articles: 39 to the 22nd century BC (revision 1381193324) and 11 to the 21st century BC (revision 1371895845). They are not independent annual histories. Much worldwide linked-article research remains undone.

## Changes to indexed game data

- Improved the existing Utu-hengal/Tirigan reference with their connected envoys Ur-Ninazu and Nabi-Enlil. The [Louvre AO 6018](https://collections.louvre.fr/en/ark:/53355/cl010166715) is a later clay tablet **copy** of a monumental royal victory inscription, not the original stele. The royal account is partisan; the precise modern calendar date and battle location are not established.
- Improved the Ur-Nammu reference with Shulgi's relationship and building-work evidence, including the [Met's inscribed brick](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/324915) and [ziggurat discussion](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/ur-the-ziggurat). Different chronologies exist. The displayed range refers to Ur-Nammu and is not Shulgi's lifespan or a proof of exact dates.
- Moved **16 GVP eruption records with imprecise dates** from deterministic hazard handling to indexed non-disruptive regional notes, keeping their IDs, nominal slots and inherited locations. This does not mean the eruptions had no effects. Saved-world consequences have not been tested. Nominal slots and radii do not establish exact dates, vents, or impact footprints.

## People for later

The research-only graph records **17 people/name references and 17 qualified links**, including the two envoys; Utu-hengal and Tirigan; Ur-Nammu, Shulgi and Mari ruler Apil-kin's family; Si'um and Lugalannatum; and the Lagash inscription dedicator Nininimgina and her father Kaku.
