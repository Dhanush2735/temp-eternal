# 1000–960 BCE: partial history collection

**Years worked:** 1000, 990, 980, 970 and 960 BCE. The years between them remain for later. None of these five years is fully collected.

## What changed in the game

I updated the indexed game history JSON: **27 corrected records, 10 uncertain volcano records moved out of active disasters, and 6 new history notes**. Fifteen corrections overlap the focus years; twelve concern nearby years. Existing IDs were kept.

- Corrected misleading locations: the **Latins** record pointed to the Danube delta rather than their Italian context; the **Andean Early Horizon** record pointed to Bolivia rather than central Peru; the **Ugric-language** record pointed to modern Hungary rather than the broadly proposed southern Urals region. These are regional references, not proof of exact movements or locations.
- Qualified dates and claims for **Nok, Chavín, Paracas, Sa Huỳnh, Villanovan culture, Dʿmt, Priene, Iranian migrations, rice cultivation in Vietnam, Assyria and early Iron Age India**. Linked articles often give different dates from the decade or century page. For example, the Dʿmt article describes an 8th–6th-century BCE hypothesis rather than a verified foundation in 980 BCE; the Paracas article gives a c.800 BCE start rather than c.1000.
- Marked the united Israel “golden age,” David and Solomon claims as debated rather than certain political events. First Temple construction dates and the identity of its builder remain debated. Two existing 957 BCE temple notes may repeat.
- Qualified nearby Egyptian, Assyrian and Zhou succession claims where chronologies differ. Added a cautious **Hallur Iron Age** archaeology note, a Proto-Villanovan period reference and four reign-context notes for Osorkon the Elder, Siamun, King Zhao of Zhou and Tiglath-Pileser II. Reign notes do not establish a person's presence or action in the selected year.
- Moved all ten overlapping volcano records to non-disruptive history notes. The Smithsonian lists **nine confirmed eruptive periods with uncertain dates**. Its 1000 BCE **Changbaishan** listing is itself an *uncertain eruption*, not a confirmed one. None of these estimates establishes the game's exact-year, 40-km disruption. Overlapping dates may refer to related activity.

The original reference-year slots are retained even where source dates differ. A note about a conflicting claim is **not proof that the event occurred in that year**. This is still a limited workaround for uncertain dates in the game's current schema.

The detailed sources and exact before/after records are in `reviews/1000_960_bce_context_2026-10.json.gz`, canonical SHA-256 `216d8bc0c9da786884d6c954df1459fb9814b89053d90c4a487e80e893a75447`. The guarded installer checks IDs and permits logical rollback. Writes are atomic per pack, not across the whole set.
