# 1969–1978: preliminary 10-year survey

This is a **preliminary survey only**, not full year collection. It saves Wikipedia year page IDs, revision IDs, revision timestamps, wikitext SHA-256 values and counts. Raw year wikitext was fetched temporarily but **not archived**; future recovery depends on Wikipedia retaining the pinned revisions. No linked articles were followed, no people profiles created, and no gameplay edits made. Fully collected years overall: **0**.

Coordinate flags are heuristic, not confirmed errors.

The survey pins and counts are in `compact_survey_1969_1978_CE.json.xz`; `../compact_year_survey.py` can survey contiguous ranges of up to 50 years if storage allows. Ten page/revision identities and wikitext hashes were independently re-fetched and verified; an independent scan of all 26 indexed packs reproduced the per-year counts. This is not a live-game test.

A full raw archive of 1969–1978 was previously estimated at 298,414 bytes before inventory and corrections; the workspace guard has only a few kilobytes available. The storage guard was not raised and no sole copies were deleted.
